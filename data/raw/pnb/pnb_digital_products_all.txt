Punjab National Bank — Digital Banking Products

PNB ONE:
PNB's all-in-one mobile banking app (Android/iOS) — fund transfers
(NEFT/IMPS/UPI), account statements and mini-statements, term deposit
booking, debit/credit card management, bill payments, mobile recharge,
and QR-code UPI payments. Supports SIM binding and device binding as
additional security layers on top of standard transaction encryption.

BHIM PNB (UPI App):
A dedicated UPI app alongside PNB ONE, letting customers transfer funds
using a payment identifier — account number + IFSC, a virtual payment
address (VPA), or a QR code.

UPI Transaction Limits:
- First-time users: Rs. 5,000 limit for the first 24 hours after
  registration (a standard anti-fraud cooling-off measure).
- Thereafter: Rs. 25,000 per transaction, Rs. 50,000 per day.

mPassbook:
A digital passbook app for viewing transaction history without visiting
a branch or ATM.

Internet Banking:
Standard net-banking portal covering fund transfers, term/recurring
deposit management, and bill payments, alongside the mobile apps above.

Confirm current transaction limits and app feature lists with the bank,
since UPI limits in particular are periodically revised by NPCI/RBI
guidance as well as bank policy.
