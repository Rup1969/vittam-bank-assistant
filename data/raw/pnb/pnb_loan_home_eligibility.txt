Punjab National Bank Home Loan — Products, Eligibility, and Structure

Products:
- Housing Loan for Public — the standard home loan.
- Digi Housing Loan — a digital, faster-processing variant.
- Housing Loan for PNB Pride Customers — a preferential scheme for
  specific existing-customer segments.

Eligibility & Structure:
Floating-rate loan, so the rate moves with RLLR/repo-rate changes over
the loan tenure. Exact eligibility (income multiple, CIBIL threshold,
maximum tenure) should be confirmed with the bank, as third-party
aggregators report inconsistent tenure figures.

This is a headline reference — RLLR-linked rates change with every RBI
repo-rate revision, so always confirm the current effective rate and
RLLR value with the bank before quoting.
