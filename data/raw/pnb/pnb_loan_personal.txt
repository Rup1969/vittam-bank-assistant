Punjab National Bank personal loan interest rate is roughly 10.25% to
16.80% p.a., starting from around 11.40% p.a. for government and
defence employees, and typically 12-13%+ p.a. for general salaried
applicants. Loan amount up to 24 times gross monthly salary.
