Punjab National Bank Personal Loan — Eligibility, Tenure, and Fees

Eligibility:
- Minimum CIBIL score of around 650 typically expected.
- Age: minimum 21 years, maximum 60 years at loan maturity.
- Salaried applicants are generally expected to either hold their salary
  account with PNB or have their employer agree to deduct the EMI
  directly from salary.

Tenure & Fees:
Tenure up to 72 months. Processing fee up to 1% of the loan amount,
waived (nil) for defence personnel in most reported schemes.

This is a headline reference summarizing third-party-reported terms —
personal loan pricing is highly profile-dependent; always confirm the
applicant's actual quoted rate and eligibility with the bank.
