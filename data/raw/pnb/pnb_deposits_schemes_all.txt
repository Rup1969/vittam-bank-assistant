Punjab National Bank — Savings Account Variants

PNB Salary Savings Account:
For salaried customers — employees of central/state government, PSUs, and
private organisations — typically with zero/relaxed minimum balance, a
free chequebook, and salary-credit facilities.

PNB Rise Savings Account:
Targeted at younger investors, roughly age 18-28, positioned as a starter
account for first-time earners/investors.

PNB Grow Savings Account:
A minors' savings account — a minor can operate/manage the account
(subject to bank rules) from around age 10, intended to build early
banking habits.

PNB Power Savings Account:
Designed for women customers, aimed at simplifying day-to-day banking
and financial management.

PNB Pension Savings Account:
For pensioners — bundles standard savings features with pension-credit
handling.

PNB Shikshak Sweep Scheme:
A savings-linked scheme for permanent teaching staff at recognized
schools, colleges, and educational institutions, combining a savings
account with a sweep-in term deposit facility.

Defence/Police Salary Account:
Supports salary and pension payments for serving and retired defence and
police personnel, typically with zero-balance and preferential service
terms.

Central Government Staff Salary Account:
A dedicated salary account for central government employees, generally
with no minimum balance requirement.

Gig Worker / Freelancer Savings Account:
A savings account variant aimed at gig-economy and freelance customers,
typically with no minimum balance and digital-first access.

This is a summary of publicly reported PNB savings account variants —
exact eligibility, minimum balance, and current features should be
confirmed with the bank or its official website before advising a
customer, since account names/features are periodically revised.
