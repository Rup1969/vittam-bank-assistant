Punjab National Bank MSME Loans

Interest Rate:
For loans with at least 25% collateral coverage, reported pricing is
RLLR + Business Strategy Premium (BSP) + 0.85%. For loans with no
collateral coverage, reported pricing is RLLR + BSP + 1.30%. Some
specific MSME schemes are separately reported at a flat rate around
10.25% p.a. Under CGTMSE-covered lending generally, market-reported
effective rates across public sector banks (PNB included) run roughly
10.5%-14% p.a.

Collateral-Free Lending (CGTMSE):
Per RBI guidance that PNB follows, loans up to Rs. 10 lakh to Micro and
Small Enterprises eligible for CGTMSE coverage are considered without
collateral security or a third-party guarantee. Loans above Rs. 10 lakh
and up to Rs. 100 lakh can also be extended without collateral or
third-party guarantee under CGTMSE coverage.

Named Schemes:
- PNB Seva — financing for MSME service-sector units.
- MSME Prime Plus Scheme — a specific eligibility-based MSME scheme.
- PNB Professional Scheme — targeted at self-employed professionals.

This is a headline reference — MSME pricing is benchmark-linked (RLLR)
and scheme-specific; confirm the current RLLR value, BSP, and applicable
scheme terms with the bank before quoting.
