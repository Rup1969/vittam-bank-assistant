Punjab National Bank Car / Vehicle Loan

Interest Rate:
Reported starting rate around 7.30%-7.60% p.a. as of mid-2026, with a
broader range up to roughly 10.70% p.a. depending on applicant profile;
both floating and fixed-rate options are offered.

Loan Amount:
Up to 85% of the on-road vehicle value financed.

Tenure:
Up to 84 months.

Fees & Concessions:
Processing fee of 0.25% of the loan amount (subject to a stated minimum
and maximum), charged at sanction. Women borrowers receive a rate
concession of up to 0.05% across all car loan variants.

This is a headline reference — the actual quoted rate depends on CIBIL
score, income profile, and the vehicle being financed; confirm the
current rate and processing-fee slab with the bank before quoting.
