Punjab National Bank Gold Loan

Interest Rate:
Reported range roughly 8.35%-10.25% p.a. for retail gold loans. A
separate PNB agricultural gold loan scheme (for farming-related purposes,
secured against gold ornaments) is reported at approximately 8.75% p.a.

Loan Amount:
Reported range roughly Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 25 lakh, subject to the gold's
appraised value and the bank's applicable loan-to-value cap.

Structure:
Loan is secured against pledged gold ornaments/jewellery; rate and
eligible amount depend on gold purity, weight, and prevailing gold
valuation at the time of the loan, in line with standard gold-loan
practice across public sector banks.

This is a headline reference — always confirm the current rate,
loan-to-value ratio, and valuation method with the bank, since gold
loan terms are sensitive to gold price movements and are reviewed
frequently.
