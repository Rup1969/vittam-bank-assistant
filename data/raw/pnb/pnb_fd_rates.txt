Punjab National Bank Fixed (Term) Deposit Interest Rates (below Rs. 3 crore)

<!--AUTO:START-->
Punjab National Bank's current fixed deposit (FD) interest rate for the general
public ranges from 3% to 6.8% per annum across its tenures,
with senior citizens earning 0.5% more on most tenures (see the table for each band's exact rate).

Effective 5 November 2026, rates by tenure (general public / senior citizen):
- 1 Year: 6.25% / 6.75%
- 681 Days to 2 years: 6.3% / 6.8%
- >2 to 3 Years: 6.3% / 6.8%
- 1205 Days to 5 Years: 6.35% / 6.85%
- 7 to 14 Days: 3% / 3.5%
- 15 to 45 Days: 3% / 3.5%
- 46 to 90 Days: 5.5% / 6%
- 91 to 179 Days: 5.75% / 6.25%
- 180 to 270 Days: 6% / 6.5%
- 271 Days to 302 Days: 6% / 6.5%
- 303 Days: 5.95% / 6.45%
- 304 Days to <1 Year: 6% / 6.5%
- >1 Year to 443 Days: 6.3% / 6.8%
- 444 Days: 6.7% / 7.2%
- 445 Days to 679 Days: 6.3% / 6.8%
- 680 Days: 6.8% / 7.3%
- >3 Years to 1203 Days: 6.35% / 6.85%
- 1204 Days: 6.3% / 6.8%
- >5 Years to 10 Years: 6% / 6.75%
<!--AUTO:END-->

Senior Citizen Premium: 0.50% for senior citizens (60-80), 0.80% for
super senior citizens (80+), over the general card rate. The 444-day
scheme (see table) pays up to 7.40% for eligible super senior citizens.

PNB Uttam Non-Callable Deposit: a non-callable premium variant for
deposits above Rs. 1 crore — 4.95%-6.45% general / 5.45%-6.95% senior,
depending on tenure.

PNB Pranam (senior citizens aged 55-60): card rate plus 0.50% once the
depositor turns 60, with no premature-withdrawal penalty after 60.

Rates are revised periodically — verify the current card rate with the
bank before quoting for a large deposit.
