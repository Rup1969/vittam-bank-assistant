Punjab National Bank Fixed (Term) Deposit Interest Rates (below Rs. 3 crore)

Punjab National Bank's current fixed deposit (FD) interest rate for the
general public ranges from 3.00% to 6.60% per annum across tenures from
7 days to 10 years, with senior citizens earning 0.50% to 0.80% more.
The 1-year FD interest rate is 6.25% for the general public and 6.75%
for senior citizens.

Effective 1 June 2026, domestic retail term deposit rates by tenure
(general public / senior citizens):
- 7-14 days: 3.00% / 3.50%
- 15-45 days: 3.00% / 3.50%
- 46-90 days: 4.50% / 5.00%
- 91-154 days: 4.90% / 5.40%
- 155 days: 5.55% / 6.05%
- 156-179 days: 4.90% / 5.40%
- 180-270 days: 5.60% / 6.10%
- 271-302 days: 5.60% / 6.10%

Senior Citizen Premium: an additional 0.50% for senior citizens aged 60-80
years, and an additional 0.80% for super senior citizens aged above 80
years, over the general public card rate, across tenures.

444 Days Special Scheme: the bank's highest-yielding retail tenure —
6.60% general public, 7.10% senior citizens, and up to 7.40% for eligible
super senior citizens.

PNB Uttam Non-Callable Deposit Scheme: a non-callable variant (the bank
cannot prematurely close it once placed) offered as a rate premium over
the callable table above in exchange for giving up premature-withdrawal
flexibility. Reported rates range 4.95%-6.45% general public / 5.45%-
6.95% senior citizens depending on tenure. Minimum single deposit is
above Rs. 1 crore, in multiples of Re. 1 thereafter; can typically be
used as loan/overdraft collateral.

PNB Pranam Fixed Deposit Scheme (Senior Citizens aged 55-60): a
preferential scheme for depositors not yet old enough for the standard
60+ senior-citizen premium. Minimum deposit Rs. 100, in multiples of
Re. 1, up to Rs. 1,99,99,999. Rate is the applicable card rate plus an
additional 0.50% once the depositor turns 60. Premature withdrawal is
allowed at the lower of the contractual or applicable rate, with no
penalty once past age 60.

This is the general/callable retail deposit table plus the two
principal special FD schemes; rates are revised periodically — verify
the current card rate with the bank before quoting for a large deposit.
