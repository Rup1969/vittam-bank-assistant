Punjab National Bank home loan interest rate starts from around 7.45%
p.a., linked to the Repo Linked Lending Rate (RLLR). The broader
reported range is 7.50% to 9.75% p.a. for salaried applicants and
7.75% to 10.05% p.a. for non-salaried applicants.
