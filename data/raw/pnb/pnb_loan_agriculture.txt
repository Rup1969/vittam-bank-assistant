Punjab National Bank — Kisan Credit Card / PNB Krishi Card (Agriculture Loan)

Purpose:
A revolving credit facility for farmers to meet short-term crop
production and allied agricultural needs, under the Kisan Credit Card
(KCC) scheme that PNB implements as PNB Krishi Card.

Interest Rate:
Regular crop loans up to Rs. 3 lakh (including KCC) are reported at
around 7% p.a. — this reflects the government's Interest Subvention
Scheme for short-term crop loans, under which the effective rate to a
prompt-repaying farmer can be lower still via an additional Prompt
Repayment Incentive. For loans up to Rs. 20 lakh (covering KCC/crop
loans above Rs. 3 lakh), reported pricing is Base Rate + 2%, with PNB's
reported Base Rate at approximately 9.6%.

Government Interest Subvention:
Farmers availing KCC-based short-term crop loans are eligible for
interest subvention under the government scheme, which reduces the
effective borrowing cost below the bank's card rate — the exact
subvention percentage and prompt-repayment incentive should be
confirmed as they are set by government policy, not the bank alone.

Eligibility (per PNB's own official Krishi Card scheme document):
Progressive literate and illiterate farmers who are owner cultivators,
tenants, lessee or allottee farmers with recorded occupancy rights, or
farmers with ancestral/perpetual cultivation rights, plus Joint
Liability Groups (JLGs). Oral tenants are eligible only if the
landowner agrees to become a co-borrower — except where the account is
secured by liquid securities (FDR/NSC/KVP), in which case no
co-borrower is required. Landless labourers, share croppers, tenant
farmers, and oral lessees can be issued a PNB Krishi Card up to
Rs. 50,000 on the basis of an affidavit alone.

Extent of Loan: Maximum Rs. 50 lakh.

Credit Limit (how PNB calculates the sanctioned amount):
First-year short-term limit = Scale of Finance for the crop (as
decided by the District Level Technical Committee) x extent of area
cultivated, plus 10% of that limit toward post-harvest/household/
consumption needs (health insurance premium coverable up to Rs. 3
lakh), plus 20% toward repairs and maintenance of farm assets, plus
crop/PAIS/asset insurance. Each subsequent year (2nd-5th) adds a
further 10% for cost escalation, plus an estimated term-loan component
for investments (land development, minor irrigation, farm equipment).
The 5th-year short-term limit plus the estimated long-term requirement
becomes the Maximum Permissible Limit (MPL) — this MPL is the actual
Kisan Credit Card limit.

Margin: Nil for crop loans under the Krishi Card Scheme.

Security:
Up to Rs. 1 lakh — crops/assets created from the loan serve as
security. Above Rs. 1 lakh — crops/assets plus one of: land
charge/mortgage under the state's Agricultural Credit Operations Act
(valued at 100% of the loan amount, 75% for marginal/small farmers),
a lien on liquid securities (NSC/FDR/KVP), or a third-party guarantee.

Validity: The PNB Krishi Card is valid for 5 years from the sanction
date.

This is a real, sourced sanction-rule summary, not a complete
substitute for the bank's own current scheme document — confirm the
current effective rate and any updated terms for a specific
farmer/loan size with the bank or nearest branch.
