PNB Loan / Overdraft Against Fixed Deposit (e-OD)

PNB's Online Overdraft (e-OD) against Fixed Deposit offers an interest
rate of 1% above the interest rate allowed on the underlying deposit
for the general public, with an advance amount from Rs. 25,000 up to
Rs. 50 lakh, available digitally via Retail Internet Banking or the
PNB ONE mobile app.

Amount of Advance:
- Minimum: Rs. 25,000.
- Maximum: Rs. 50 lakh.

Loan Period: up to 3 years, or the FD's residual period, whichever is
less.

Eligible Fixed Deposit: FD in the same name with a residual period of
up to 3 years. Tax Saver / Capital Gains FDs are NOT eligible.

Margin (General Public):
- 5% — if maturity period remaining at the time of granting advance is
  up to 2 years.
- 7.5% — if maturity period remaining is above 2 years and up to 3 years.

Margin (Staff & Ex-Staff):
- Up to Rs. 10 lakh: 5%.
- Above Rs. 10 lakh: as applicable to the general public.

Rate of Interest (Staff & Ex-Staff):
- Advance up to Rs. 10 lakh: at par with the FD's own interest rate.
- Advance above Rs. 10 lakh: 1% above the FD's interest rate (same as
  general public).

Special Terms:
- One OD per one FD — multiple FDs cannot be combined for one OD limit.
- No stamped overdraft agreement or physical documentation required.
- Periodical FD interest auto-credits to the loan account.
- The e-OD is liquidated automatically on FD maturity; surplus funds
  credit back to the linked account.
- Cheque book facility available on the e-OD account.
- FD auto-renewal mandate is cancelled once e-OD is availed against it.
- Partial withdrawal from the FD is not allowed once e-OD is active.
- The e-OD account cannot be closed until the FD matures, even if its
  balance reaches zero or goes into credit.

Verify the current rate and margin structure with the bank before
quoting to a customer, since these are revised periodically.
