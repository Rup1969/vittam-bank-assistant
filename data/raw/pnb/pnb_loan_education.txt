Punjab National Bank Education Loan

Interest Rate:
Floating rate reported at approximately 8.10%-10.10% p.a.; a fixed-rate
option is also reported at approximately 9.10%-11.60% p.a. Actual rate
depends on the loan amount, course/institution category, and whether
collateral is offered.

Repayment Structure:
Moratorium period of the course duration plus one year (i.e. repayment
EMIs typically begin one year after course completion), with a
repayment tenure of up to 15 years thereafter.

Coverage:
Covers tuition fees, hostel/accommodation charges, books and equipment,
and other course-related expenses for eligible domestic and
international courses, per standard IBA education-loan-scheme norms
that most public sector banks including PNB follow.

This is a headline reference — course eligibility lists, collateral
requirements above a certain loan amount, and the exact applicable rate
should be confirmed with the bank for a specific applicant and course.
