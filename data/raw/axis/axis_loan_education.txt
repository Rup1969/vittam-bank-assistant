Axis Bank Education Loan

Axis Bank's education loan interest rate is Repo Rate linked, starting
from Repo + 3.5% p.a., for an unsecured loan of up to Rs. 1.5 crore for
higher studies in India or abroad.

Loan Amount:
- Maximum without collateral: Rs. 1.5 crore.
- Maximum with collateral: as per the security/collateral value offered.

Key Features:
- Tax benefit under Section 80(E) (old tax regime).
- Loan tenure up to 15 years.
- Pre-admission sanction available (sanction letter issued before
  admission, based on applicant profile).
- Existing education loan balance transfer at zero fees.

Types of Education Loan:
- Loan for Higher Study: for higher education in India or abroad; no
  collateral security required up to Rs. 7.5 lakh; no pre-closure
  charges; door-step service.
- Loan for Working Professionals: unsecured loan up to Rs. 40 lakh for
  working professionals; no co-applicant required; repayment tenure up
  to 10 years.
- Balance Transfer: switch an existing high-cost education loan to
  Axis Bank to reduce the monthly EMI; no pre-closure charges;
  door-step service.

Rates are Repo-Rate-linked and revised periodically; verify the current
applicable rate and spread with the bank before quoting to a customer.
