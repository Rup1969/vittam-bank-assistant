Axis Bank Kisan Credit Card / Kisan Power

Purpose:
A revolving credit facility for farmers' cultivation and personal needs
under the nationwide Kisan Credit Card scheme, positioned as an
alternative to unorganized-sector high-interest borrowing. Also
branded "Kisan Power" for a related farmer-funding product line.

Loan Amount:
Eligible for loans up to Rs. 250 lakh (Rs. 2.5 crore) — a notably high
ceiling compared to most other banks' KCC schemes, which typically cap
around Rs. 3 lakh for the subsidized-rate tier specifically (larger
KCC loans above that threshold move to standard, non-subsidized
pricing).

Digital KCC (RBI Innovation Hub Pilot):
Axis Bank, via a partnership with the RBI Innovation Hub's Public Tech
Platform for Frictionless Credit, piloted a fully digital, no-
paperwork Kisan Credit Card in Madhya Pradesh, available up to Rs.
1,60,000 — the collateral-free KCC threshold under standard RBI rules.

This is a headline reference — confirm the current effective rate
(including any government interest subvention applicable for the
subsidized tier) and exact collateral thresholds with the bank or
nearest branch.
