Axis Bank personal loan interest rate starts from 9.99% p.a.
(officially advertised), with a broader reported range of 9.99% to
22% p.a. depending on credit score, income, and loan amount.

This is a headline reference — personal loan pricing is highly
profile-dependent; always confirm the applicant's actual quoted rate
and eligibility with the bank.
