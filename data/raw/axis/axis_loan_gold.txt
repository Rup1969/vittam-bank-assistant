Axis Bank Gold Loan

Interest Rate:
Floating rate option: reported range 9.75%-17.00% p.a. Fixed rate
option: reported flat at 17.00% p.a. — the fixed-rate option is priced
at the TOP of the floating range, meaning most applicants would likely
find the floating option more competitive unless they specifically
value payment certainty.

Fees:
Processing fee reported at 0.50% of the gold loan value.

Structure:
Loan secured against pledged gold ornaments/jewellery; exact rate and
eligible amount depend on gold purity, weight, and prevailing gold
valuation at the time of the loan.

This is a headline reference — always confirm the current rate,
loan-to-value ratio, and which rate type (fixed/floating) applies with
the bank before quoting, since gold loan terms are sensitive to gold
price movements and are reviewed frequently.
