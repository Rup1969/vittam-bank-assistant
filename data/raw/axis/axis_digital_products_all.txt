Axis Bank — Digital Banking Products

open by Axis Bank (formerly "Axis Mobile"):
Axis Bank's mobile banking app — rebranded from "Axis Mobile" to "open
by Axis Bank." Covers UPI payments, adding payees, scan-and-pay, and
creating a personal UPI QR code. Supports mobile/DTH recharges and
standard account management. Both names ("Axis Mobile" and "open by
Axis Bank") may still appear across app stores/search results during
the rebrand transition.

UPI Features:
Real-time instant transfers, available even on weekends/holidays. Users
can create and share a unique UPI ID, and set up to 10 frequent
transactions as favorites for one-click repeat payments. UPI LITE is
also supported for faster, smaller transactions. Even non-Axis Bank
customers can use the app's UPI service.

BHIM Axis Pay:
A separate, dedicated UPI-only app alongside the main mobile banking
app.

Security:
A 6-digit MPIN validates fund transfers and app transactions. An
"enhanced Safety Center" feature helps customers monitor and control
their fund transfers and UPI activity.

Confirm current app names (the Axis-Mobile-to-open rebrand may still be
rolling out) and UPI limits with the bank, since these are periodically
revised by NPCI/RBI guidance as well as bank policy.
