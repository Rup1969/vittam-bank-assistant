Axis Bank Savings Account — Minimum Balance Requirements

Axis Bank's savings account minimum balance requirement depends on the
account variant, ranging from zero (Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account)
up to Rs. 10 lakh (Burgundy premium banking), with the most commonly
opened variants requiring Rs. 12,000 to Rs. 25,000 as monthly average
balance.

Minimum balance by account variant (as listed on Axis Bank's official
account-finder page):
- Easy Access Digital Savings Account: Min. Avg. Balance Rs. 12,000;
  initial funding Rs. 16,000.
- Liberty Digital Savings Account: Min. Avg. Balance Rs. 25,000; initial
  funding Rs. 25,000.
- Burgundy Digital Savings Account (premium): Min. Avg. Balance Rs. 10
  lakh; initial funding Rs. 5 lakh.
- Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA) / PMJDY account: NO minimum
  balance requirement at all — "top-tier banking facilities without the
  necessity of maintaining a minimum balance." Available to resident
  individuals, including minors, who don't already hold a BSBD/PMJDY
  account elsewhere. Comes with a free RuPay debit card and zero balance
  facility.

Axis Bank's official account-finder tool also lists Regular, Premium,
Women's, Senior Citizen, Kids, and Small & Basic account categories with
minimum-balance filter bands from "Zero Balance" up to "Rs. 5 Lakh+" —
the exact variant and its balance requirement should be confirmed
against the specific product page before quoting, since Axis offers
around 20 distinct savings account variants in total.

Interest is credited quarterly. Fees and charges vary by account variant
— check the specific product's fees page before quoting non-maintenance
charges.

Rates and minimum-balance requirements are revised periodically; verify
against Axis Bank's official website before quoting to a customer as
final.
