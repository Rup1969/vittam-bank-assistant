Axis Bank Fixed Deposit Interest Rates (below Rs. 3 crore)

Axis Bank's current fixed deposit (FD) interest rate for the general
public ranges from 3.00% to 6.50% per annum across tenures from 7 days
to 10 years, with senior citizens earning an additional 0.50% to 0.75%
depending on tenure. The 1-year (1 year to 1 year 10 days) FD interest
rate is 6.25% for the general public and 6.75% for senior citizens;
Axis's highest published slab is 6.50% general / up to 7.25% senior
(5 years to 10 years, senior citizen only — the general-public rate is
capped at 6.50% across every tenure from 1 year onward).

Effective 14 August 2026, domestic retail term deposit rates by tenure
(general public / senior citizen):
- 7-14 days: 3.00% / 3.50%
- 15-29 days: 3.00% / 3.50%
- 30-45 days: 3.25% / 3.75%
- 46-60 days: 4.00% / 4.50%
- 61-87 days: 4.75% / 5.25%
- 88 days to 3 months 24 days: 5.00% / 5.50%
- 3 months 25 days to less than 4 months: 5.00% / 5.50%
- 4 months to less than 6 months: 5.00% / 5.50%
- 6 months to less than 9 months: 5.50% / 6.00%
- 9 months to less than 1 year: 5.75% / 6.25%
- 1 year to 1 year 10 days: 6.25% / 6.75%
- 1 year 11 days to less than 13 months: 6.25% / 6.75%
- 13 months to less than 15 months: 6.25% / 6.75%
- 15 months to less than 18 months: 6.45% / 6.95%
- 18 months to less than 2 years: 6.50% / 7.00%
- 2 years to less than 3 years: 6.50% / 7.00%
- 3 years to less than 5 years: 6.50% / 7.00%
- 5 years to 10 years: 6.50% / 7.25%

Senior Citizen Premium: an additional 0.50% for most tenures, rising to
0.75% for the 5-to-10-year band specifically.

Note: this data is sourced from Axis Bank's own downloadable rate-card
PDF, not its HTML rate page — the HTML page only displays 2 summary
tenure bands ("1 year to 1 year 10 days" and "18 Months < 2 Years").
The full table above is the complete, precise picture.

Axis Bank's rate card also separately lists a Domestic Fixed Deposits
Plus variant (no premature withdrawal) and several bulk-deposit tiers
(Rs. 3 crore and above) with their own rate tables — this document
covers only the standard, premature-withdrawal-allowed, retail (below
Rs. 3 crore) tier.

Rates are revised periodically; verify the current card rate with the
bank before quoting for a large deposit.
