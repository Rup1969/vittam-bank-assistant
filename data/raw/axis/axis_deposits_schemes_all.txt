Axis Bank — Deposit Scheme Variants (Recurring Deposit & Tax Saver FD)

Axis Bank offers a Recurring Deposit (RD) starting from a minimum
monthly instalment of Rs. 500, with tenure from 6 months up to 10 years,
and a 5-year Tax Saver Fixed Deposit for tax-saving investors — both
distinct from the standard callable FD covered in axis_fd_rates.txt.

Recurring Deposit (RD):
- Minimum monthly instalment: Rs. 500.
- Tenure: flexible, 6 months up to 10 years.
- Interest compounded quarterly; the rate remains fixed for the chosen
  tenure once the RD is opened.
- TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) applies as per prevailing Income Tax
  regulations.
- Designed for disciplined monthly savings toward a specific goal
  (e.g. home, education, emergency fund).

Tax Saver Fixed Deposit:
- Minimum investment: Rs. 100 (in multiples of Rs. 100).
- Maximum investment: Rs. 1,50,000 per financial year.
- Lock-in period: 5 years (no premature withdrawal permitted).
- Tax benefit: deduction of up to Rs. 1.5 lakh under Section 123 read
  with Schedule XV of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (the current tax-saving
  FD provision — supersedes the earlier Section 80C reference used for
  older tax-saver deposits).
- Interest payout options: Reinvestment/Cumulative (quarterly
  compounding), Quarterly Payout, or Monthly Payout — depositor's
  choice at account opening.
- Interest rates automatically shown are for Resident Deposits below
  Rs. 3 crore.
- TDS applies to accumulated interest per prevailing tax rates.

Both schemes are separate products from Axis Bank's standard FD (see
axis_fd_rates.txt) — verify current RD and Tax Saver FD rates on the
bank's live rate card before quoting to a customer, since these are
revised periodically.
