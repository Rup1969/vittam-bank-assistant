Axis Bank Loan Against Property (LAP) / Mortgage Loan

Axis Bank's Loan Against Property interest rate ranges from 9.15% to
10.25% per annum (effective rate, floating, Repo-Rate-linked), for
tenures of up to 20 years, with the exact rate depending on loan type
and PSL (Priority Sector Lending) category.

Rate Table — Term Loans:
- Floating Rate, PSL Category: Repo Rate + 3.90% to Repo Rate + 4.75%;
  effective rate 9.15% - 10.00% p.a.
- Floating Rate, Non-PSL Category: Repo Rate + 4.15% to Repo Rate +
  5.00%; effective rate 9.40% - 10.25% p.a.

Rate Table — Overdraft Loans:
- Floating Rate, PSL Category: Repo Rate + 4.15% to Repo Rate + 4.75%;
  effective rate 9.40% - 10.00% p.a.
- Floating Rate, Non-PSL Category: Repo Rate + 4.40% to Repo Rate +
  5.00%; effective rate 9.65% - 10.25% p.a.

Benchmark: floating rates are linked to the RBI Repo Rate (5.25% as of
this rate card). The bank charges an additional spread over this
benchmark. The lending rate for existing customers resets once every
three months, or as decided by the bank, whichever is earlier. The
benchmark rate and applicable spread are reviewed at the start of every
month, and apply until the next review.

Loan Structure: available as Term Loans or Overdraft facility.

Tenure: up to 20 years.

Rates are floating and revised monthly/quarterly per the schedule above;
verify the current benchmark rate and applicable spread with the bank
before quoting to a customer.
