Axis Bank MSME / Zero-Collateral Loans

Zero-Collateral Loans:
Axis Bank offers collateral-free business loans up to Rs. 2 crore
specifically for MSMEs — a notably higher collateral-free ceiling than
several public sector banks' typical Rs. 10 lakh threshold. In line
with RBI regulatory requirements, no collateral security is required at
all for loans up to Rs. 10 lakh (this floor applies across the industry,
not just to Axis).

Digital MSME Lending (RBI Innovation Hub Partnership):
Axis Bank has partnered with the RBI Innovation Hub to offer Kisan
Credit Card and unsecured MSME loans through the Public Tech Platform
for Frictionless Credit — a fully digital process requiring no document
submission. As of the pilot phase, MSME loans through this channel are
available nationally up to Rs. 10 lakh.

This is a headline reference — confirm current interest rates,
processing fees, and eligibility criteria (including which channel/
scheme applies) with the bank before advising a customer.
