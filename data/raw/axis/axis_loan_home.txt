Axis Bank Home Loan

Axis Bank's home loan interest rate currently starts from around
7.95%-8.35% per annum for well-qualified applicants, with a broader
reported range of 8.35%-11.90% p.a. depending on CIBIL score, customer
profile, loan variant, and job profile.

Rate Type:
Both fixed and floating interest rate options are offered.

Special Offer:
A concessional interest rate is available for female applicants —
women applying alone, or jointly with their spouse, can receive a
slightly lower rate than the standard applicant rate.

Tenure:
Up to 30 years.

This is a headline reference — sources show a fairly wide range for
the "starting" rate specifically (7.95% to 8.35% across different
snapshots); confirm the current rate and the applicant's specific
quoted spread with the bank before quoting.
