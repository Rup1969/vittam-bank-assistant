Axis Bank Loan Against Fixed Deposit (Overdraft Against FD / ODFD)

Axis Bank's Loan/Overdraft Against Fixed Deposit (ODFD) offers up to
85% of the value of an Axis Bank Fixed Deposit, available 24x7 through
a digital, instant process, with no fixed headline interest rate
published — the page states only "competitive rates," confirm the
exact rate at application.

Key Features:
- Avail up to 85% of the value of your Fixed Deposit (Axis Bank FDs
  only — FDs held with other banks are not eligible).
- 24x7 overdraft facility — digital, instant, simple application.
- No prepayment charges.
- Interest charged only on the amount utilised, for the duration
  utilised.
- No need to break/liquidate the FD to access funds — the FD continues
  earning interest.

Eligibility: the ODFD facility is available to Individuals (Resident
Indian; Guardian/GPA against a deposit held by a minor; NRI / Person
of Indian Origin / Overseas Citizen of India), HUF, and proprietorship
concerns (per the bank's eligibility list).

This is a headline reference — since no fixed rate is published on the
page, confirm the current applicable rate with the bank before quoting
to a customer.
