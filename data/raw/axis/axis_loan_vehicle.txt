Axis Bank Car / Vehicle Loan

Axis Bank's new car loan interest rate ranges from 8.85% to 11.70% per
annum (effective rate of interest), for loans up to 7 years tenure,
starting from Rs. 1 lakh with up to 100% on-road funding.

Rate Table — New Car Loans (per official rate card):
- 1-year MCLR: 8.85%
- Spread over MCLR: 0.0% - 2.90%
- Effective ROI Range (per annum), tenure up to 36 months: 8.85% - 11.70%
- Effective ROI Range (per annum), tenure over 36 months: 8.85% - 11.70%
- Reset: No reset (up to 36 months)
- Processing Fee: between Rs. 3,500 and Rs. 12,000
- Documentation Charges: Rs. 700

(A separate, lower rate table applies for Used Car Loans — see the
bank's "For a Used Car" tab on the same page for those figures.)

Key Features:
- Loan starting from Rs. 1 lakh.
- Up to 100% on-road funding.
- Maximum tenure of up to 7 years.
- Sanction in less than 5 minutes (per the bank's own marketing claim).
- Quick disbursal — as little as 30 minutes.
- Flexible repayment: pre-payment/part-payment options available.
- Special benefits for priority banking and other premium segments.

Loan Variants: New Car Loan, and Used Car Loan (Sale Purchase), both
starting from Rs. 1 lakh.

Rates are revised periodically; verify the current applicable rate,
spread, and processing fee with the bank before quoting to a customer.
