SBI Education Loan

Interest Rate:
Starting from 6.90% p.a. (terms and conditions apply). The page does
not display a single last-updated date for this headline rate —
verify against the current rates page before quoting to a customer.

Eligibility:
- Resident Indian, or OCI (Overseas Citizen of India) resident in
  India.
- A co-applicant (typically a parent or guardian) may be required in
  certain cases.
- Admission must be secured at a recognised institution (in India or
  overseas, depending on the scheme).

Purpose:
Financing higher education, including studies at Quality Higher
Educational Institutions (QHEIs) in India, overseas studies, and skill
development courses.

Scheme Variants (indicative loan-amount bands):
- Skill Loan: up to Rs. 7.5 lakh.
- Studies Abroad / Global Ed-Vantage scheme: above Rs. 20 lakh (for
  overseas study financing).
- Scholar Loan Scheme: for premier institutions (IITs, IIMs, NITs,
  etc.) — offers up to 100% financing with no processing fee.
- Student Loan Scheme: for Indian nationals pursuing higher education
  with secured admission, in India or abroad.

Government-Backed Route:
Applicants can also apply through the PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme
(pmvidyalaxmi.co.in / sbi.bank.in/web/personal-banking/sbi-pm-vidyalaxmi),
which offers collateral-free, guarantor-free education loans to
students admitted at QHEIs, aggregating multiple lenders including
SBI.

Documents and exact tenure/moratorium terms vary by scheme — check the
specific scheme page (Student Loan, Scholar Loan, Global Ed-Vantage)
or a branch for a firm quote before advising a customer.
