SBI Loan / Overdraft Against Fixed Deposit

As shown on SBI's official loan-against-securities rates page, last
updated 15 August 2025:

- Loan against SBI Time Deposits (domestic): 1.00% over the interest
  rate paid on the underlying time deposit.
- Loan against NRO/NRE Fixed Deposits: 1.00% over the interest rate
  paid on the underlying time deposit.
- Loan against FCNR(B) Deposit: 2.90% over the 1-year MCLR (which was
  8.70% as of that page, giving an effective ~11.60%).

Key Features:
- Up to 90% of the fixed deposit's value can be borrowed.
- The FD does not need to be broken and keeps earning its own interest.
- No prepayment penalty.

Related — Loan Against Securities (same page):
- Loan against mutual fund units: 9.95% p.a. (w.e.f. 15 December 2025).
- Loan against NSC/KVP: 11.20% p.a.

Given the underlying deposit rate changes periodically, the effective
loan rate should be recalculated against the current FD rate rather
than assumed fixed.
