SBI Personal Loan (Xpress Credit) — Eligibility, Fees, and Documents

Eligibility:
- For employees of government, defence, railway, police, PSU, and
  corporate sectors holding an SBI Salary Package Account.
- Age: 21 to 60 years (case-by-case consideration for government/
  defence employees above 60).
- Minimum service duration: 6 months for government/defence employees,
  12 months for corporate employees.
- Minimum monthly salary: Rs. 20,000 for government/defence employees,
  Rs. 25,000 for corporate employees.
- Indian national with a satisfactory credit score per bank policy.

Processing Fee:
Up to 1.50% of the loan amount (minimum Rs. 1,000, maximum Rs. 15,000)
plus GST. Waivers/discounts available for defence-sector employees,
certain salary package variants, government employees (50% waiver),
and applicants with a CIBIL score of 800 or above.

Documents Required:
PAN, last 6 months' salary slips, employment proof, latest ITR/Form 16,
6 months' salary account bank statements, and standard KYC documents
(requirements are lighter for existing SBI customers).

Other Features:
Low/no hidden administrative charges, minimal documentation for
existing customers, no prepayment penalty. An "Approval in Principle"
letter (where issued) is valid for 45 days; final approval depends on
satisfactory KYC, income, and credit-worthiness verification.
