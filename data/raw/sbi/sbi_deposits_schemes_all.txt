SBI — COMPLETE DEPOSIT SCHEMES

========================================
SECTION A: SAVINGS BANK ACCOUNTS
========================================

Current SB Interest Rate: 2.50% p.a., effective 15 June 2025. Interest
credited at calendar-quarter intervals. No minimum balance required on
the standard account or the BSBD variants (see below).

1. SAVINGS BANK ACCOUNT (REGULAR)
Standard retail savings account. Can be opened via Video KYC (remote,
digital) or at a branch.

2. BASIC SAVINGS BANK DEPOSIT ACCOUNT (BSBDA)
Minimum balance: NIL. For customers with minimal banking/documentation
needs. Available both via branch and as a simplified digital variant.

3. BASIC SAVINGS BANK DEPOSIT SMALL ACCOUNT (BSBDA-SMALL)
Minimum balance: NIL, but balance at any point must not exceed
Rs. 50,000. For customers unable to provide full KYC documents
(financial inclusion).

4. SAVINGS ACCOUNT FOR MINORS — PEHLAKADAM
Target: Minors of any age.
Operation: Jointly with parent/guardian, or singly by parent/guardian
on the minor's behalf.

5. SAVINGS ACCOUNT FOR MINORS — PEHLIUDAAN
Target: Minors aged 10 years and above who can sign uniformly.
Operation: Singly operated by the minor.
Both PehlaKadam and PehliUdaan: no monthly average balance
requirement, maximum balance cap Rs. 10 lakh, Photo ATM-cum-Debit Card
with Rs. 5,000 withdrawal/POS limit, mobile banking capped at Rs. 2,000
per day, personalised chequebook (10 leaves), auto-sweep facility with
a Rs. 20,000 threshold, interest calculated on daily balance.

6. SAVINGS PLUS ACCOUNT
A Savings Bank account linked to the Multi Option Deposit (MOD)
scheme — surplus balance above a threshold auto-sweeps into a term
deposit and earns FD-linked interest, while remaining accessible.

7. MOTOR ACCIDENT CLAIM ACCOUNT (MACT)
For holding court-directed motor accident compensation amounts;
court-monitored withdrawals.

8. RESIDENT FOREIGN CURRENCY (DOMESTIC) ACCOUNT — RFC(D)
For residents to hold foreign currency earned abroad (e.g. from
overseas employment) domestically.

Other salary/premium account packages exist (e.g. Corporate Salary
Package) — check the relevant scheme page for exact eligibility and
benefits.

========================================
SECTION B: TERM / FIXED DEPOSIT SCHEMES
========================================

Retail Domestic Term Deposit rates effective 15 December 2025 — see
sbi_fd_rates / sbi_rates_deposit_structured for the full tenor table.

1. FIXED DEPOSIT (REGULAR)
Standard term deposit; rate depends on tenor (7 days to 10 years) —
see sbi_fd_rates.

2. RECURRING DEPOSIT (RD)
Tenure 12-120 months, minimum monthly instalment Rs. 100 (multiples of
Rs. 10 thereafter). Rate tracks the equivalent-tenor FD rate.

3. HAR GHAR LAKHPATI
A newer variant of the Recurring Deposit scheme, structured to help a
depositor reach a target lump-sum ("lakhpati") through disciplined
monthly saving — exact target/tenure combinations and rate should be
confirmed on SBI's current scheme page.

4. MULTI OPTION DEPOSIT (MOD)
A term deposit linked to a Savings/Current account: functions as a
combination of a transactional account and a deposit. Funds can be
withdrawn in units of Rs. 1,000 from the deposit by issuing a cheque
against the linked SB account, without breaking the whole deposit.

5. ANNUITY DEPOSIT SCHEME
A one-time lump-sum deposit that pays back a fixed monthly amount
(principal + interest) to the depositor, with payouts starting on the
anniversary date of the month of deposit.

6. SBI TAX SAVINGS SCHEME, 2006
5-year mandatory lock-in term deposit; qualifies for a tax deduction
under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act (up to Rs. 1.5 lakh/year, same
limit as the general 80C cap). Rate is the same as the regular FD rate
for that tenor.

7. SBI PATRONS
A term deposit offering tailored specifically for super senior
citizens, with enhanced benefits/rate over the standard senior-citizen
premium — confirm the exact extra premium on the current rates page.

8. SBI GREEN RUPEE TERM DEPOSIT (SGRTD)
An environmentally-themed term deposit; funds are earmarked toward
green/eco-friendly initiatives.

9. SPECIAL TERM DEPOSIT
A premium deposit product offering an enhanced rate for specific
tenure bands — check current terms before quoting.

10. NON-CALLABLE TERM DEPOSIT — RETAIL (NCD-R) / BULK (NCD-B)
Premature withdrawal is not permitted (or heavily restricted); in
exchange, the deposit typically earns a higher rate than a regular,
callable FD of the same tenor. NCD-B applies to bulk-value deposits
(Rs. 3 crore and above), NCD-R to retail-value deposits.

11. AMRIT VRISHTI (444 DAYS)
A limited-tenor special FD at 444 days. Per SBI's NRI-services rate
page (15 March 2026), this tenor is priced at 6.45% p.a. for domestic
retail deposits below Rs. 3 crore — always check the current live rate,
since special-tenor schemes like this are periodically revised or
retired and replaced with a new special tenor.

12. MACAD (MOTOR ACCIDENT CLAIMS ANNUITY/TERM DEPOSIT ACCOUNT)
For managing motor accident claim settlement funds under court order,
similar in purpose to MACT but structured as a term/annuity deposit.

========================================
SECTION C: NRI DEPOSITS
========================================
See sbi_nri_deposits.txt for the full NRE Fixed Deposit rate table.
SBI also offers NRO and FCNR(B) term deposits, and NRE/NRO savings
accounts — each carries its own separate rate table, distinct from
domestic retail rates. Do not quote a domestic rate to an NRI customer
or vice versa.

Verify all rates and scheme availability against SBI's live
interest-rates and deposits pages before quoting, since special-tenor
schemes (like Amrit Vrishti) and rates change periodically.
