SBI Personal Loan (Xpress Credit) interest rate starts from 10.00%
p.a. (terms and conditions apply), effective 1 April 2026, charged on
a daily reducing balance.

Loan Amount: Minimum Rs. 1 lakh, maximum Rs. 50 lakh, subject to an
EMI/Net Monthly Income (NMI) ratio up to 65% and a cap of roughly 30
times NMI.

Tenure: Minimum 6 months, maximum 84 months, must close by the
applicant's retirement or age 60, whichever is earlier.
