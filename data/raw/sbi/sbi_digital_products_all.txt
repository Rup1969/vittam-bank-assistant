SBI — COMPLETE DIGITAL PRODUCTS & SERVICES

========================================
SECTION A: YONO — UNIFIED APP
========================================

1. YONO SBI ("You Only Need One")
SBI's unified app for banking, UPI payments, shopping, and rewards —
account opening, payments, loans, and insurance access from one app.
Also lets a customer link and manage accounts held at OTHER banks from
within the same app.

2. UPI VIA YONO (BHIM UPI)
Instant UPI payments — send/receive by searching a contact or entering
a UPI ID/VPA. No charge for UPI payments made through YONO; no hidden
transaction fees.

3. YONO CASH
Card-less cash withdrawal (launched 16 March 2019) — withdraw at any
SBI ATM or most SBI merchant PoS terminals/Customer Service Points
(CSPs) in India using an in-app generated reference number/OTP,
without a physical debit card.

4. YONO LITE
A lighter-weight mobile banking variant of YONO, focused on core
banking/transaction functions.

========================================
SECTION B: MISSED CALL / SMS / WHATSAPP BANKING
========================================

5. SBI QUICK — MISSED CALL BANKING
Free service: get account balance, mini statement, and more via a
missed call or a keyword SMS from the mobile number registered with
the account — no data connection needed.

6. SMS BANKING
Send 'BAL' to 9223766666 for account balance, or 'MSTMT' to the same
number for the last 5 transactions.

7. WHATSAPP BANKING
Send "Hi" to SBI's WhatsApp Banking number (+91 90226 90226) to access
banking services conversationally — supports multiple regional
languages and voice commands. Covers balance enquiry, mini statement,
account details, loan account details, and FD details.

========================================
SECTION C: CARDS
========================================

8. DEBIT CARDS
Multiple variants: SBI Virtual Debit Card (card-not-physical, instantly
generated in the YONO app), SBI Platinum International Debit Card, SBI
Global International Debit Card, SBI Mastercard World Debit Card, plus
standard classic/gold-tier cards. Supports ATM withdrawal, POS,
contactless tap-and-pay, and online shopping; international usage on
the Platinum/Global/World variants.

9. CREDIT CARDS (SBI Card)
Issued via SBI's credit-card arm (SBI Card) — reward points, cashback
on select categories, EMI conversion, and premium-tier benefits
(lounge access etc. on higher variants). Apply via the SBI Card portal
or YONO.

10. PREPAID CARDS
Reloadable cards not linked to a bank account in the same way as a
debit card — used for gifting, travel, or general prepaid spending.

========================================
SECTION D: PAYMENTS & TOLL
========================================

11. SBI FASTAG (NETC FASTAG)
RFID-based electronic toll payment — sticker tag on the windscreen,
toll deducted automatically while the vehicle is in motion, funded
from a prepaid wallet or linked savings account. Minimum balance at
first issuance: Rs. 100 for cars/jeeps (Class 4), Rs. 300 for
commercial vehicles (Classes 5-7, 12, 15, 16). Recharge via the FASTag
portal, net banking, debit/credit card, UPI, or authorised agents; cash
recharge capped at Rs. 50,000/month for eligible accounts. Apply
online or at an authorised SBI Point-of-Service location — needs the
vehicle's Registration Certificate and KYC documents.

12. BHARAT BILL PAY / BILL PAYMENTS
Pay mobile recharge, DTH, electricity, gas, or FASTag recharge through
Bharat Bill Pay, earning reward points on eligible payments.

========================================
SECTION E: OTHER SERVICES
========================================

13. DOORSTEP BANKING (DSB)
Available via the mobile app or by calling the toll-free number
1800-1111-03 (9 AM-4 PM on working days). Covers services like cash/
cheque pickup and document collection at the customer's registered
address.

14. SBI SECURITIES
SBI's own platform for demat, trading (NSE/BSE), and investment
services — a separate arm from core retail banking.

For product-specific limits (UPI transaction caps, YONO Cash
withdrawal limits, FASTag recharge limits) and eligibility, check
SBI's current help pages, since these limits are revised periodically.
