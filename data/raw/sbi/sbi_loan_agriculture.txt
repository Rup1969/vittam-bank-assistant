SBI Agriculture Loans

SBI's agriculture lending page groups its offerings into two broad
categories:
- The Bank's own Agriculture Products.
- Government Sponsored Schemes (routed through agriculture/rural
  banking channels).

The landing page states that interest rates "vary for different Agri
Products" and does not display a single consolidated rate — each
individual agriculture product/scheme has its own rate, eligibility,
and loan-amount terms on its own page.

This is a summary-level entry only. For a specific agriculture loan
(e.g. crop loan, Kisan Credit Card, allied-activity loans), check the
specific scheme page or a branch, since the rate and eligibility
depend heavily on the exact product and government scheme involved.
