SBI Personal Gold Loan (Jewel Loan)

Interest Rate (as of 16 June 2026), by variant:
- 12-Month Bullet Repayment: 9.15% p.a.
- Personal Gold Loan (EMI-based): 10.10% p.a.
- Liquid Gold Loan (Overdraft): 10.10% p.a.
- Blended/mean rate across variants: approximately 9.25% p.a.

Eligibility:
- Age 18 years and above.
- Any individual with a steady source of income, including bank
  employees and pensioners.
- Income documentation is required only for loans exceeding Rs. 2.5
  lakh.

Loan Amount:
Minimum Rs. 20,000, maximum Rs. 1 crore per individual. An individual
may hold up to 3 concurrent gold loans, subject to an overall Rs. 50
lakh ceiling across them.

Loan-to-Value / Margin (i.e. the portion of gold value NOT lent):
- EMI-based variant: 28% margin.
- 12-month bullet repayment variant: 35% margin.
- Overdraft variant: 26% margin.

Tenure:
- EMI-based: up to 36 months.
- 12-month bullet repayment: 12 months.
- Overdraft: up to 36 months.

Processing Fee (slab-based):
- Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 2,00,000: 0.50% + GST (minimum Rs. 500).
- Rs. 2,00,001 to Rs. 25,00,000: 0.30% + GST (minimum Rs. 1,000).
- Above Rs. 25,00,000: 0.25% + GST (maximum Rs. 15,000).

Documents Required:
- Existing KYC-compliant SBI customers: application form plus 2
  passport-size photographs.
- New customers: a copy of any Officially Valid Document (OVD) as
  proof of identity (passport, Aadhaar, voter ID, driving licence, or
  MGNREGA job card) plus photographs.
