SBI Savings Bank Account Interest Rate

As displayed on SBI's official interest rates page, the Savings Bank
Account interest rate is 2.50% p.a., effective 15 June 2025.

This rate applies to the standard Savings Bank Account. Certain other
savings account variants (such as those for minors, or premium savings
products) may carry different terms; check the specific account type.

Rates are subject to change without prior notice and should be confirmed
on SBI's official website before quoting to a customer as final.
