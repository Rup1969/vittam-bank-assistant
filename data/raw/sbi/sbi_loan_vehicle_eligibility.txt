SBI Auto Loan (Car / Two-Wheeler) — Scheme Variants, Purpose, Tenure

Scheme Variants:
- SBI New Car Loan Scheme.
- Certified Pre-Owned Car Loan.
- SBI Loyalty Car Loan Scheme — for existing SBI home loan borrowers.
- SBI Assured Car Loan Scheme — for existing SBI term deposit holders.
- Super Bike Loan Scheme — Rs. 1.50 lakh to Rs. 25 lakh, for premium
  two-wheelers.
- SBI Two-Wheeler Loan Scheme — up to Rs. 3 lakh.
- Green Car Loan — for electric vehicles.

Purpose:
Financing new cars, certified pre-owned vehicles, electric vehicles,
and two-wheelers (including premium bikes under the Super Bike scheme).

Tenure:
Available for 3-5 years and beyond 5 years, depending on the scheme.

Eligibility, exact loan amount ranges (outside Super Bike/Two-Wheeler),
processing fee, margin/down-payment, and documents required are not
published on SBI's general auto loan landing page — these vary by the
specific scheme chosen. Check the individual scheme's page or a branch
for a firm quote.
