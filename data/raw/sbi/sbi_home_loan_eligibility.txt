SBI Home Loan — Eligibility, LTV, Tenure, Fees, and Documents

Eligibility:
- Resident of India, or NRI.
- Minimum age 18 years at loan commencement; repayment must be
  completed by age 70.
- Loan amount is sized off the EMI-to-Net-Monthly-Income (EMI/NMI)
  ratio, which ranges roughly 20%-70% depending on the applicant's
  income slab. Including a co-applicant can increase eligible amount.

Loan-to-Value (LTV):
Up to 75% (for balance-transfer-with-top-up cases per the FAQ page);
LTV for a fresh purchase depends on property value and is governed by
regulatory LTV slabs.

Tenure:
3 to 30 years. Shorter tenure means a higher EMI but less total
interest paid; longer tenure lowers the EMI but increases total
interest.

Processing Fee:
0.35% of the loan amount plus GST, subject to a minimum of Rs. 2,000 +
GST and a maximum of Rs. 10,000 + GST. Additional out-of-pocket costs
may apply: CERSAI charges, legal opinion fee, and valuation fee.

Prepayment:
No prepayment penalty — the loan can be prepaid at any time without
charge.

Documents Required:
- KYC: PAN, proof of identity, proof of address.
- Property papers: sale agreement, occupancy certificate, building
  plans.
- Last 6 months' bank statements.
- Salaried applicants: last 3 months' salary slips plus 2 years'
  Form 16/IT returns.
- Non-salaried applicants: 3 years' IT returns, balance sheets, and
  business licence.

This is a headline/summary reference — confirm the applicant's exact
eligible amount and rate slab with a branch or SBI's home loan portal
before quoting.
