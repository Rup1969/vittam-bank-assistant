SBI — Structured Deposit Rate Table

Savings Bank Account: 2.50% p.a. (effective 15 June 2025)

Retail Domestic Term Deposits (below Rs. 3 crore), rates effective
15 December 2025, General Public | Senior Citizen:
- 7 days to 45 days:      3.05% | 3.55%
- 46 days to 179 days:    4.90% | 5.40%
- 180 days to 210 days:   5.65% | 6.15%
- 211 days to <1 year:    5.90% | 6.40%
- 2 years to 3 years:     6.95% | (senior-citizen premium applies)
- 5 years to 10 years:    7.05% | (senior-citizen premium applies)

(This mirrors sbi_fd_rates.txt — kept here too so the consolidated
rates pool has a single structured reference table, matching the
Indian Bank rates_deposit_structured convention.)

Bulk Term Deposits (Rs. 3 crore and above): priced separately — see
SBI's domestic bulk term deposit rates page for the current table.

Recurring Deposit: follows the same rate table as term deposits of the
equivalent tenor — no separate RD-specific rate is published.

Senior-citizen premium generally applies across term-deposit tenors;
exact premium and any super-senior-citizen slab should be confirmed on
SBI's live rates page, since it is not uniformly listed for every tenor
band on the summary page.
