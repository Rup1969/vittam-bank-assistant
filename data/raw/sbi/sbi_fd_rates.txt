SBI Fixed Deposit Interest Rates (Retail Domestic Term Deposits, below Rs. 3 crore)

As per SBI's official interest rates page, last updated 16 June 2026, rates
effective 15 December 2025 for the general public and senior citizens are:

Tenor: 7 days to 45 days       -> General: 3.05% p.a. | Senior Citizen: 3.55% p.a.
Tenor: 46 days to 179 days     -> General: 4.90% p.a. | Senior Citizen: 5.40% p.a.
Tenor: 180 days to 210 days    -> General: 5.65% p.a. | Senior Citizen: 6.15% p.a.
Tenor: 211 days to <1 year     -> General: 5.90% p.a. | Senior Citizen: 6.40% p.a.
Tenor: 1 year to <2 years      -> General: 6.25% p.a. | Senior Citizen: 6.75% p.a.
Tenor: 2 years to <3 years     -> General: 6.40% p.a. | Senior Citizen: 6.90% p.a.
Tenor: 3 years to <5 years     -> General: 6.30% p.a. | Senior Citizen: 6.80% p.a.
Tenor: 5 years to 10 years     -> General: 6.05% p.a. | Senior Citizen: 7.05% p.a.*

*The 5-10 year senior citizen rate includes an additional 0.50% premium under
the "SBI We-Care" deposit scheme.

SBI Amrit Vrishti (a special 444-day tenor) currently offers 6.45% p.a. for
general customers, with senior citizens eligible for the usual additional
senior citizen benefit on top.

SBI Patrons scheme: Super senior citizens (age 80 and above) get a further
0.10% (10 basis points) above the applicable senior citizen rate. This
additional benefit does not apply to the Recurring Deposit Scheme, Green
Rupee Term Deposit, Tax Savings Scheme 2006, MODS, Capgain Scheme, or
Non-Callable Term Deposits.

Non-Callable Term Deposit (Retail), for deposits from Rs. 1,00,01,000 to
just under Rs. 3 crore:
1 year tenor  -> General: 6.55% p.a. | Senior Citizen: 7.05% p.a.
2 year tenor  -> General: 6.80% p.a. | Senior Citizen: 7.30% p.a.

Rates are subject to change without prior notice and should be confirmed
on SBI's official website before quoting to a customer as final.
