SBI PM Surya Ghar Loan (Solar Rooftop)

As shown on SBI's loan schemes summary page, the PM Surya Ghar Loan
(for residential solar rooftop installation under the government's PM
Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana) is priced between 5.75% and 7.90% p.a.,
with the exact rate varying by loan amount.

Purpose:
Financing installation of solar rooftop panels for residential use,
under the central government's PM Surya Ghar scheme.

Exact eligibility, subsidy interaction, loan amount slabs, and tenure
should be confirmed on SBI's dedicated Surya Ghar loan page or with a
branch, since pricing is amount-tiered.
