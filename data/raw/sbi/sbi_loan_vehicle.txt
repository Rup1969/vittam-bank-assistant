SBI car loan / auto loan interest rate is 8.70% to 9.85% p.a. for
Standard, NRI, and Assured Car Loans; 8.75% to 9.85% p.a. for Green Car
Loans (electric vehicles); and 11.70% to 15.70% p.a. for Two-Wheeler
Loans. Rates are fixed at disbursement.
