SBI Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

SBI's Kisan Credit Card interest rate, eligibility, and loan limit — the
scheme provides timely and adequate credit to farmers to meet
cultivation (production credit) needs plus contingency expenses.

Eligibility:
- Individual owner cultivators.
- Tenant farmers, oral lessees, and share croppers.
- Self-Help Groups (SHGs) or Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) of farmers.

Loan Amount:
Need-based, determined by the cropping pattern, acreage, and the
District Level Technical Committee's Scale of Finance (SOF) for the
crop — no fixed minimum or maximum ceiling is stated.

Interest Rate:
- Up to Rs. 3 lakh: 7% p.a., subject to Government of India interest
  subvention.
- Above Rs. 3 lakh to below Rs. 50 lakh: 3.25% above the 1-Year MCLR.
- Rs. 50 lakh and above: priced per the borrower's Credit Risk
  Assessment (CRA) rating.
- An additional 3% interest subvention applies as a Prompt Repayment
  Incentive (PRI) on loans up to Rs. 3 lakh repaid on time, effectively
  lowering the cost further for prompt-paying farmers.

Margin: Nil.

Security / Collateral:
- Primary security: hypothecation of crops grown / assets created out
  of the loan.
- Collateral is waived for limits up to Rs. 2 lakh (or up to Rs. 3 lakh
  under a tie-up arrangement).
- Above these thresholds: equitable or registered mortgage of land,
  valued at 100% of the loan amount, is required.

Repayment:
Aligned to the crop's cultivation and marketing cycle (short-duration
or long-duration crop, as applicable) — not a fixed EMI schedule.

Tenure:
5 years, with the sanctioned limit eligible for a 10% annual increase,
subject to periodic review.

Additional Benefits:
- RuPay Kisan Credit Card (debit card) issued against the account.
- Optional Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) crop insurance.
- Optional personal accident and health insurance cover.
