SBI NRE Fixed Deposit Interest Rates (for NRI customers)

Effective 15 March 2026:

Tenor                        | Below Rs. 3 crore | Rs. 3 crore & above
1 year to <2 years            | 6.25%              | 6.25%
444 days (AMRIT VRISHTI)      | 6.45%              | not offered at this slab
2 years to <3 years           | 6.40%              | 6.15%
3 years to <5 years           | 6.30%              | 6.00%
5 years to 10 years           | 6.05%              | 6.00%

Key Terms:
- No interest is payable if the NRE deposit is withdrawn before
  completing one year.
- Premature withdrawal penalties apply once past the one-year mark.
- Rates are subject to change without prior notice.

Other NRI Deposit Products (rate not detailed in this note — check
SBI's NRI Services interest-rate pages individually):
- NRO Fixed Deposit — for NRIs' India-sourced (repatriable-limited)
  income.
- FCNR(B) Deposit — foreign-currency denominated term deposit.
- NRE / NRO Savings Bank accounts — separate rates from resident
  savings accounts.
- RFC (Resident Foreign Currency) accounts.
- Loans against NRI deposits — see sbi_loan_against_deposit, which
  already covers the NRO/NRE loan-against-FD spread.

This is an NRI-specific rate table, distinct from the domestic retail
FD table in sbi_fd_rates — do not quote domestic rates to an NRI
customer or vice versa.
