SBI — Structured Lending Rate Table

Home Loan: from 7.25% p.a. (effective 1 April 2026)
Personal Loan: from 10.00% p.a. (effective 1 April 2026)
Education Loan: from 6.90% p.a.
Gold Loan (Personal): from 9.15% p.a. (effective 1 April 2026)
Auto Loan (Car): 8.70% - 9.85% p.a. (Green/EV: 8.75% - 9.85%)
Two-Wheeler Loan: 11.70% - 15.70% p.a.
PM Surya Ghar (Solar Rooftop) Loan: 5.75% - 7.90% p.a. (amount-tiered)
Loan Against Property: 1-year MCLR + spread (1.45%-3.00% depending on
  income type and loan size) — source page stale (2019), reverify.
MSME/SME Loans: External Benchmark Rate (Repo + 2.65%) + product
  spread, or 6-month MCLR for non-MSME business borrowers — detailed
  spread table stale (2018/2020), reverify.
Loan Against Fixed Deposit: 1.00% over the underlying FD's own rate.
Loan Against Mutual Fund Units: 9.95% p.a. (w.e.f. 15 December 2025)
Loan Against NSC/KVP: 11.20% p.a.

All rates above are headline "starting from" or benchmark-plus-spread
figures — the rate actually offered to an individual applicant depends
on credit profile, loan amount, tenure, and scheme chosen. Rates
flagged "stale" above should be re-verified against SBI's live pages
before being quoted as current.
