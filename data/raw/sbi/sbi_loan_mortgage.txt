SBI Loan Against Property (Mortgage Loan)

Yes — SBI offers a Loan Against Property (mortgage loan), priced as a
spread over the 1-year MCLR, reset annually from the date of first
disbursement.

For applicants where salary income is >50% of net monthly income:
- Loan up to Rs. 1 crore: 1.45% + 1-year MCLR
- Rs. 1-2 crore: 2.10% + 1-year MCLR
- Rs. 2-7.50 crore: 2.50% + 1-year MCLR

For applicants where business/profession/rental income is >50% of net
monthly income:
- Loan up to Rs. 1 crore: 2.10% + 1-year MCLR
- Rs. 1-2 crore: 2.60% + 1-year MCLR
- Rs. 2-7.50 crore: 3.00% + 1-year MCLR

Purpose (per SBI's Personal Loan Against Property product, P-LAP):
Personal needs such as education, marriage, or healthcare expenses —
not permitted for business purposes under this scheme. Minimum loan
amount Rs. 10 lakh, maximum Rs. 5 crore (subject to property location),
maximum tenure 15 years.
