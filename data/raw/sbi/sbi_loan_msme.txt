SBI MSME / SME Business Loans

Pricing Basis:
SBI prices MSME loans off the External Benchmark Rate (EBR), currently
Repo Rate + 2.65%, plus a product-specific spread set by the
borrower's rating. Non-MSME business borrowers are typically priced
off the 6-month MCLR instead. The detailed per-product spread table on
SBI's SME segment page was last shown as updated in 2020 (referencing
a 2018 MCLR quarter) — treat those specific spreads as stale and
re-verify before quoting.

Eligibility (CGTMSE-backed collateral-free route):
- Obtaining an Udyam Registration Certificate (URC) is compulsory for
  branches to process an MSME loan proposal — URC is the sole
  criterion used to classify a unit as a Micro or Small Enterprise
  (MSE).
- Loans to MSEs can be covered under the Credit Guarantee Fund Scheme
  for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE/CGS-I), in force since
  1 August 2000.
- Guarantee coverage under CGTMSE is 85% of the credit facility.
- Creation of primary security is not a pre-requisite for the
  guarantee cover, and initiating legal action is not required to
  invoke the guarantee.

Product Range:
SBI's MSME/SME offerings include Asset Backed Loan, E-Dealer Finance
Scheme, SME Smart Score (rating-based scoring model), Fleet Finance,
Commercial Vehicle Loan, SME Credit Card, SME Loan to Women
Entrepreneurs (SBI Asmita), Finance to Start-ups (MSME Udaan), and
government-backed schemes such as PMEGP and PMFME, alongside CGTMSE.

For a specific product's rate, eligibility, and documentation, check
that product's own page or a branch — the umbrella SME segment page
does not carry current figures for every product.
