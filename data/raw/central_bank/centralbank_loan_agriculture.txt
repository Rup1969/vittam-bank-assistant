Central Bank of India Kisan Credit Card (Central KCC)

Central Bank of India's Kisan Credit Card interest rate is tiered by
loan amount (for advances not covered under the government's Interest
Subvention Scheme):
- Up to Rs. 3 lakh: MCLR + 1.35%.
- Rs. 3 lakh to Rs. 10 lakh: MCLR + 2.50%.
- Rs. 10 lakh to Rs. 100 lakh: MCLR + 3.00%.

Purpose:
Covers crop cultivation, dairy, fisheries, poultry, post-harvest
expenses, produce marketing, consumption needs, and maintenance of farm
equipment.

Eligibility:
Individual or joint-borrower owner cultivators, tenant farmers, oral
lessees, share croppers, and Self-Help Groups or Joint Liability Groups
of farmers.

Loan Amount:
Cash credit limits sanctioned for 5-year periods as a short-term
revolving credit facility.

Margin:
Nil — built into the scale of finance.

Security/Collateral:
- Up to Rs. 2 lakh: no collateral required.
- Under a tie-up arrangement: no collateral up to Rs. 3 lakh.
- Above Rs. 2 lakh (outside a tie-up): collateral security required.
- Primary security: hypothecation of crops and farm assets financed by
  the bank.
