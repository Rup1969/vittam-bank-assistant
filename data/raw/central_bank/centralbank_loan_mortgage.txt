Central Bank of India Loan Against Property (Mortgage)

Central Bank of India's loan against property interest rate ranges from
8.65% to 12.25% p.a. (RBLR + 0.40% to RBLR + 4.00%, floating,
RBLR = 8.25% as of this rate card).

This is a real, listed retail loan product offered by the bank; the
product's own landing page is a navigation hub without inline detail
(eligibility, exact LTV, tenure, fees are not separately published on
the site beyond the rate card figure above) — confirm the specific
terms with the bank or a branch before relying on anything beyond the
interest-rate range stated here.
