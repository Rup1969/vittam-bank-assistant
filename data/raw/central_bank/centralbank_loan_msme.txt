Central Bank of India MSME / Business Loan (Cent Business Loan)

Central Bank of India offers working capital, term loans, and non-fund-
based facilities under Cent Business Loan for manufacturers, traders,
processors, self-employed individuals, and professionals holding
enterprise registration certificates.

Loan Amount:
Maximum Rs. 25 crore.

Margin:
25% own contribution required from the borrower.

Tenure:
Maximum 84 months, including a 6-month moratorium period.

Collateral:
Mortgage of self-occupied or vacant residential, commercial, or
industrial property, located in metro/urban/semi-urban/rural areas,
registered under the bank's sole charge.

Interest Rate:
Not explicitly published on this product page — the page directs
customers to the bank's separate lending-rate card / branch for the
current effective rate on business loans; verify directly with the
bank before relying on any assumed figure.
