Central Bank of India Vehicle Loan (Cent Vehicle) — Eligibility, Amount, Fees

Purpose:
Purchase of a new two- or four-wheeler for personal use (not for hiring
or ferrying passengers).

Eligibility:
Age 18-65. Salaried employees (government, PSU, corporate, banks, MNCs),
self-employed professionals, business entrepreneurs, farmers with
regular income, and NRIs (jointly with a resident Indian relative).
Minimum gross annual income: Rs. 3,00,000 for a 4-wheeler, Rs. 1,80,000
for a 2-wheeler. Minimum credit score of 700 across all major bureaus.

Loan Amount:
- 2-wheeler: up to Rs. 10 lakh.
- 4-wheeler: up to Rs. 200 lakh (Indian or foreign vehicles).
- Calculated as 24x monthly salary (salaried) or 2x average annual income
  (others).

Margin:
Minimum 10% up to Rs. 25 lakh loan amount; minimum 20% above Rs. 25 lakh.

Tenure:
60 months (2-wheeler); 84 months (4-wheeler).

Fees:
Processing charge 0.50% of loan amount (+ GST for 4-wheelers) — fully
waived until 31 December 2026. Documentation charges: nil.
