Central Bank of India Home Loan (Cent Home)

Central Bank of India's home loan interest rate ranges from 7.00% to
9.25% p.a., floating, benchmarked to the Repo-Linked Benchmark Rate
(RBLR = 8.25% as of this rate card):
- Cent Grih Lakshmi: Repo + 1.75% to Repo + 3.00% (7.00%-8.25%).
- Cent Home (standard, new/existing property): RBLR - 1.05% to
  RBLR + 0.50% (7.20%-8.75%).
- Cent Home for 3rd/4th/5th house or flat: RBLR - 0.55% to
  RBLR + 1.00% (7.70%-9.25%).

Eligibility:
Individuals aged 18+ with a steady source of income, including those
engaged in agriculture and allied activities. Co-borrowers with
independent income are permitted if the property holder lacks sufficient
income on their own. Minimum credit score of 700 across all major
bureaus (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF).

Loan Amount / Loan-to-Value (LTV):
- Up to Rs. 30 lakh: 90% LTV, minimum 10% margin.
- Rs. 30 lakh to Rs. 75 lakh: 80% LTV, minimum 20% margin.
- Above Rs. 75 lakh: 75% LTV, minimum 25% margin.

Tenure:
Maximum 30 years, or until the borrower turns 75, whichever is earlier.
Loans for repairs/renovation are capped at 10 years.

Processing Fee:
0.50% of loan amount + GST, subject to a maximum of Rs. 20,000 — fully
waived until 31 December 2026.
