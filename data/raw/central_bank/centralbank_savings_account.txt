Central Bank of India Savings Accounts

Central Bank of India offers several savings account variants, each with
its own minimum average monthly balance (AMB) requirement. Interest is
paid at up to 2.9% p.a. across all variants.

- Cent Bachat Khata (basic/budget account): AMB Rs. 50, no-frills, low
  maintenance charges.
- Cent Balbhavishya (for minors): AMB Rs. 100, additional interest rate
  benefit.
- HSS Account: AMB Rs. 500 - Rs. 2,000, online banking access.
- Cent Yuva (for young adults, age 18-30): Zero AMB, special loan rates.
- Cent Param (for armed forces personnel): Zero AMB, special fee waivers.
- Cent Samarth: Zero AMB, comes with a pre-approved overdraft facility
  and free accident insurance.
- Cent Samvridhi: minimum average QUARTERLY balance Rs. 25,000, auto
  sweep-in feature, free ATM-cum-debit card.
- Cent Premium: AMB Rs. 50,000 - Rs. 1,00,000, higher transaction
  limits, free insurance coverage, personalized debit card, dedicated
  relationship manager.

The exact minimum balance requirement depends on which variant a
customer opens; the basic Cent Bachat Khata (Rs. 50) and the zero-balance
variants (Cent Yuva, Cent Param, Cent Samarth) are the lowest-barrier
options.
