Central Bank of India Digital Banking Products

Central Bank of India's UPI payment app is called Cent PAY (also
available as BHIM Cent UPI), supporting digital payments and fund
transfers over the unified payments infrastructure.

Other digital products:
- Mobile Banking App: account management and transactions from a
  smartphone, available on both Android and iOS.
- Cent M-Passbook: mobile access to account activity/statement.
- Cent eeZ: the bank's broader digital banking platform.
- Internet Banking: full online banking portal.

Fund transfer support: IMPS (immediate), NEFT (next-day settlement),
and RTGS (real-time gross settlement), alongside UPI and bill-payment
integration.
