Central Bank of India Education Loan

Central Bank of India's education loan interest rate is tiered by
institute category:
- AAA-category institutes: Repo + 1.50% (6.75% p.a.).
- A-category (MBA/Medical/Engineering courses): RBLR - 0.35% (7.90% p.a.).
- C-category institutes: RBLR + 1.25% (9.50% p.a.).

Schemes Offered:
- Cent Vidyarthi: main education loan scheme, up to Rs. 200 lakh for
  studies in India and abroad, covering fees payable to the
  college/school/hostel.
- Cent Skill Loan: for Indian nationals admitted to government-run or
  NSDC-supported skill-development courses; no age restriction.
- Cent PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme: for meritorious students admitted to India's
  top-ranked Quality Higher Educational Institutions (covers a curated
  list of institutes).
- MBA/E-MBA Education Loan: no margin required, no collateral security
  except assignment of future income.

The actual rate applicable depends on which institute/course category
the student's admission falls under — verify the specific category with
the bank before assuming a rate.
