Central Bank of India — Other Deposit Schemes (Recurring Deposit and More)

Central Bank of India offers recurring deposit (RD) products, including:
- Cent Surakshit Samriddhi RD.
- Cent Swashakti (Flexi RDS) — a flexible recurring deposit variant.

The bank also lists, without inline figures on this page: a Fixed
Deposit Receipt (FDR) scheme, a Senior Citizens Savings Scheme, tax-
saving deposits, and several callable/non-callable term-deposit variants.

This is a summary-level entry only — the recurring-deposit landing page
names these schemes but does not publish minimum amount, tenure, or rate
figures inline; those live on each individual scheme's own sub-page,
which was not separately fetched in this pass. Confirm exact terms with
the bank before relying on any figure for these specific schemes.
