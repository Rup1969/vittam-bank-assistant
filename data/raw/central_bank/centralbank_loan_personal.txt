Central Bank of India Personal Loan (Cent Personal)

Central Bank of India's personal loan interest rate is 11.25% or 11.75%
p.a. (RBLR + 3.00% or RBLR + 3.50%, depending on the applicant's internal
risk rating), floating. A pre-approved variant is priced at RBLR + 3.50%
(11.75%).

Eligibility:
- Permanent government/institutional employees with at least 1 year of
  service.
- Confirmed employees of Indian/multinational companies with at least 3
  years of service.
- Minimum gross annual salary of Rs. 1.80 lakh.
- Must maintain a salary account with the bank.
- Minimum CIBIL score of 700 across all major bureaus (TransUnion CIBIL,
  Experian, Equifax, CRIF).

Loan Amount:
Maximum Rs. 20 lakh, calculated as 24 times gross monthly salary, subject
to the take-home pay remaining at least 50% of gross salary after all
deductions.

Tenure:
Up to 84 EMIs (in multiples of 12 months).

Fees:
- Processing fee: 1.00% of loan amount + GST (nil for defence personnel).
- Documentation charge: Rs. 270 + GST up to Rs. 2 lakh, Rs. 450 + GST
  above Rs. 2 lakh (nil for defence personnel).
