Central Bank of India Fixed Deposit (FD) Interest Rates

Central Bank of India's current fixed deposit (FD) interest rate for the
general public ranges from 3.00% to 6.25% p.a. depending on tenure. The
1-year FD interest rate is 6.10% for the general public and 6.60% for
senior citizens.

Domestic Term Deposit Rates (below Rs. 3 crore), w.e.f. 10.08.2026:

Tenure                              General    Senior Citizen
7-14 days                           3.00%      3.50%
15-30 days                          3.50%      4.00%
31-45 days                          3.50%      4.00%
46-59 days                          4.25%      4.75%
60-90 days                          4.00%      4.50%
91-179 days                         4.25%      4.75%
180-270 days                        5.50%      6.00%
271-364 days                        6.00%      6.50%
1 year to less than 2 years         6.10%      6.60%
2 years to less than 3 years        6.25%      6.75%
3 years to less than 5 years        6.00%      6.50%
5 years and above up to 10 years    6.00%      6.50%

Note: senior citizens receive a flat additional premium built into the
rates above (0.50% across most tenures). Bulk deposits (Rs. 3 crore and
above) are priced separately and not covered here.
