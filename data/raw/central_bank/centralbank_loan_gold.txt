Central Bank of India Gold Loan (Cent Gold Loan)

Central Bank of India's gold loan interest rate is RBLR + 0.25% (8.50%
p.a., retail/MSME segment).

Eligibility:
Individuals aged 18 and above with KYC-compliant accounts. Gold/jewelry
shop owners and goldsmiths are not eligible.

Loan Amount:
Minimum Rs. 10,000, maximum Rs. 60 lakh (subject to an overall RAM
segment cap of Rs. 60 lakh).

Loan-to-Value (LTV) and Margin:
LTV ranges from 68% to 78% per borrower/CIF depending on profile and
loan size; margin ranges from 22% to 32% correspondingly.

Tenure:
12 months, bullet repayment structure.

Processing Fee:
Nil up to Rs. 5 lakh; above Rs. 5 lakh, 0.30% of loan amount + GST,
maximum Rs. 5,000 (offer valid through 31 March 2027).

Other Charges:
Appraisal fee 0.20%-0.22% of loan amount (+ GST) for first-time
appraisal, minimum Rs. 100-110, maximum Rs. 3,000-3,300. No prepayment
or foreclosure charges.
