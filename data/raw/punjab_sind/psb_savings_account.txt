Punjab & Sind Bank Savings Accounts

Punjab & Sind Bank offers several savings account variants, including
the General Savings Account, Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA),
and PSB Privilege Savings Account, with eligibility covering single/
joint accounts, accounts for persons with disabilities, and minor
accounts with restricted transactions.

This is a summary-level entry only — the bank's savings-account landing
and General Savings Account pages list the product variants but do not
publish an inline minimum-average-balance figure or interest rate; those
are referenced as living on the bank's separate "Interest Rate" and
"Service Charges" pages, which were not separately fetched in this pass.
Confirm the exact minimum balance and current rate with the bank before
relying on any figure.
