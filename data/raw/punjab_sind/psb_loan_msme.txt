Punjab & Sind Bank MSME / Business Loan Schemes

Punjab & Sind Bank offers multiple MSME loan schemes: PSB Vyapar Loan,
PSB Start Up Scheme, PSB Commercial Vehicle, PSB PMEGP Scheme, PM
Vishwakarma Scheme, PM Svanidhi Scheme, PSB Professional Assist, Stand
Up India Scheme, and PSB Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana.

This is a summary-level entry only — the bank's MSME landing page names
these schemes but does not publish inline interest rates, loan-amount
ranges, collateral requirements, or CGTMSE coverage details; those live
on each scheme's own sub-page, which was not separately fetched in this
pass. The bank also references the Jansamarth Portal
(jansamarth.in) for MSME loan applications. Confirm exact terms with the
bank before relying on any figure for these specific schemes.
