Punjab & Sind Bank PSB Udyan Scheme interest rate: 1-Year MCLR + 1.10%
for loans up to Rs. 2 lakh, and 1-Year MCLR + 2.10% above Rs. 2 lakh.
This is Punjab & Sind Bank's agricultural loan scheme for orchard and
plantation development. Margin: nil up to Rs. 2 lakh; 15% above
Rs. 2 lakh. Tenure: 15 years for establishment.
