Punjab & Sind Bank Digital Banking Products

Punjab & Sind Bank's UPI/mobile banking app is called PSB UNIC BHIM.
Users can create a unique Virtual Payment Address (VPA) using their
name, numbers, or characters (e.g. choice@psb or choice@psbpay).

Key Features:
- Peer-to-peer money transfers via VPA or QR code scanning.
- Collect-and-pay through Virtual Payment Address.
- Merchant payments without revealing account details.
- Balance checking across linked accounts.
- UPI Lite for transactions under Rs. 1,000, no PIN required.
- Recurring payments via UPI Autopay.
- Two-factor authentication and device binding for security.
- Supports both UPI and Bharat QR codes.

All transfers are instant, available 24x7 365 days a year, and free of
cost. The bank's broader digital banking platform is called PSB UnIC.
