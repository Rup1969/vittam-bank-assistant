Punjab & Sind Bank — Kisan Credit Card (KCC) and Agriculture Loan Schemes

The PSB Udyan Scheme interest rate at Punjab & Sind Bank (orchard and
plantation development loan) is 1-Year MCLR + 1.10% for loans up to
Rs. 2 lakh, and 1-Year MCLR + 2.10% above Rs. 2 lakh.

Punjab & Sind Bank also offers a Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme, like
every scheduled bank in India — the nationwide revolving credit
facility for farmers' cultivation and allied agricultural needs.
However, Punjab & Sind Bank's own agriculture financing page does not
publish a dedicated KCC section with its own rate/eligibility detail —
KCC is referenced only as an alternative working-capital facility
under several other schemes, not documented separately. This is a
genuine content gap on the bank's own site, not a fetch failure.

Real agriculture schemes with published terms, found on this page:

PSB Krishak Rath (farm vehicle financing):
- Loan amount: two-wheelers up to Rs. 2 lakh; four-wheelers up to
  Rs. 50 lakh.
- Interest rate: new two-wheeler — 1-Year MCLR + 3.00%; new four-wheeler
  — 1-Year MCLR + 0.50%.
- Margin: 15% of on-road price (inclusive of one-time road tax,
  registration, and insurance).
- Tenure: 3 years (two-wheelers); 7 years (four-wheelers).

PSB Udyan Scheme (orchard and plantation development):
- Interest rate: up to Rs. 2 lakh — 1-Year MCLR + 1.10%; above Rs. 2
  lakh — 1-Year MCLR + 2.10%.
- Margin: nil up to Rs. 2 lakh; 15% above Rs. 2 lakh.
- Tenure: 15 years for establishment.

PSB Dairy Development Scheme:
- Interest rate: 1-Year MCLR + 1% (up to Rs. 5 lakh).
- Maximum loan: Rs. 50 lakh.
- Margin: nil up to Rs. 2 lakh; 15% thereafter.

For Kisan Credit Card (KCC) details specifically, contact the bank
directly (toll-free 1800-419-8300) or check jansamarth.in.
