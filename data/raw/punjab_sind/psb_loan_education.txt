Punjab & Sind Bank education loan interest rate ranges approximately
9.40% to 10.15% p.a. depending on the loan amount slab, across its
PSB Vidyalaxmi Scheme, PSB Education Loan, and PSB Skill Education
Loan products.

Rate slabs (PSB Model Education Loan Scheme), per the bank's own
published rate card: up to Rs. 4 lakh at 10.15%; above Rs. 4 lakh up to
Rs. 7.50 lakh at 9.45%; above Rs. 7.50 lakh at 9.40% (the lowest
published rate, for larger loans). Confirm eligibility, maximum loan
amount, margin, and collateral requirements for a specific scheme with
the bank before relying on any figure.
