Punjab & Sind Bank Personal Loan — Eligibility, Fees, and Other Requirements

Eligibility:
- Salaried employees with at least 2 years of service and gross monthly
  salary above Rs. 30,000.
- Government/PSU employees, and private-sector employees of reputed
  companies.
- Pensioners, up to maximum age 70.
- Must hold a salary account with Punjab & Sind Bank or another bank.
- Minimum entry age 21 years; maximum age at loan end is the retirement
  date for salaried employees, or 70 years for pensioners.

Fees:
- Processing fee: 0.50% + GST (government/PSU employees and pensioners);
  1.00% + GST (private/MNC employees).
- Documentation charges: actual stamp/revenue expenses only.
- Inspection and prepayment charges: nil.

Other Requirements:
Guarantee required from spouse, major children, or a third party. ECS
mandate is mandatory if the salary account is held at another bank.
