The Punjab & Sind Bank personal loan interest rate is 11.25% to
14.00% p.a., depending on employment category (11.25%-13.05% for
government/PSU/pensioners, 12.15%-14.00% for private/MNC employees).
Loan amount: Rs. 5 lakh to Rs. 20 lakh. Maximum tenure 84 months.
