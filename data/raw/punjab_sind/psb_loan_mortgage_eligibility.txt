Punjab & Sind Bank Mortgage Loan (Loan Against Property) — Eligibility, LTV, Tenure, and Purpose

Loan Amount:
Minimum Rs. 0.50 lakh, maximum Rs. 10 crore.

Eligibility:
Minimum gross monthly income Rs. 30,000; age 18-60 (salaried) or up to
65 (others with pension income); must be an income-tax assessee. Open
to salaried employees, self-employed professionals, business owners,
NRIs, and entities (HUFs, partnerships).

Loan-to-Value (LTV):
Finance available up to 65% of the realizable value of the mortgaged
property (effective from 10/02/2022). Alternative securities accepted:
bank deposits (10% of balance), LIC policies (70% of surrender value),
National Savings Certificates (25% of face value).

Tenure:
Maximum 15 years, based on repayment capacity, with the loan end date
not exceeding the borrower's retirement age. Loans with a tenor above 10
years carry an interest rate 1% above the applicable rate.

Purpose:
General-purpose credit — marriage, medical, education, property
repairs/renovation, consumer durables, or other unforeseen expenses.
