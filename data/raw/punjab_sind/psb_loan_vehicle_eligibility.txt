Punjab & Sind Bank Vehicle Loan (PSB Apna Vahan) — Eligibility, Amount, Tenure, Margin, and Fees

Eligibility:
Minimum age 18 years; maximum age adjusted before 60 (non-pension
service) or 70 (pension/others). Individuals, proprietorships, and
business concerns are eligible; income can be combined with a spouse or
children if they become co-borrowers.

Loan Amount:
- New vehicles: no maximum ceiling stated.
- Old (used) vehicles: up to Rs. 75 lakh.

Tenure:
- New four-wheelers: maximum 84 months.
- New two-wheelers: maximum 36 months.
- Old vehicles: maximum 60 months (total repayment period plus vehicle
  age cannot exceed 84 months).

Margin (Down Payment):
- New vehicles, general: 10%.
- Government/PSU employees: 5%.
- Government/PSU employees with a salary account at Punjab & Sind Bank:
  0%.

Processing Charges:
- Four-wheeler: 0.25% of loan amount, minimum Rs. 1,000, maximum
  Rs. 15,000.
- Two-wheeler: 0.25%, minimum Rs. 1,000 (waived for bank staff and
  pensioners).
