Punjab & Sind Bank — Other Deposit Schemes

Beyond its core FD/term deposit table (see psb_fd_rates.txt, which
already includes the bank's PSB Green Earth-style Callable/Non-Callable
term-deposit variants), Punjab & Sind Bank also facilitates government
small-savings schemes through its branches: Public Provident Fund (PPF),
Senior Citizen Savings Scheme, Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme, and Sukanya
Samriddhi Scheme.

This is a summary-level entry only — the bank's deposit navigation page
lists these products/schemes but does not publish inline figures
(minimum amount, tenure, current rate) for them; those live on the
scheme's own government-mandated terms or a separate bank sub-page, not
separately fetched in this pass.
