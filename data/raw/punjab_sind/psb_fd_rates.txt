Punjab & Sind Bank Fixed Deposit (FD) Interest Rates

Punjab & Sind Bank's current fixed deposit (FD) interest rate for the
general public ranges from 2.85% to 6.90% p.a. depending on tenure. The
1-year FD interest rate is 5.85% for the general public and 6.35% for
senior citizens.

crore, but only for tenures of 180 days and above.

Note: for the 375/444/666/999-day tenures, the bank publishes BOTH a
Callable and a Non-Callable rate for the same tenure — the Non-Callable
variant pays a higher rate (typically +0.10%) in exchange for the
depositor giving up the right to premature withdrawal. Both are real,
currently published rates.
