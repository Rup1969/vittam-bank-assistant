Punjab & Sind Bank Gold Loan (PSB Gold Loan)

Loan Amount:
Minimum Rs. 25,000, maximum Rs. 50 lakh per borrower (including any
top-up loans).

Eligibility:
Fully KYC-compliant customers, including staff and ex-staff. NRI
customers may borrow only jointly with an Indian resident.

Collateral Limits:
- Gold jewelry: maximum 1 kg per borrower.
- Silver jewelry: maximum 10 kg per borrower.
- Gold coins: maximum 50 grams.
- Silver coins: maximum 500 grams.

Valuation:
The bank values gold/silver collateral at the LOWER of (a) the average
closing price over the preceding 30 days, or (b) the closing price on
the preceding day, both sourced via IBJA or a SEBI-regulated exchange.

Loan Types Offered:
Demand Loan (bullet repayment), Overdraft (regular interest intervals),
and Term Loan (equal monthly installments).

Interest Rate, LTV/Margin, Processing Fee:
Not stated as specific figures on this product page — refer to the
bank's service-charges and lending-rate pages for current terms.
