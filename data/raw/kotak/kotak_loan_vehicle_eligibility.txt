Kotak Mahindra Bank Car Loan / Vehicle Loan / Auto Loan — Features and Underwriting

Kotak Mahindra Bank's car loan (also called vehicle loan or auto loan)
finances a wide range of car models — passenger cars, sedans,
hatchbacks, SUVs, and multi-utility vehicles.

New Car Loan Features:
- Up to 100% funding of the car's on-road price.
- Tailor-made car financing schemes based on individual requirement.
- Speedy car loan processing, easy documentation.
- Flexible car loan repayment tenure.

Used Car Loan: also available, for financing a pre-owned vehicle
purchase, with its own eligibility and rate structure.

Car loan availability: only at select locations.

Underwriting: Kotak Mahindra Bank's car loan / vehicle loan is
underwritten through Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited (KMPL), a subsidiary
of Kotak Mahindra Bank — applying for a car loan on the bank's website
redirects to the KMPL platform. Car loan disbursement is subject to
complete documentation and credit approval at KMPL's sole discretion,
and subject to RBI guidelines in force at the time.

This is a headline reference only — confirm the current car loan
interest rate and terms with KMPL before quoting to a customer.
