Kotak Mahindra Bank Fixed Deposit Interest Rates (below Rs. 3 crore)

Kotak Mahindra Bank's current fixed deposit (FD) interest rate for the
general public ranges from 2.75% to 6.80% per annum across tenures
from 7 days to 10 years, with senior citizens earning 0.25% to 0.50%
more depending on tenure. The 1-year (365 days to less than 15 months)
FD interest rate is 6.35% for the general public and 6.85% for senior
citizens; Kotak's highest published slab is 6.80% general / 7.30%
senior, for the 2-years-to-less-than-3-years band.

Effective 10 June 2026, domestic retail term deposit rates by tenure
(general public / senior citizen):
- 7-14 days: 2.75% / 3.25%
- 15-30 days: 2.75% / 3.25%
- 31-45 days: 3.00% / 3.50%
- 46-90 days: 3.50% / 4.00%
- 91 days: 4.00% / 4.50%
- 92-179 days: 4.25% / 4.75%
- 180 days: 5.00% / 5.50%
- 181-269 days: 5.50% / 6.00%
- 270 days: 5.50% / 6.00%
- 271-363 days: 6.00% / 6.50%
- 364 days: 6.00% / 6.50%
- 365 days to less than 15 months: 6.35% / 6.85%
- 15 months to less than 18 months: 6.45% / 6.95%
- 18 months to less than 2 years: 6.55% / 7.05%
- 2 years to less than 3 years: 6.80% / 7.30%
- 3 years to less than 4 years: 6.40% / 6.90%
- 4 years to less than 5 years: 6.40% / 6.90%
- 5 years to 10 years (inclusive): 6.25% / 6.75%

Senior Citizen Premium: reported as an additional 0.50% for most
tenures (does not apply to NRO/NRE deposits, and not offered on
ActivMoney sweep deposits or Non-withdrawable FDs).

Minimum Deposit: Rs. 5,000, no maximum limit.

Note: Kotak's rate page also separately lists rate tables for the Rs.
3 crore-to-Rs. 5 crore band, several higher bulk-deposit tiers (up to
Rs. 300 crore+), and a separate Non-Withdrawable (premature-withdrawal-
not-allowed) FD variant with its own, generally higher, rate table —
this document covers only the standard, premature-withdrawal-allowed,
retail (below Rs. 3 crore) tier.

Rates are revised periodically; verify the current card rate with the
bank before quoting for a large deposit.
