Kotak Mahindra Bank — Digital Banking Products

Kotak811 App:
Kotak Mahindra's flagship mobile banking app — enables instant,
end-to-end digital account opening (zero-balance savings account,
minutes to open, no branch visit needed). Offers 250+ features
including balance checks, transaction history, statement downloads,
bill payments, shopping, investments, credit card management, personal
loan applications, and fixed deposit account opening, all from within
one app. A "Pay Your Contact" feature lets users send money using just
a mobile number.

UPI (via Kotak811):
Registration for Kotak811 UPI enables instant money transfers and bill
payments. Secured via advanced encryption and biometric authentication.

Confirm current app features and UPI limits with the bank, since these
are periodically revised by NPCI/RBI guidance as well as bank policy.
