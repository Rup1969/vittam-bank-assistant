Kotak Mahindra Bank Home Loan

Kotak Mahindra Bank's home loan interest rate currently starts from
around 7.60% to 7.70% per annum for well-qualified applicants, with a
broader reported range of 7.99%-9.45% p.a. depending on applicant
profile.

Rate by Profile:
- Salaried employees: reported range 8.65%-9.00% p.a.
- Self-employed: reported range 8.75%-9.45% p.a.

Rate depends on CIBIL score, employment type, and the down payment
amount provided — self-employed applicants are consistently priced
somewhat higher than salaried applicants, reflecting typically less
predictable income verification.

This is a headline reference — confirm the current rate and the
applicant's specific quoted spread with the bank before quoting.
