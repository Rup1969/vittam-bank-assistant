Kotak Mahindra Bank — Deposit Scheme Variants

ActivMoney (2-Way Sweep Deposit):
A sweep-in/sweep-out facility linking a savings/current account to a
fixed deposit — balances above a set threshold automatically move into
an FD to earn a higher rate, while remaining accessible for
withdrawal. Note: ActivMoney FDs are priced at the Regular (non-senior)
FD rate for ALL customers, including senior citizens — the usual senior
citizen premium does not apply to this specific product.

Non-Withdrawable Fixed Deposit:
A premature-withdrawal-not-allowed FD variant offering a rate premium
over the standard callable table, available from Rs. 3 crore and
above. No sweep-in facility, no auto-renewal, and no monthly interest
payout option on this variant.

Kotak811 Zero Balance Savings Account:
A fully digital, instantly-opened zero-balance savings account
(opened via the Kotak811 app), positioned as the bank's flagship
digital-first account for new customers.

Kotak Gold Loan Smart Choice Scheme:
See the loan document for details — while primarily a loan product,
it's a notable no-income-documentation offering worth knowing alongside
deposit products for a customer comparing Kotak's overall product
range.

This is a summary of publicly listed Kotak Mahindra Bank scheme names —
exact current rates, minimum amounts, and eligibility should be
confirmed with the bank before advising a customer.
