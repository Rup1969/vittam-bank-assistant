Kotak Mahindra Bank MSME / Business Loans

Collateral-Free Business Loan:
Kotak Mahindra Bank offers business loans up to Rs. 1 crore without
collateral, for general business/working-capital needs.

CGTMSE Collateral-Free MSME Loans:
Under the government's CGTMSE scheme, Kotak offers collateral-free
MSME loans up to Rs. 2 crore (and potentially higher in some cases),
with final approval based on the bank's assessment of financials,
repayment ability, and documentation — a notably higher collateral-
free ceiling than several public sector banks' typical Rs. 10 lakh
threshold.

Agri-SME Business Loans:
A specific collateral-free scheme for commodity traders, integrated
with the government's PMMY (Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana) scheme.

Overdraft (OD) / Cash Credit (CC):
Working-capital facilities allowing MSMEs to draw funds up to a
sanctioned limit, paying interest only on the amount actually used.

This is a headline reference — confirm current interest rates,
processing fees, and eligibility criteria (including which scheme
applies to a specific business) with the bank before advising a
customer.
