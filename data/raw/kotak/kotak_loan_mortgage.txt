Kotak Mahindra Bank Loan Against Property (LAP) / Mortgage Loan

Kotak Mahindra Bank's Loan Against Property offers loan values from
Rs. 10 lakh to Rs. 5 crore against commercial, residential, or
industrial property, for both personal and business needs.

Interest Rate Structure — Hybrid Interest Rate:
Offers a fixed interest rate for an initial tenure of 39, 52, or 65
months, followed by a floating rate linked to the prevailing Repo Rate
plus an applicable interest spread thereafter (the exact spread is
communicated at onboarding, not published as a fixed headline figure).

Key Benefits:
- Predictable EMIs during the initial fixed-rate tenure.
- Protection from interest rate volatility during that period.
- Flexibility to benefit from future market-linked rate movements
  once the floating period begins.

Loan Structure Options:
- EMI-based loans, or
- Overdraft facility.

Fees: A non-refundable processing fee of up to 1% is charged.

Eligible Property Types: Commercial, residential, or industrial
property.

This is a headline reference — the exact applicable interest spread is
disclosed only at onboarding, not published generally. Confirm the
current rate and spread with the bank before quoting to a customer.
