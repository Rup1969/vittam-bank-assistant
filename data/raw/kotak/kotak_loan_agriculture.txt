Kotak Mahindra Bank Kisan Credit Card

Purpose:
A revolving credit facility for farmers' cultivation and allied
agricultural credit needs, under the nationwide Kisan Credit Card
scheme — aimed at providing hassle-free, timely credit support through
a single-window facility for cultivation and other farming-related
needs. Kotak calls this product "Crop Loan," with the Kisan Credit
Card as its working-capital component and a separate Agri Term Loan
for medium-term needs (land development, irrigation, farm assets).

Eligibility (from Kotak's own eligibility page):
All Farmers — Individuals or Joint borrowers who are owner
cultivators. Minimum age 21, maximum age 65 at loan tenure end; if the
primary borrower is over 65, a co-borrower within the normal age
range must be included.

Credit Limit:
The working-capital (KCC) limit is fixed based on the Scale of
Finance (SOF) for the crops grown on the borrower's land, with an
annual review; the limit is renewed after 5 years. The separate Agri
Term Loan component runs for a maximum fixed term of 5 years.

Interest Rate: Prevailing MCLR/reference rate at the low end, up to a
stated maximum of 24% p.a. depending on the borrower's profile.

Charges (from Kotak's own fees-and-charges page): processing fee up
to 2% of the sanctioned limit; documentation charge Rs. 5,000; credit
administration charge 1% of the sanctioned limit; supervision/crop
verification Rs. 750-3,000 every 6 months depending on limit size;
penal charge 8% p.a. on overdue amounts.

Validity: Kisan Credit Card working-capital limit is renewed after 5
years, with an annual review in between.

Honest gap: Kotak's own pages state processing fees and other charges
in detail but do not publish a specific Margin percentage or a
rupee-threshold collateral/security requirement for the Crop
Loan/KCC — unlike PNB, BOI, Indian Bank, or Bank of Maharashtra, whose
own pages do state these. Not inferred or borrowed from another
bank's rules; confirm the current margin and security requirement
directly with Kotak or a nearest branch.
