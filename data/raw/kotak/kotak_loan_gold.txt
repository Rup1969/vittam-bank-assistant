Kotak Mahindra Bank Gold Loan

Interest Rate:
Standard gold loan: starting from 9% p.a. onwards, for loan amounts up
to Rs. 1.5 crore and tenure up to 4 years.

Kotak Gold Loan Smart Choice Scheme:
A special no-income-documentation gold loan scheme at a fixed rate of
0.88% per month (approximately 10.56% p.a. equivalent), for loan
amounts up to Rs. 6 lakh and a 12-month tenure — a distinct, higher-
rate product from the standard gold loan, trading a higher rate for
reduced documentation requirements.

Structure:
Loan secured against pledged gold ornaments/jewellery; exact rate and
eligible amount depend on gold purity, weight, and prevailing gold
valuation at the time of the loan.

This is a headline reference — always confirm the current rate,
loan-to-value ratio, and which specific scheme applies with the bank
before quoting, since gold loan terms are sensitive to gold price
movements and are reviewed frequently.
