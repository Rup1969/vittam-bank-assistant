Kotak Mahindra Bank Education Loan

Interest Rate:
Not published by Kotak Mahindra Bank directly — the education loan is
administered by partner NBFC Credila Financial Services under its own
credit and underwriting norms, so the applicable rate is set by
Credila, not disclosed as a fixed Kotak Mahindra Bank rate.

Kotak Mahindra Bank's education loan is an unsecured loan of up to
Rs. 75 lakh for higher studies, offered through its "Education Loans
from Credila" program — a tie-up with Credila Financial Services, a
dedicated education-loan NBFC, rather than a loan Kotak underwrites
directly in-house. As of March 2024, Credila had funded 178,000+
Indian students across 63 countries and 4,600+ institutes under this
partnership.

Loan Amount: Unsecured loan up to Rs. 75 lakh.

Collateral Options:
- Moveable/liquid collateral, such as a fixed deposit.
- Immovable collateral, such as a flat, house, bungalow, non-agricultural
  land, or shop.

Special Benefits:
- Tax benefits under Section 80E of the Income Tax Act.
- Seamless loan sanctions.
- NIL TCS (Tax Collected at Source) on foreign remittance.
- Up to 100% financing, covering tuition fee and ancillary expenses.

Eligibility: Applicant and co-applicant(s) must meet Credila's own
credit and underwriting norms, applicable from time to time. Sanctioning
is at Credila's sole discretion, not Kotak Mahindra Bank's.

Note: because this is a third-party-administered partnership product
rather than a Kotak-underwritten loan, exact interest rates, tenure, and
processing fees are set by Credila and should be confirmed on Credila's
own terms before quoting a rate to a customer.
