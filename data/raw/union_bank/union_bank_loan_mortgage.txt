Union Bank of India — Union Mortgage (Loan Against Property)

Products:
- Union Mortgage — the standard loan-against-property product.
- Union Mortgage Plus — a variant reported to carry an interest rate
  0.75% higher than the equivalent home loan rate, presumably for a
  differently-structured eligibility or higher loan-to-value than the
  base Union Mortgage product.

Interest Rate:
Reported figures vary widely across sources — from as low as 9.45% p.a.
onwards to a 12.05%-12.90% p.a. range depending on property type and
CIBIL score. Given this spread, always confirm the applicant's actual
quoted rate with the bank rather than relying on a single headline
figure.

Loan Amount & Tenure:
Up to Rs. 10 crore, with tenure up to 15 years. A CIBIL score of 750+
is generally expected for the most preferential pricing; lower scores
may lead to a higher rate or rejection.

This is a headline reference with unusually wide source disagreement on
the exact rate — confirm directly with the bank before quoting.
