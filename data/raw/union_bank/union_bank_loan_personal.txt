Union Bank of India Personal Loan

Interest Rate:
Reported starting from around 8.85% p.a. for the most-qualified
applicants, with a broader reported range of 8.85%-14.45% p.a.
depending on employment profile and credit score. A separate reported
figure of 10.75% p.a. starting rate applies to loans up to Rs. 15 lakh
with a maximum tenure of 5 years — confirm which pricing tier applies
to a specific applicant with the bank.

Loan Amount & Tenure:
Up to Rs. 15 lakh (for the tenor-capped scheme noted above); broader
personal loan amounts may be available depending on income and
eligibility. Maximum tenure reported at 5 years for that scheme.

This is a headline reference — personal loan pricing is highly
profile-dependent; always confirm the applicant's actual quoted rate
and eligibility with the bank.
