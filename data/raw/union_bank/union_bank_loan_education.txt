Union Bank of India — Union Education Loan (PM-Vidyalaxmi)

Interest Rate:
Union Education loans under the government's PM-Vidyalaxmi scheme are
reported with effective interest rates ranging from 7.10% to 8.50% p.a.,
depending on the category/ranking of the institute the student is
admitted to (higher-ranked/NIRF-listed institutions typically qualify
for the lower end of this range).

Scheme Context:
PM-Vidyalaxmi is a central government education-loan scheme integrating
interest subvention for eligible lower-income families — Union Bank
participates in this scheme alongside its own standard education loan
product line, so the applicable rate and subsidy depend on both the
institute category and the applicant's eligibility for government
interest subvention.

This is a headline reference — confirm the current rate for a specific
institute/course category and the applicant's PM-Vidyalaxmi eligibility
with the bank before quoting.
