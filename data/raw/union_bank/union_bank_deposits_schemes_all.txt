Union Bank of India — Deposit Scheme Variants

Monthly Income Scheme (MIS):
A term deposit designed to pay out guaranteed monthly income rather
than compounding/maturity payout — reported interest rates roughly
3.00% to 5.60% p.a. depending on tenure. Useful for customers who need
a regular income stream (e.g. retirees) rather than a lump sum at
maturity.

Savings Flexi Deposit Scheme (SBNFD):
A sweep-in facility linked to a savings account — balances above a
threshold automatically move into a linked term deposit to earn a
higher FD rate, while remaining accessible (swept back) for withdrawal
needs. Positioned as "higher returns" without fully locking away the
funds like a standalone FD.

Union Non-Callable Term Deposit Scheme:
For deposits of Rs. 3 crore and above — offers a rate premium in
exchange for giving up premature-withdrawal rights (see
union_bank_fd_rates for the retail below-Rs.-3-crore rate table this
scheme sits alongside).

FCNR(B) / RFC / Unique FCNR(B) Deposit Scheme:
NRI-focused foreign-currency term deposit products — rates and terms
differ from the domestic-rupee table and are published separately by
the bank.

This is a summary of publicly listed Union Bank deposit scheme names —
exact current rates, minimum amounts, and eligibility should be
confirmed with the bank before advising a customer.
