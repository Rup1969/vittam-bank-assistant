Union Bank of India Gold Loan

Interest Rate:
Reported starting from 8.80% p.a., with a broader reported range up to
11.00% p.a. depending on loan amount, tenure, and the pledged gold's
valuation.

Loan-to-Value (LTV):
Up to 75% of the appraised gold value — a slightly more conservative
LTV cap than some peer banks' 80-90% gold-loan offerings.

Structure:
Loan secured against pledged gold ornaments/jewellery; exact rate and
eligible amount depend on gold purity, weight, and prevailing gold
valuation at the time of the loan.

This is a headline reference — always confirm the current rate,
loan-to-value ratio, and valuation method with the bank, since gold
loan terms are sensitive to gold price movements and are reviewed
frequently.
