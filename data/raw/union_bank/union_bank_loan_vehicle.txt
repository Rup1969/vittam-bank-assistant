Union Bank of India car loan / vehicle loan interest rate (Union Miles
scheme): new 4-wheelers 8.85% to 10.50% p.a. depending on CIC score.
Electric cars (Union Green Miles): 8.80% to 10.40% p.a. Used
4-wheelers: 12.9% to 13% p.a.

Loan Amount:
Up to 85% of the vehicle's value for new cars; up to 60% of the
vehicle's value for used cars.

Commercial Vehicles:
A separate Commercial Vehicle Loan product exists with its own rate
card, distinct from the personal-use Union Miles/Union Green Miles
products above.

This is a headline reference — confirm the current CIC-score-linked
rate and processing terms with the bank before quoting.
