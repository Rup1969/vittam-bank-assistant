Union Bank of India — Digital Banking Products

Vyom (rebranded "Union ease"):
Union Bank's flagship mobile banking app (Android/iOS) — supports
NEFT/IMPS/RTGS/UPI fund transfers, links savings, current, overdraft,
and NRE/NRO accounts to a single UPI ID, bill payments with autopay and
reminders, mutual fund investments, loan applications, and lifestyle
bookings (flights, hotels, cabs) from within the app. The app was
renamed from "Vyom" to "Union ease" as part of a redesign — both names
may appear in search results/app stores during the transition.

UPI:
Supported within the app via BHIM UPI — instant transfers using a
Virtual Payment Address (VPA), QR code, or account number+IFSC, without
sharing bank account details directly. Requires UPI PIN authentication
for every transaction.

Security:
Device binding is used — the app is not designed to run on multiple
devices simultaneously, an anti-fraud measure alongside standard
transaction encryption and UPI PIN authentication.

Fees:
The bank does not charge a fee for using the app itself, though mobile
carrier data/SMS charges may apply separately.
