Union Bank of India Savings Account — Minimum Balance Requirements

Union Bank of India's savings account minimum balance ranges from zero
(Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account — Small Accounts) to Rs. 1,000
(Regular Savings Bank Account, metro/urban), tracked as an average
quarterly balance.

Regular Savings Bank Account (SBGEN) — confirmed directly from Union
Bank's official page FAQ: "For metro and urban branches, you must
maintain an average quarterly balance of Rs. 1000, for semi-urban
branches of Rs. 500, and rural branches of Rs. 250 in your savings bank
account."

Regular SBGEN account also includes: nil debit card issuance charges,
free personal accidental insurance (death) cover of Rs. 2 lakh with the
debit card, 20 free personalized cheque leaves per year, 5 free ATM
transactions per month (3 free at metro centres), nil account closure
charges within 14 days or after 12 months.

Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account — Small Accounts (BSBDS): "Enjoy all
basic banking services and facilities without any minimum balance
requirement and simple paperwork." Opened under the PMJDY scheme;
requires only a self-attested photo and declaration. Comes with a RuPay
debit card at no issuance/annual maintenance charge, plus accidental
insurance cover.

Union Digital Savings Account (UDSA): per Union Bank's own product
listing, opening this account requires no minimum balance.

Premium tier — SB High Networth Customers (SBHNI): minimum Quarterly
Average Balance (QAB) of Rs. 1,00,000, for customers seeking a
relationship-manager-backed premium account.

Verify current minimum balance requirements against Union Bank of
India's official website before quoting to a customer as final, since
these are periodically revised.
