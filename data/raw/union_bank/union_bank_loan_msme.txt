Union Bank of India MSME Loans

Collateral-Free Lending:
Union Bank offers collateral-free credit assistance up to Rs. 10 lakh
for MSME borrowers, making initial working-capital/business credit
quicker to access without pledging security — in line with the
CGTMSE-backed collateral-free lending norms most public sector banks,
including Union Bank, follow for smaller MSME loan amounts.

Policy Context:
The bank maintains a dedicated MSME Credit policy (periodically
revised, e.g. "MSME Policy 2022-23") covering eligibility, sector
focus, and pricing norms for micro, small, and medium enterprise
lending — specific product-level interest rates were not found in
public secondary sources during this research pass and should be
confirmed directly with the bank.

This is a partial reference — confirm current MSME product names,
interest rates, and collateral-free thresholds above Rs. 10 lakh with
the bank before advising a customer.
