Union Bank of India — Kisan Credit Card (Union Green Card)

Purpose:
A revolving credit facility for farmers, branded by Union Bank as the
"Union Green Card," covering short-term and long-term credit needs for
cultivation, post-harvest expenses, produce marketing loans,
consumption requirements of farmer households, and working capital for
farm-asset maintenance and allied agricultural activities.

Security Requirements:
- Limits up to Rs. 1.60 lakh: hypothecation of crops only (no
  additional collateral).
- Limits up to Rs. 3 lakh under a tie-up arrangement: also just crop
  hypothecation.
- Limits above these thresholds: mortgage of land and/or third-party
  guarantee required, in addition to hypothecated crops/assets.

Interest Rate:
Not confirmed from public secondary sources for the current period —
Union Bank participates in the government's Interest Subvention Scheme
for short-term crop loans (like other public sector banks, typically
around 7% p.a. effective rate for qualifying loans up to Rs. 3 lakh);
confirm the current effective rate with the bank.

This is a headline reference — confirm the current effective rate and
exact collateral thresholds with the bank or nearest branch.
