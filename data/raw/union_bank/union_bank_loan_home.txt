Union Bank of India Home Loan

Union Bank of India's home loan interest rate currently ranges from
approximately 7.15% to 9.35% per annum for well-qualified applicants,
linked to the bank's External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR). This is
the standard housing loan for purchasing or constructing a home — not
to be confused with Union Mortgage, the bank's separate loan-against-
property product for borrowing against an already-owned property.

Interest Rate:
Linked to the bank's External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR) — EBLR was
8.00% as of 24 July 2026 (RBI Repo Rate 5.25% + a spread of 2.75%).
Reported home loan rates range roughly 7.15%-9.35% p.a. for well-
qualified salaried/non-salaried applicants, with a broader reported
range up to 12.65% p.a. depending on credit score, loan amount, and
property location — third-party aggregator figures vary noticeably, so
confirm the applicant's actual quoted rate with the bank.

Structure:
Floating-rate loan — moves with EBLR/repo-rate changes over the loan
tenure. Rate depends on CIBIL score, employment category (salaried vs.
self-employed), loan amount, and tenure.

This is a headline, EBLR-linked reference — confirm the current EBLR
value and the applicant's specific quoted spread with the bank before
quoting.
