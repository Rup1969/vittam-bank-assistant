Bank of India Gold Loan

Interest Rate:
Reported range roughly 8.35%-9.05% p.a., with minimal processing fees
and flexible repayment options reported across aggregator sources.

Structure:
Loan secured against pledged gold ornaments/jewellery; the exact rate
and eligible amount depend on loan amount, tenure selected, and the
purity/weight of gold pledged, in line with standard gold-loan practice
across public sector banks.

This is a headline reference — always confirm the current rate,
loan-to-value ratio, and valuation method with the bank, since gold
loan terms are sensitive to gold price movements and are reviewed
frequently.
