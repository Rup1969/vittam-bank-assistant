Bank of India — Savings Account Variants

BOI Saral Salary Account:
A zero-minimum-balance salary account with free internet banking, mobile
banking, monthly e-statements, and unlimited transactions at BOI ATMs.

BOI Private Salary Account:
A premium salary account bundling up to Rs. 2 crore air accident cover,
zero balance requirement, and loan-processing concessions.

Star Salary Plus Account (Public Sector):
A salary account variant for public sector employees, nil minimum
balance.

Star Salary Account (Private Sector):
The private-sector-employee equivalent of the above, also nil minimum
balance.

Star Salary Plus Rakshak Salary:
A salary account specifically for police personnel.

BOI Elite Salary Account:
A higher-tier salary account variant with additional privileges over the
standard salary accounts.

Star General Current Account:
A current account variant requiring a minimum cash balance, for
non-salaried/business customers.

Star Sunidhi Tax-Saving Deposit Scheme:
A 5-to-10-year fixed deposit qualifying for an income tax deduction on
the principal (up to Rs. 1,50,000 annually) under Section 80C. Interest
rate reported up to 6.00% p.a. general public / 6.75% p.a. senior
citizens. Carries a mandatory 5-year lock-in — premature withdrawal and
loan/overdraft-against-FD facilities are not permitted during this
period, a real trade-off customers should understand before choosing it
over a regular tax-saver FD.

This is a summary of publicly reported BOI account variants — exact
eligibility, minimum balance, and current features should be confirmed
with the bank before advising a customer, since account names/features
are periodically revised.
