Bank of India Star Vehicle Loan interest rate is reported around 7.60%
to 8.85% p.a., linked to MCLR plus spread. Finances new and used
four-wheeler vehicles for personal use.

Scheme:
BOI Star Vehicle Loan Scheme finances new and used four-wheeler
vehicles for personal use (does not require a heavy-duty license);
available to resident and non-resident individuals as well as
non-individual entities. A separate "Star Vehicle Loan — Entities Other
Than Individuals" variant exists for business/firm borrowers.

Loan Amount & Tenure:
Up to Rs. 50 lakh for cars made in India. Repayment tenure up to 7 years
for individuals (5 years for businesses/firms).

Commercial Vehicles:
A separate Star Vehicle Express Loan product exists for commercial
vehicle financing, distinct from the personal-use car loan above.

This is a headline reference — confirm the current MCLR/rate spread and
processing-fee terms with the bank before quoting.
