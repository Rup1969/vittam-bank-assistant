Bank of India home loan interest rate (Star Home Loan) starts from
around 8.30% p.a. for well-qualified applicants, linked to the Repo
Based Lending Rate (RBLR) at 8.10%. Women, senior citizens, and NRIs
are reported in a 9.35% to 10.30% p.a. band.
