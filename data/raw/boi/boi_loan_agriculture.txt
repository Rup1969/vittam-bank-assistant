Bank of India — Kisan Credit Card (Agriculture Loan): Sanction Rules

Bank of India's Kisan Credit Card (KCC) sanction rules cover
eligibility, credit limit, margin, security, and validity, as
detailed below.

Purpose:
A revolving credit facility for farmers covering both cultivation
(crop-production) needs and non-farming allied activities, offered
cost-effectively for timely credit support.

Interest Rate:
Short-term crop loans / KCC up to Rs. 3 lakh carry a government-mandated
rate of 7% p.a. under the Interest Subvention Scheme, with an
additional 3% p.a. Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI) available to
farmers who repay on time — meaning the effective rate for a
prompt-repaying farmer can be significantly below the headline 7%.

Coverage:
Short-term credit for consumption/domestic needs is available up to 25%
of the farmer's gross estimated income, in addition to the core
crop-production credit component.

Eligibility:
All farmers — individual or joint borrowers — who are owner
cultivators, plus tenant farmers, oral lessees, and share croppers,
within the sanctioning branch's service area.

Credit Limit: short-term production-loan quantum is based on cultivated
area, crop type, and the applicable scale of finance; domestic/
consumption needs are covered up to 25% of gross estimated income or
Rs. 50,000, whichever applies; marketing/storage needs up to 50% of
produce cost. Maximum limit reported at Rs. 10 lakh per farmer for a
12-month tenure.

Margin: Nil — built into the scale of finance.

Security:
Up to Rs. 50,000 — hypothecation of the crops grown. Above Rs. 50,000
— hypothecation of standing crops plus mortgage of land (may be
waived for government warehouse-receipt financing or in specific
cases per RBI norms); banks may additionally seek collateral security
for limits above Rs. 1.60 lakh (without a tie-up arrangement) or
above Rs. 3 lakh (with a tie-up arrangement).

Validity: Card validity is up to 5 years. Each individual withdrawal's
repayment tenure is normally 12 months from the sanction date (18
months for banana and sugarcane crops specifically).
