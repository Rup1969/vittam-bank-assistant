Bank of India Fixed Deposit Interest Rates (below Rs. 3 crore)

Bank of India's current fixed deposit (FD) interest rate for the
general public ranges from 3.00% to 6.60% per annum across tenures
from 7 days to 10 years, with senior citizens earning an additional
0.50%. The 1-year FD interest rate is 6.25% for the general public and
6.75% for senior citizens.

Effective 2 March 2026, domestic retail term deposit rates by tenure
(general public / senior citizen):
- 7 to 14 days: 3.00% / 3.50%
- 15 to 30 days: 3.00% / 3.50%
- 31 to 45 days: 3.00% / 3.50%
- 46 to 90 days: 4.50% / 5.00%
- 91 to 179 days: 4.25% / 4.75%
- 180 to 210 days: 5.50% / 6.00%
- 211 to 269 days: 5.50% / 6.00%
- 270 days to below 1 year: 5.50% / 6.00%
- 1 year: 6.25% / 6.75%
- above 1 year to below 2 years: 6.30% / 6.80%
- 450 days (special tenor): 6.60% / 7.10%
- 2 years to below 3 years: 6.30% / 6.80%
- 3 years to below 5 years: 6.25% / 6.75%
- 5 years to below 8 years: 6.00% / 6.50%
- 8 to 10 years: 6.00% / 6.50%

Special Scheme — Star Sunidhi (tax-saving FD, 5 years): 6.00% general
public, 6.50% senior citizens, same rate as the standard 5-8 year band.

Note: rates above are for callable domestic/NRO term deposits below
Rs. 3 crore. Bank of India separately publishes tables for Rs. 3-10
crore deposits and NRE/FCNR deposits, not covered here.

Since this data is secondary-sourced (the bank's own site could not be
reached), it should be treated as indicative rather than final —
verify the current card rate directly with the bank, especially for a
large deposit, before quoting it as certain. Rates are revised
periodically at RBI's bi-monthly Monetary Policy Committee reviews and
occasionally between reviews.
