Bank of India — Digital Banking Products

BOI Mobile:
Bank of India's mobile banking app (Android/iOS) — view and transact
across all linked accounts including loans and deposits, check balances,
view/email detailed statements and passbook, and transfer funds via
IMPS/NEFT/RTGS/UPI. Includes a "Favourite" feature for fast repeat
transfers, ATM locator, cheque-book requests, and cheque status
tracking.

UPI:
Supported within the BOI Mobile app — instant 24x7 fund transfer linked
directly to the customer's BOI account, using account number+IFSC, VPA,
or QR code.

Omni Neo (replacing StarConnect):
Bank of India's next-generation internet banking platform — a unified
digital framework converging retail, corporate, and mobile banking
features, positioned as the successor to the bank's older StarConnect
internet banking service.

BOI Biz Pay:
A dedicated app for BOI merchants to accept digital payments (a
merchant-side UPI/QR payment-acceptance product, distinct from the
customer-facing BOI Mobile app).

Confirm current app names and feature availability with the bank, since
BOI's platform (Omni Neo) is described as a recent migration from its
older StarConnect service and naming may still be in transition.
