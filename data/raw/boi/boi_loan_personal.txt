Bank of India Star Personal Loan

Interest Rate:
Reported starting from around 10.85% p.a. for general applicants, with a
broader reported range up to 13.85% p.a. depending on income, credit
score, and employer category. The bank's own Star Personal Loan page is
separately reported starting around 15.20% p.a. for its standard
salaried/self-employed/business-owner segment — the discrepancy between
aggregator-reported and bank-reported starting rates likely reflects
different eligible-applicant segments (e.g. preferential schemes for
government/defence/pensioners vs. the general public rate); confirm
which applies to a specific applicant with the bank.

Loan Amount & Tenure:
Up to Rs. 25 lakh, depending on income and repayment capacity. Repayment
tenure up to 84 months.

Eligibility & Concessions:
Available to salaried individuals, self-employed professionals, and
business owners. Special schemes exist for pensioners, doctors, and
persons with disabilities. Women applicants receive a reported 0.50%
interest rate concession.

This is a headline reference — personal loan pricing is highly
profile-dependent; always confirm the applicant's actual quoted rate
and eligibility with the bank.
