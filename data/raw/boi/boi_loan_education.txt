Bank of India Star Education Loan

Products:
- Star Education Loan (Studies in India)
- Star Education Loan (Studies Abroad) — up to Rs. 150 lakh, interest
  rate reported starting around 8.40% p.a. (as of May 2026)
- Star Education Loan for Working Professionals
- Star PM-Vidyalaxmi Education Loan — linked to the government's
  PM-Vidyalaxmi portal/scheme

Moratorium:
Course duration plus one year, for both the India and Abroad variants.
Only simple interest is charged during the moratorium period; the full
applicable rate applies once regular repayment begins.

Government Interest Subsidy:
Families with annual income up to Rs. 8 lakh may be eligible for an
interest subsidy under CSIS (now integrated into the PM-Vidyalaxmi
portal), plus a reported 3% interest subvention under PM-Vidyalaxmi
during the moratorium period for eligible applicants.

This is a headline reference — exact eligibility (course/institution
list, income thresholds, collateral requirement above a certain loan
size) and the current applicable rate should be confirmed with the bank
for a specific applicant.
