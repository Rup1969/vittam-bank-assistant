Bank of India MSME Loans

Collateral-Free Lending (CGTMSE):
Bank of India participates in the Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro
and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) scheme, run jointly by the Ministry of
MSME and SIDBI, which lets participating banks extend collateral-free
credit up to Rs. 10 crore to eligible Micro and Small Enterprises. The
scheme provides 75%-85% guarantee coverage to the lending bank (i.e. the
government-backed trust absorbs most of the default risk), with an
Annual Guarantee Fee starting at approximately 0.37% p.a. — this fee is
typically factored into the borrower's overall cost even though the
loan itself carries no collateral requirement. Special benefit
provisions exist for women, transgender entrepreneurs, and informal
micro enterprises.
