Yes, Bank of India offers a Star Loan Against Property (mortgage loan)
against an owned residential or commercial property, with interest
rate starting from 9.10% p.a., loan amount up to Rs. 7.5 crore, tenure
up to 15 years.

Structure:
A mortgage-backed loan against an owned residential or commercial
property. Also offered as a reducible overdraft facility, where the
drawing limit reduces over time based on the repayment schedule — a
useful alternative to a plain term loan for customers who want
flexible, running access to the sanctioned limit rather than a single
lump-sum disbursal.

This is a headline reference — confirm the current rate, loan-to-value
ratio, and eligibility with the bank before advising a customer.
