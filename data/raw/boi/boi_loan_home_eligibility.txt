Bank of India Star Home Loan — Rate Detail, LTV, and Tenure

Interest Rate detail:
RBLR was cut from 8.85% to 8.35% effective 6 June 2025, and reported at
8.10% as of 1 January 2026. Below-600-CIBIL-score applicants start
around 8.50% — third-party aggregator figures vary, so always confirm
the exact quoted rate with the bank.

Loan Amount & LTV:
Up to 90% of the property value financed.

Tenure:
Up to 360 months (30 years).

This is a floating, RBLR-linked rate — it moves with RBI repo-rate
changes over the loan tenure. Confirm the current RBLR value and the
applicant's specific quoted spread with the bank before quoting.
