ICICI Bank Home Loan

ICICI Bank's home loan interest rate currently starts from 7.50% per
annum for pre-approved/well-qualified applicants, linked to the bank's
repo-rate-linked benchmark. This is the standard housing loan for
purchasing or constructing a home.

Interest Rate:
Officially advertised starting rate: 7.50% p.a. (for customers with a
pre-approved home loan sanction). Broader reported range across
applicant profiles: 7.70%-10.05% p.a., depending on bureau/credit
score, customer segment, and overall profile.

Rate Type:
Both fixed and floating interest rate options are offered. Floating
rates are linked to the repo rate.

Tenure & Fees:
Tenure up to 30 years. Per RBI rules, no prepayment/foreclosure charges
on floating-rate home loans for individual borrowers; fixed-rate home
loans may carry a prepayment charge up to 2% of the prepaid amount.

This is a headline reference — confirm the current rate and the
applicant's specific quoted spread with the bank before quoting.
