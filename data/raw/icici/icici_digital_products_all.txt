ICICI Bank — Digital Banking Products

iMobile Pay:
ICICI Bank's unified mobile banking + payments app (Android/iOS) —
described as India's first app offering both UPI payments and full
banking services for ANY bank's customers, not just ICICI account
holders. Covers savings accounts, investments, loans, and credit cards
alongside payments.

UPI Features:
Pay to any UPI ID or merchant via QR code, peer-to-peer transfers, and
bill payments/recharges, all secured by UPI PIN. A "Pay to Contacts"
feature shows UPI IDs of contacts across different payment apps/wallets
for easier transfers. UPI transactions are instant and available 24x7,
including bank holidays. Multiple bank accounts (ICICI or otherwise)
can be linked within the app, with the user choosing which account to
use per transaction.

Other Services via iMobile Pay:
Utility bill payments, mobile recharges, movie ticket booking, travel
ticket booking, gift cards, and investments in mutual funds, FDs, and
RDs — all from within the single app.

Confirm current app features and UPI limits with the bank, since these
are periodically revised by NPCI/RBI guidance as well as bank policy.
