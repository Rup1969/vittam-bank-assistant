ICICI Bank Savings Account — Minimum Balance and Interest Rate

ICICI Bank's savings account interest rate is 2.5% p.a. across all
balance tiers — confirmed directly from the bank's official savings
account page ("ICICI Bank currently offers interest rate of 2.5% p.a.
(per annum) across all savings account balances"), calculated daily on
the End-of-Day balance and credited quarterly (end of March, June,
September, December).

Minimum balance: for accounts opened on or after 1 August 2025, ICICI
Bank's Monthly Average Balance (MAB) requirement is Rs. 15,000 for
metro/urban locations, Rs. 7,500 for semi-urban locations, and Rs. 2,500
for rural locations. This exact figure was NOT directly readable from
ICICI's page in this fetch — the page's own FAQ ("What is the ICICI Bank
Savings Account minimum balance for new accounts opened after 01 August
2025?") confirms this is the bank's own stated policy, but the answer
text loads client-side on click and did not render during automated
fetch. The rupee figures above are cross-referenced from secondary
aggregators that cite ICICI's official MAB policy — verify the exact
current figure on ICICI's live page before quoting as final.

Account variants: ICICI Bank offers savings accounts tailored to
different customer segments (Senior Citizen, Homemaker, Minor, Working
Professional, Student, Doctor, Pension Account) — each may carry a
different MAB requirement; check the specific variant's page.

Accounts opened before 1 August 2025 may be governed by a different,
earlier fee/MAB schedule — the bank's page explicitly separates "Schedule
of charges: Accounts opened from August 01, 2025" from "Accounts opened
till 31 July 2025."

If the MAB requirement is not maintained, a penalty is charged based on
the shortfall amount — use ICICI's MAB Calculator (linked from the
savings account page) for the exact penalty computation.

Verify current MAB figures and interest rate against ICICI Bank's
official website before quoting to a customer as final, since these are
periodically revised (confirmed changed as recently as August 2025).
