ICICI Bank Loan Against Property (LAP) / Mortgage Loan

ICICI Bank's Loan Against Property interest rate ranges from 10.60% to
12.25% per annum, Repo-Rate-linked, depending on loan amount and
customer segment (rates valid till 31 July 2026, per the bank's own
page).

Rate Table — Non-Housing Loans (LAP / Non-Residential Premises / Lease
Rental Discounting):
- Up to Rs. 50 lakh: Salaried/Self-Employed PSL customers 11.10%-11.75%;
  Self-Employed Non-PSL customers 11.60%-12.25%.
- Rs. 50 lakh to Rs. 100 lakh: Salaried/Self-Employed PSL customers
  10.85%-11.50%; Self-Employed Non-PSL customers 11.35%-12.00%.
- Above Rs. 100 lakh: Salaried/Self-Employed PSL customers 10.60%-11.25%;
  Self-Employed Non-PSL customers 11.10%-11.75%.

Benchmark: rates are linked to the RBI Repo Rate, effective 5.25% as of
5 December 2025. Actual rate within each band depends on Bureau Score
(credit score), customer profile, and segment.

Loan Features:
- Loan up to 75% of the property's market value.
- Loan tenure of up to 15 years.
- Overdraft facility available — interest charged only on the amount
  utilised.
- Nil foreclosure charges for SMEs.
- Top-up facility available on an existing mortgage loan.
- Special LTV (Loan-to-Value) terms for doctors.
- Online application with minimal documentation and instant provisional
  sanction.

Eligibility:
- Age: Salaried 20-65 years; Self-employed 21-70 years.
- Employment type: salaried, self-employed professionals, and
  self-employed non-professionals.
- Credit Score: 700 and above required.
- Citizenship: Indian residents and NRIs eligible.
- Income proof: stable and regular income required.

Rates are Repo-Rate-linked and revised periodically (the current table
is explicitly valid only till 31 July 2026); verify the current rate
band and repo rate with the bank before quoting to a customer.
