ICICI Bank Personal Loan

Interest Rate:
Officially advertised starting rate: 9.99% p.a. Broader reported range
across applicant profiles: 9.99%-16.5% p.a., depending on credit
score, monthly income, and the applicant's existing relationship with
ICICI Bank (existing customers with a strong banking history typically
get more preferential pricing).

This is a headline reference — personal loan pricing is highly
profile-dependent; always confirm the applicant's actual quoted rate
and eligibility with the bank.
