ICICI Bank Education Loan

Interest Rate:
ICICI Bank's education loan rates range approximately 8.50%-10.95% p.a.
depending on the course/institute category and whether the loan is
secured (collateral-backed) or unsecured — per ICICI's own published
rate card. Representative rates: Engineering/Management at select
domestic institutes (secured) 8.50%; Medical courses (secured) 8.75%;
Abroad studies (secured) 9.00%; PM Vidyalaxmi government scheme 9.45%;
CGFSEL government subsidy scheme 10.95%. Unsecured variants of the same
course category run roughly 0.75-1.25 percentage points above the
secured rate.

ICICI Bank does offer education loan products for domestic and
international study, typically with a moratorium of the course
duration plus a grace period and a multi-year repayment tenure —
confirm the exact rate for a specific course/institute category,
moratorium terms, and collateral thresholds with the bank before
advising a customer.
