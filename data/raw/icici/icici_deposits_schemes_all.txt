ICICI Bank — Deposit Scheme Variants

iWish Flexible Recurring Deposit:
A goal-based recurring deposit product allowing flexible, variable
instalment amounts (unlike a fixed-instalment RD) — positioned for
customers saving toward a specific goal (e.g. a vacation, a purchase)
rather than a rigid monthly commitment. Rate reported up to 7.10% p.a.

Recurring Deposit:
Standard fixed-instalment recurring deposit, rate reported up to
7.10% p.a.

FD Special Features:
- Flexible payout options: monthly, quarterly, annual, or cumulative
  (compounded, paid at maturity).
- Overdraft Against FD (ODFD): instant liquidity against an existing
  FD without breaking it.
- Instant FD creation via net banking/iMobile app.
- Premature withdrawal penalty: 0.50% for deposits held less than 1
  year, 1.00% for deposits held 1-5 years (both tiers, regardless of
  deposit size below/above Rs. 25 crore), and 1.00%-1.50% for 5+ year
  deposits (1.00% below Rs. 25 crore, 1.50% at or above Rs. 25 crore).
- A non-withdrawable (no premature closure) FD variant is also offered
  for domestic, NRO, and NRE deposits, typically at a different rate
  than the standard withdrawable FD.

This is a summary of publicly listed ICICI Bank deposit scheme names —
exact current rates, minimum amounts, and eligibility should be
confirmed with the bank before advising a customer.
