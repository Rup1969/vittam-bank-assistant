ICICI Bank Kisan Credit Card

Purpose:
A revolving credit facility for farmers' short-term crop cultivation,
harvesting, and farm-maintenance credit needs, under the nationwide
Kisan Credit Card scheme (launched by the Indian government in August
1998) that ICICI Bank, like other banks, implements.

Interest Rate:
A subsidized rate of 7% p.a. is available for loans up to Rs. 3 lakh
in rural or semi-urban branches, reflecting the government's Interest
Subvention Scheme for short-term crop loans.

Collateral:
Collateral-free lending is available under the KCC scheme for amounts
up to Rs. 1.60 lakh — no security required for loans at or below this
threshold.

This is a headline reference — confirm the current effective rate and
exact collateral thresholds with the bank or nearest branch.
