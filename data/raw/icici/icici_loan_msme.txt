ICICI Bank MSME / Collateral-Free Business Loans

Collateral-Free Loan:
ICICI Bank offers a dedicated Collateral-Free Loan product for MSMEs,
built under the CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and
Small Enterprises) scheme — a joint SIDBI/Ministry of MSME government
scheme under the MSMED Act, 2006. Loan amount ranges from Rs. 10 lakh
to Rs. 5 crore, with the full range available without collateral under
CGTMSE coverage — notably a higher collateral-free ceiling (Rs. 5
crore) than several public sector banks' typical Rs. 10 lakh
collateral-free threshold.

This is a headline reference — confirm current interest rates,
processing fees, and eligibility criteria with the bank before
advising a customer.
