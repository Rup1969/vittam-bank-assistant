ICICI Bank Overdraft Against Fixed Deposit (Loan Against Deposit)

ICICI Bank's Overdraft Against FD is priced at the FD's own interest
rate plus 2% for general customers, or FD rate plus 1% for senior
citizens, with up to 90% of the FD value available instantly as an
overdraft via the iMobile app or Net Banking, without breaking the FD.

Key Terms:
- Standard rate: FD rate + 2% (General customers) / FD rate + 1%
  (Senior citizens).
- Overdraft limit: up to 90% of the FD value, available instantly and
  digitally.
- No processing fee.
- Interest charged only on the amount actually utilised, for the
  period utilised.
- The underlying FD continues to earn interest at its contracted rate
  throughout.
- Penalty: 5% p.a. on overdue or overdrawn amounts (default charge).
- Fund transfer restriction: transfers from the OD account allowed only
  to the linked Savings Account — no UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS, or cash
  deposits into the OD account itself.

Eligibility: must be an existing ICICI Bank FD holder (Regular FD, Tax
Saver FD, or Money Multiplier FD), with a linked Savings Account and
valid Customer ID.

Documents Required: KYC documents, FD receipt, signed application form.

How to Apply: via ICICI Bank's iMobile app or Net Banking — fully
digital, no branch visit required.

Verify the current FD rate and applicable spread with the bank before
quoting the exact overdraft rate to a customer, since it's derived
from the specific FD's own rate.
