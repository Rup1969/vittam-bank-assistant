ICICI Bank Fixed Deposit Interest Rates (below Rs. 3 crore)

ICICI Bank's current fixed deposit (FD) interest rate for the general
public ranges from 2.75% to 6.50% per annum across tenures from 7 days
to 10 years, with senior citizens earning 0.50% to 0.60% more
depending on tenure. The 3-year-1-day to 5-year FD interest rate is
6.50% for the general public and 7.10% for senior citizens — ICICI's
highest published slab, thanks to an extra promotional premium beyond
the standard 0.50% on this specific tenure band.

Effective 14 August 2026, domestic retail term deposit rates by tenure
(general public / senior citizen):
- 7 to 45 days: 2.75% / 3.25%
- 46 to 90 days: 4.00% / 4.50%
- 91 to 184 days: 4.50% / 5.00%
- 185 days to less than 1 year: 5.50% / 6.00%
- 1 year to less than 18 months: 6.25% / 6.75%
- 18 months to 2 years: 6.30% / 6.80%
- 2 years 1 day to 3 years: 6.45% / 6.95%
- 3 years 1 day to 5 years: 6.50% / 7.10%
- 5 years 1 day to 10 years: 6.50% / 7.00%
- 5-year Tax Saver FD: 6.50% / 7.10%

Senior Citizen Premium: an additional 0.50% for most tenures; the
3-year-1-day-to-5-year and Tax Saver bands carry a larger 0.60%
premium instead of the standard 0.50%.

Minimum Deposit: Rs. 10,000.

Note: ICICI's rate page also separately lists rates for the Rs. 3
crore to Rs. 5 crore band and further bulk-deposit tiers up to Rs. 500
crore — this document covers only the retail (below Rs. 3 crore) tier.

Rates are revised periodically; verify the current card rate with the
bank before quoting for a large deposit.
