Bank of Maharashtra Education Loan

Bank of Maharashtra's education loan interest rate is 6.85% p.a., with
additional concessions available for specific borrowers.

Coverage:
Financing for meritorious students pursuing higher studies at premier
institutions (IIMs, ISB, IITs, NITs, XLRI, MBBS/medical colleges, etc.),
graduation and diploma programs at UGC-approved institutions, and
overseas studies.

Collateral:
- Up to Rs. 7.50 lakh: no collateral security required.
- Above Rs. 7.50 lakh: acceptable tangible security with sufficient
  margin required.
- Under the Maha Scholar scheme for premier institutes: no collateral
  needed for loans up to Rs. 40 lakh.

Margin:
- Maha Scholar (List A institutes): zero margin.
- Maha Scholar (List B & C institutes): 5% margin.
- Model Education Loan up to Rs. 4 lakh: no margin required.
- Model Education Loan above Rs. 4 lakh: 5% margin (domestic studies),
  15% margin (overseas studies).

Interest Concessions:
Up to 0.50% total — 0.50% for girl students, 0.50% for existing home
loan borrowers, up to 0.25% for meritorious students.
