Bank of Maharashtra MSME / Business Loan Schemes

Bank of Maharashtra offers a broad range of MSME schemes:
- Own schemes: GST Credit Scheme, hospitality-sector loans, contractor
  financing, transport-operator vehicle loans, machinery/equipment
  schemes, professional/doctor loans, startup financing.
- Government-backed programs: Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana, Stand-Up
  India, PM SVANidhi, Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS
  5.0).
- General facilities: collateral-free term loans, collateral-free cash
  credit, regular cash credit arrangements.

This is a summary-level entry only — the bank's MSME landing page names
these schemes but does not publish inline interest rates, exact loan-
amount ranges, collateral specifications, or CGTMSE guarantee coverage
percentages; those live on each individual scheme's own sub-page, not
separately fetched in this pass. Confirm exact terms with the bank
before relying on any figure for these specific schemes.
