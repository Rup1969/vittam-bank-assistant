Bank of Maharashtra Car Loan

Bank of Maharashtra's car loan interest rate starts from 7.45% p.a.,
ranging up to 12.00% p.a. depending on credit score. A 0.25% rate
concession applies for corporate salary account holders and existing
home loan borrowers.

Eligibility:
Salaried employees with at least 1 year of continuous service,
self-employed professionals and business owners, agriculturists with
verifiable income, and companies/corporate entities.

Loan Amount:
Up to 90% of the car's value, typically capped at 36 times net monthly
salary, subject to total deductions not exceeding 65% of gross income.

Tenure:
Maximum 84 months (7 years), with flexible repayment periods.

Other Features:
No pre-payment, pre-closure, or part-payment charges. Interest charged
on a daily reducing balance. The bank advertises "most competitive"
processing fees without stating an exact figure on this page.
