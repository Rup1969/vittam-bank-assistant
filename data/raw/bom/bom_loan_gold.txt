Bank of Maharashtra Gold Loan

Bank of Maharashtra's gold loan interest rate is 8.50% p.a. (RLLR +
0.45%).

Eligibility:
All individuals, including bank staff, aged 18 and above, who own the
gold ornaments being pledged. Basic KYC (identity and address proof)
required.

Loan Amount:
Minimum Rs. 20,000, maximum Rs. 100 lakh (subject to terms).

Loan-to-Value (LTV):
For EMI/interest-only repayment (up to 12 months): 75% LTV up to Rs. 100
lakh, 80% up to Rs. 5 lakh, 85% up to Rs. 2.5 lakh.
For bullet repayment (up to 12 months): 65% LTV up to Rs. 100 lakh, 70%
up to Rs. 5 lakh, 75% up to Rs. 2.5 lakh.

Tenure:
Standard loans up to 24 months; bullet repayment up to 12 months.

Processing Fee:
Nil up to Rs. 3 lakh; Rs. 500-Rs. 2,000 above that.

Other Features:
No pre-payment/pre-closure/part-payment charges, no guarantor required,
no CIBIL score check.
