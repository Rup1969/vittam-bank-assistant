Bank of Maharashtra Loan Against Property — Eligibility, LTV, and Fees

Eligibility:
Permanent employees of Central/State Government or PSUs, plus
individual businessmen, professionals, and self-employed individuals
who own unencumbered residential or commercial property. Minimum annual
income Rs. 5 lakh.

Loan Amount:
Minimum Rs. 5 lakh; maximum Rs. 10 crore (metro properties) or Rs. 5
crore (other locations), determined by repaying capacity and property
valuation.

Loan-to-Value (LTV) / Margin:
- Up to Rs. 1 crore: 60% LTV (40% margin).
- Above Rs. 1 crore: 50% LTV (50% margin).

Tenure:
Maximum 10 years, or until the borrower turns 75, whichever is earlier.

Fees:
Processing fee 1% of loan amount + GST. Debt obligation ratio (all EMIs
including proposed) must not exceed 60% of gross income.

Security:
Mortgage of the property.

Interest Rate:
Not stated as a specific percentage on this product page — refer to the
bank's "Retail Interest Rates" page for the current effective rate.
