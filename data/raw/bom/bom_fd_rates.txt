Bank of Maharashtra Fixed Deposit (FD) Interest Rates

Bank of Maharashtra's current fixed deposit (FD) interest rate for the
general public ranges from 2.60% to 6.40% p.a. depending on tenure. The
1-year FD interest rate is 6.40% for the general public and 6.90% for
senior citizens — the single highest-rate slab on the entire retail
table.

Retail FD Rates (below Rs. 3 crore), w.e.f. 10.08.2026:

Tenure                  General    Senior Citizen
7-30 days                2.60%      3.10%
31-45 days                2.75%      3.25%
46-90 days                3.75%      4.25%
91-119 days               4.00%      4.50%
120-180 days              4.75%      5.25%
181-270 days              5.00%      5.50%
271-364 days              5.25%      5.75%
1 Year                    6.40%      6.90%
Above 1 year to 2 years   6.15%      6.65%
Above 2 years to 3 years  5.25%      5.75%
Above 3 years to 5 years  5.00%      5.50%
Above 5 years             5.00%      5.50%

Senior citizens receive a flat +0.50% premium across every tenure shown
above. Non-callable retail rates are not published separately from the
callable rate for deposits below Rs. 3 crore (unlike some other banks)
— only bulk/crore-tier deposits carry a distinct non-callable rate.
