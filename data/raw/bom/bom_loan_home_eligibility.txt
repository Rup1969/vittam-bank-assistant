Bank of Maharashtra Home Loan — Eligibility, LTV, and Fees

Eligibility:
Individual salaried employees with at least 1 year of continuous
service, self-employed professionals and businessmen, and NRIs holding
an Indian passport or PIOs/OCIs with a valid employment contract.

Loan Amount / LTV:
The sanctioned amount is the lowest of: the loan amount assessed via
permissible-deduction norms, the maximum permissible LTV ratio, and the
amount requested. Exact LTV percentage not separately published.

Processing Fee:
0.25% of loan amount + GST — fully waived through 31 December 2025.

Other Features:
No pre-payment, pre-closure, or part-payment charges. Online application
available.
