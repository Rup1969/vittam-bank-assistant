Bank of Maharashtra Kisan Credit Card (MKCC)

Yes — Bank of Maharashtra offers a Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme, a
revolving credit facility for farmers' cultivation and allied
agricultural needs, under the nationwide Kisan Credit Card scheme.

Bank of Maharashtra's Kisan Credit Card interest rate:
- Up to Rs. 3 lakh: 7% p.a. fixed, under the government's interest
  subvention scheme, up to one year.
- Rs. 3 lakh to Rs. 10 lakh: 1-Year MCLR + 0.50% + 2.00%.
- Above Rs. 10 lakh: 1-Year MCLR + 0.50% + 3.00%.

Eligibility:
All farmers — individual or joint landholders — plus tenant farmers,
sharecroppers, and farmer groups (SHGs/JLGs).

Credit Limit:
The first-year limit combines the crop-based scale of finance with an
additional 10% for post-harvest expenses and 20% for equipment
maintenance. Subsequent years allow a 10% annual increase for cost
escalation.

Margin:
Nil up to Rs. 2 lakh; 15%-20% above Rs. 2 lakh, depending on loan
purpose and amount.

Security:
Crop hypothecation alone suffices up to Rs. 2 lakh; above that,
hypothecation of crops plus third-party guarantee/mortgage of land is
required.

Validity:
The KCC limit is valid for 5 years, subject to annual review.
