Bank of Maharashtra Loan Against Property: yes, this bank offers a
mortgage loan against unencumbered residential or commercial property,
for salaried and self-employed borrowers. Loan amount Rs. 5 lakh to
Rs. 10 crore at up to 60% LTV, tenure up to 10 years.
