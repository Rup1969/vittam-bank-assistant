Bank of Maharashtra Digital Banking — Zen Lyfe App

Bank of Maharashtra's mobile banking app is called Zen Lyfe, described
as a "next-generation" platform combining banking with lifestyle
services.

Key Features:
- Payments/transfers: UPI, NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, and unified recurring
  payments, all in one place.
- Unified account overview across all accounts and transactions.
- Instant savings-account opening via remote onboarding and e-KYC.
- Apply for home, personal, auto, and business loans directly in-app.
- Book, manage, and close fixed and recurring deposits instantly.
- Card controls: lock/unlock cards, set PINs, customize limits, manage
  virtual debit cards.
- Lifestyle marketplace: book hotels, flights, buses; buy gift coupons;
  make donations; consult doctors.
- Security: 100+ app security features, continuous risk analytics, and
  multi-factor authentication.

Available on both iOS and Android; registration requires device binding
and identity verification via debit card, internet banking, or a branch
token. The bank's internet banking portal is at mahaconnect.bank.in.
