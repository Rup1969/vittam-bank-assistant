Bank of Maharashtra — Special Deposit Schemes and Recurring Deposit

Special Term Deposit Schemes (Non-Callable only — a customer forgoes
premature withdrawal in exchange for a fixed higher rate):
- 400 Days Special Scheme: 6.65% p.a. for deposits below Rs. 3 crore —
  higher than both the neighbouring 271-364 day slab (5.25%) and the
  standard 1-year slab (6.40%) on the regular callable table.
- Green Deposit (1777 days): 6.05% p.a. below Rs. 3 crore; 5.45% p.a.
  for Rs. 3 crore to Rs. 10 crore.

Recurring Deposit:
Bank of Maharashtra states that recurring deposit interest rates are
"the same as the interest rates applicable to Term Deposits" (see
bom_fd_rates.txt for the full tenure table) — no separate RD-specific
rate table or minimum-installment figure is published on this page.

Tax-Saving Scheme:
Bank Term Deposit Scheme 2006 (5-year tax-saving FD), applicable up to
Rs. 1.5 lakh.

Senior Citizen Premium:
An additional 0.50% p.a. applies for senior citizens on all maturity
slabs of 91 days and above, for deposits up to Rs. 5 crore.
