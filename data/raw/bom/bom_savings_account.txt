Bank of Maharashtra Savings Account

Bank of Maharashtra pays interest on the DAILY closing balance in a
savings account, credited quarterly. An illustrative example on the
bank's own page: a 2.75% p.a. rate on a Rs. 5 lakh balance generates
approximately Rs. 1,130 in interest per month.

Key Features:
- Free RuPay/Visa debit card.
- Digital banking access (mobile app, internet banking, UPI).
- Deposit insurance coverage up to Rs. 5 lakh (standard DICGC cover).
- Cheque book availability.
- Bill payment and fund transfer capabilities.

Minimum Balance:
The bank states that a minimum balance must be maintained on a Monthly
Average Balance (MAB) basis, and that service charges apply if it is
not maintained — but does NOT publish the exact rupee figure (by branch
location — metro/urban/semi-urban/rural) on this page. The precise MAB
requirement lives on the bank's separate Service Charges schedule, not
fetched in this pass; confirm the exact amount with the bank before
relying on any figure.
