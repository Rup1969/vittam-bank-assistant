Bank of Maharashtra Personal Loan — Eligibility, Tenure, and Fees

Eligibility:
Salaried employees, professionals, or business owners. Minimum annual
income Rs. 3 lakh. Self-employed individuals need 2-3 years of IT
returns.

Tenure:
- Salaried customers with a Bank of Maharashtra salary account: 84
  months.
- Salaried customers with an account at another bank: 60 months.
- Professionals: 60 months.
- Business-class applicants with an existing home loan: 84 months.

Processing Fee:
1.00% of loan amount + GST.

Other Features:
No guarantor required, no prepayment penalty, no hidden charges,
minimal documentation. Digital application available via the MahaMobile
app or online portal with QR-code scanning.
