HDFC Bank — Digital Banking Products

PayZapp:
HDFC Bank's all-in-one payment app — combines UPI, a digital wallet,
bill payments, and rewards, usable by both HDFC and non-HDFC customers.
Supports mobile/DTH/data-card recharges, utility bill payments, flight/
bus/hotel booking and comparison, online shopping, and movie ticket
booking, all from within the single app. A "swipe to pay" feature lets
users pay with their Zapp Account or HDFC debit/credit card without an
OTP for supported transactions.

HDFC Bank Mobile Banking App:
The bank's core mobile banking app (separate from PayZapp) covering
account management, fund transfers, and standard banking services.

UPI:
Real-time money transfers 24x7, including holidays, with funds
credited instantly. UPI limit reported at Rs. 1 lakh per day, with no
charges for UPI payments. A "Request Money" feature lets a user request
funds from another UPI ID directly within the Mobile Banking app or
PayZapp.

Security:
OTP, MPIN, and standard secure banking protocols protect transactions.

Confirm current app names, features, and UPI limits with the bank,
since these are periodically revised by NPCI/RBI guidance as well as
bank policy.
