HDFC Bank Education Loan

Interest Rate:
HDFC Bank's education loan carries a flat published rate of 10.50%
p.a., per the bank's own rate card. HDFC does not publish separate
secured/unsecured or course-category tiers for this product the way
some other banks do — this single rate applies across the product.

HDFC Bank offers education loans for domestic and international study,
typically with a moratorium of the course duration plus a grace period
before EMI repayment begins, and a multi-year repayment tenure —
confirm current eligibility, moratorium terms, and collateral
thresholds with the bank before advising a customer.
