HDFC Bank Kisan Credit Card

Purpose:
A revolving credit facility for farmers' cultivation and allied
agricultural credit needs, under the nationwide Kisan Credit Card
scheme, aimed at providing access to collateral-free credit alongside
skill training, modern farming tools, and market-linkage support.

Credit Limit & Collateral:
Credit limit up to Rs. 3 lakh. For amounts up to Rs. 1.60 lakh, no
collateral is required. Above Rs. 1.60 lakh, the bank may require
collateral in the form of tractors, trolleys, or by taking possession
of the crop the loan financed.

This is a headline reference — confirm the current effective rate
(including any government interest subvention applicable) and exact
collateral thresholds with the bank or nearest branch.
