HDFC Bank Personal Loan

Interest Rate:
Reported starting rate: 9.99% p.a. Broader reported range across
applicant profiles: 10.9%-24% p.a., depending on credit score, monthly
income, job profile, and employer's profile — a notably wide upper end
compared to several peer banks, suggesting HDFC prices lower-profile
applicants more conservatively.

This is a headline reference — personal loan pricing is highly
profile-dependent; always confirm the applicant's actual quoted rate
and eligibility with the bank.
