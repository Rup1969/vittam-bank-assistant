HDFC Bank MSME / Collateral-Free Business Loans

CGTMSE Scheme:
HDFC Bank offers loans under the government's Credit Guarantee Fund
Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) scheme — collateral-free
working capital and term loan solutions for MSMEs, without requiring
collateral or a third-party guarantee.

Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups (CGSS):
A separate scheme offering collateral-free debt funding up to Rs. 10
crore specifically for startups, distinct from the general CGTMSE
scheme aimed at established MSMEs.

This is a headline reference — confirm current interest rates,
processing fees, and eligibility criteria (including which scheme
applies to a specific business) with the bank before advising a
customer.
