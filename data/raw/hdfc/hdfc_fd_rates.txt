HDFC Bank Fixed Deposit Interest Rates (below Rs. 3 crore)

<!--AUTO:START-->
HDFC Bank's current fixed deposit (FD) interest rate for the general
public ranges from 2.75% to 6.6% per annum across its full set
of published tenures, with senior citizens earning an additional 0.5% over the general rate on most tenures (one or more bands carry a wider premium -- the table below always shows each band's own real senior rate, never assume a flat 0.5%).

Effective 4 November 2026, domestic retail term deposit rates by tenure
(general public / senior citizen):
- 7 - 14 days: 2.75% / 3.25%
- 15-29 days: 2.75% / 3.25%
- 30-45 days: 3.25% / 3.75%
- 46-60 days: 4.25% / 4.75%
- 61-89 days: 4.75% / 5.25%
- 90 days <= 6 months: 4.75% / 5.25%
- 6 months 1 day <=9 months: 5.65% / 6.15%
- 9 months 1 day to < 1 Year: 5.9% / 6.4%
- 1 Year to < 15 months: 6.25% / 6.75%
- 15 months to < 18 months: 6.35% / 6.85%
- 18 months to < 21 months: 6.5% / 7%
- 21 months to 2 years: 6.5% / 7%
- 2 Years 1 day to < 2 Year 11 Months: 6.55% / 7.05%
- 2 Years 11 Months (35 months): 6.55% / 7.05%
- 2 Years 11 Months 1 day <= 3 Year: 6.55% / 7.05%
- 3 Years 1 day to < 4 Years 7 Months: 6.6% / 7.2%
- 4 Year 7 Months (55 months): 6.6% / 7.2%
- 4 Year 7 Months 1 day <=5 Years: 6.6% / 7.2%
- 5 Years 1 day to 10 Years: 6.6% / 7.1%
<!--AUTO:END-->

Note: this is an unusually finely-segmented tenure table compared to
most other banks — several bands are only a few weeks or months wide
(e.g. "2 years 11 months 1 day to 3 years" is its own distinct slab
from the surrounding bands). Always quote the exact published rate for
the requested tenure rather than rounding to a nearby band.

HDFC Bank's rate page also separately lists a bulk-deposit tier (Rs. 3
crore to Rs. 5 crore) with its own rate table — this document covers
only the retail (below Rs. 3 crore) tier.
