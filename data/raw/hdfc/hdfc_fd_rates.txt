HDFC Bank Fixed Deposit Interest Rates (below Rs. 3 crore)

HDFC Bank's current fixed deposit (FD) interest rate for the general
public ranges from 2.75% to 6.50% per annum across tenures from 7 days
to 10 years, with senior citizens earning an additional 0.50% across
most tenures. The 1-year-to-under-15-months FD interest rate is 6.25%
for the general public and 6.75% for senior citizens; HDFC's highest
published slab is 6.50% general / 7.10% senior, for the 3-years-1-day
to under-4-years-7-months band — the senior rate here carries a wider
0.60% premium over general, not the flat 0.50% every other tenure gets.

Effective 19 August 2026, domestic retail term deposit rates by tenure
(general public / senior citizen):
- 7-14 days: 2.75% / 3.25%
- 15-29 days: 2.75% / 3.25%
- 30-45 days: 3.25% / 3.75%
- 46-60 days: 4.25% / 4.75%
- 61-89 days: 4.25% / 4.75%
- 90 days to 6 months: 4.25% / 4.75%
- 6 months 1 day to 9 months: 5.50% / 6.00%
- 9 months 1 day to less than 1 year: 5.75% / 6.25%
- 1 year to less than 15 months: 6.25% / 6.75%
- 15 months to less than 18 months: 6.35% / 6.85%
- 18 months to less than 21 months: 6.45% / 6.95%
- 21 months to 2 years: 6.45% / 6.95%
- 2 years 1 day to less than 2 years 11 months: 6.45% / 6.95%
- 2 years 11 months (35 months): 6.45% / 6.95%
- 2 years 11 months 1 day to 3 years: 6.45% / 6.95%
- 3 years 1 day to less than 4 years 7 months: 6.50% / 7.10%
- 4 years 7 months (55 months): 6.40% / 6.90%
- 4 years 7 months 1 day to 5 years: 6.40% / 6.90%
- 5 years 1 day to 10 years: 6.15% / 6.65%

Senior Citizen Premium: an additional 0.50% over the general public
rate for every tenure listed above, EXCEPT the 3-years-1-day to
under-4-years-7-months band, where the senior premium is 0.60% (6.50%
general / 7.10% senior) — confirmed against HDFC's own live page, not
a typo.

Note: this is an unusually finely-segmented tenure table compared to
most other banks — several bands are only a few weeks or months wide
(e.g. "2 years 11 months 1 day to 3 years" is its own distinct slab
from the surrounding bands). Always quote the exact published rate for
the requested tenure rather than rounding to a nearby band.

HDFC Bank's rate page also separately lists a bulk-deposit tier (Rs. 3
crore to Rs. 5 crore) with its own rate table — this document covers
only the retail (below Rs. 3 crore) tier.

Rates are revised periodically; verify the current card rate with the
bank before quoting for a large deposit.
