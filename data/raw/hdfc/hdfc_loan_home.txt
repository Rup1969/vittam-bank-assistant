HDFC Bank Home Loan

HDFC Bank's home loan interest rate currently starts from around 7.75%
to 8.15% per annum for well-qualified applicants, with a broader
reported range of roughly 7.75%-13.20% p.a. depending on credit
profile, loan amount, and employment type.

Loan Types:
- Standard floating-rate (adjustable-rate) home loan.
- TruFixed Loan: interest rate stays fixed for an initial period (e.g.
  the first two years of the tenure), then converts to a floating
  rate for the remainder of the loan.

Special Category Rate:
A dedicated home loan scheme for government employees is reported at a
notably preferential 6.90% p.a., with repayment tenure of 5 to 20
years — a meaningfully lower rate than the general-public range,
reflecting the lower credit risk of a government-salaried applicant
base.

This is a headline reference — confirm the current rate, applicable
loan type, and the applicant's specific quoted spread with the bank
before quoting.
