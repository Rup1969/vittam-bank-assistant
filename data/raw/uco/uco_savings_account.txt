UCO Bank Savings Account

UCO Bank's savings account interest rate is 2.50% p.a., flat across all
balance tiers (effective 26.08.2025).

Minimum Average Quarterly Balance (AQB), by location — with a cheque
book:
- Metro/Urban/Semi-Urban: Rs. 2,000.
- Rural: Rs. 500.

Without a cheque book:
- Metro/Urban/Semi-Urban: Rs. 1,000.
- Rural: Rs. 250.

Penalty for shortfall: Rs. 50 + GST per quarter (shortfall up to
Rs. 500) up to Rs. 150 + GST per quarter (shortfall of Rs. 1,001-2,000).

UCO Bank also offers specialized zero-balance and product-specific
variants (referenced elsewhere on the site, not detailed here): UCO GIG
(for gig workers, zero AQB), UCO Tierised, UCO Asha Pension, UCO Suvidha
Salary Account, UCO Rising Star (for minors), UCO Aparajita (for women),
and Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA).
