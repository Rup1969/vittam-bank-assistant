UCO Bank Car Loan (UCO Car)

UCO Bank's car loan interest rate ranges from 7.70% to 10.25% p.a.
(UCO Float benchmark 8.30%, final rate depending on credit score).
Borrowers with a CIBIL score of 825+ get the best rate: 0% spread with
a -0.60% discount, landing at 7.70%.

Eligibility:
Age 21-70 years.

Loan Amount:
- New car: no upper cap.
- Pre-used car (up to 3 years old): no upper cap, subject to the lower
  of 75% of the OEM-backed certified dealer price or 75% of valuation/
  market price; other purchases capped at 70% of agreed price or
  approved-assessor valuation.
- Reimbursement (new car): no upper cap.

Margin (Down Payment):
- New car: minimum 20%.
- Pre-used car: 20% (dealer-sourced) or 30% (other sources).
- Reimbursement: 25% (vehicle up to 3 months old) or 40% (3-6 months
  old).

Tenure:
- New car: maximum 84 months.
- Pre-used car: maximum 60 months.
- Reimbursement: maximum 60 months.

Processing Fee:
Not stated as a specific figure on this product page.
