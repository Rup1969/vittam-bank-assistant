UCO Bank Fixed Deposit (FD) Interest Rates

UCO Bank's current fixed deposit (FD) interest rate for the general
public ranges from 2.90% to 6.60% p.a. depending on tenure. The 1-year
FD interest rate is 6.10% for the general public.

year (over the general rate for the matching tenure).

Note: several tenures (333 days, 444 days, 535 days, and the "Green
Deposit" tenures at 1000/2000/3000 days) are named special products
paying a distinctly HIGHER rate than the standard slab immediately
surrounding them — e.g. 444 days pays 6.45% while both 445-534 days and
536 days-2 years pay only 6.10%. These are real, separately published
special-tenor rates, not a data error.
