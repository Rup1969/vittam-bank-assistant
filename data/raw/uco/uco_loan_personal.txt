UCO Bank Personal Loan (UCO Cash)

UCO Bank's personal loan interest rate ranges from 10.20% to 13.45%
p.a. (UCO Float benchmark 8.30%, final rate depending on employment
type and CIBIL score). Government employees with a CIBIL score of 800+
get the best rate: a 1.90% spread, landing at 10.20%.

Eligibility and Loan Amount:
- Central/State Government employees WITH a salary account at UCO
  Bank: up to 20 times gross monthly income, capped at Rs. 20 lakh.
- Government employees WITHOUT a salary account at UCO Bank: up to 5
  times gross monthly income, capped at Rs. 5 lakh.
- Other salaried employees: up to 10 times gross monthly income, capped
  at Rs. 10 lakh.
- Non-salaried individuals: up to 5 times gross monthly income, capped
  at Rs. 5 lakh.

Processing Fee:
Nil for UCO Cash personal loans.

Tenure:
Not specified for the standard scheme on this page; the related
"Shopper Loan" variant caps at 60 months.
