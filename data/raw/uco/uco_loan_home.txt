UCO Bank Home Loan (UCO Home)

UCO Bank's home loan interest rate ranges from 7.40% to 9.50% p.a.
(UCO Float benchmark 5.50% + 2.80% base = 8.30%, with the final rate
depending on CIBIL score). Borrowers with a CIBIL score of 850+ get the
best rate: a 0.05% spread with a -0.95% strategic discount, landing at
7.40%.

Eligibility:
Indian residents with a regular source of income; NRIs and PIOs also
eligible. Minimum age 21 years; maximum age for full repayment is 75
years for all borrower classes.

Loan Amount:
No upper cap for construction, purchase, extension, or takeover loans.
Repair and renovation loans are capped at Rs. 50 lakh.

Margin:
- Up to Rs. 30 lakh: 10%.
- Rs. 30 lakh to Rs. 75 lakh: 20%.
- Above Rs. 75 lakh: 25%.

Tenure:
Maximum 30 years.

Processing/Documentation Charges:
50% waiver for offline sanctions, 100% waiver for STP (straight-through
processing) sanctions — both valid until 31 March 2026.

Other:
Women borrowers get an additional 0.05% rate concession. An interest
concession is also available for takeover (balance-transfer) loans.
