UCO Bank Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

UCO Bank's Kisan Credit Card interest rate:
- Up to Rs. 3 lakh: 7%.
- Above Rs. 3 lakh to Rs. 25 lakh: MCLR + 1%.
- Above Rs. 25 lakh to Rs. 1 crore: MCLR + 2%.

Purpose:
Meets farmers' short-term credit needs for crop cultivation, post-
harvest expenses, produce marketing, household consumption, and
maintenance of farm assets.

Eligibility:
Individual or joint-borrower owner cultivators, tenant farmers, oral
lessees, sharecroppers, and Self-Help Groups (SHGs) or Joint Liability
Groups (JLGs) of farmers.

Loan Amount:
Need-based finance, considering cropping pattern, acreage, and the
Scale of Finance (SOF) determined by the District Level Technical
Committee (DLTC).

Margin:
Nil.

Repayment:
5 years, with a 10% annual increase in the sanctioned limit, subject to
annual review.

Security:
Primary security is hypothecation of crops. No collateral required for
limits up to Rs. 2 lakh, or up to Rs. 3 lakh under a recovery tie-up
arrangement.

Incentives:
Interest subvention of 1.5% (up to Rs. 3 lakh limit), plus a Prompt
Repayment Incentive of 3% (also up to Rs. 3 lakh limit) — together
these can lower the effective rate well below the headline 7%.
