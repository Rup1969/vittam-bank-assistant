UCO Bank Digital Banking Products

UCO Bank's UPI app is called BHIM UCO UPI. It works with any bank's
customers, not just UCO account holders.

Key Features:
- Direct bank payments using a UPI ID or by scanning a QR code.
- Virtual Payment Address (VPA) creation, format name@uco.
- Send and request payments; QR code scanning and generation.
- Merchant payments without revealing account details.
- Balance inquiry.
- Two-factor authentication and 12-digit transaction reference tracking
  for security.

Daily Transaction Limits:
Person-to-person transfers up to Rs. 1,00,000; merchant payments up to
Rs. 2,00,000.

Available on Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

UCO Bank also offers "UCO Bank mBanking Plus" as a mobile banking app,
referenced in the bank's navigation but without a dedicated feature
description page found in this pass.
