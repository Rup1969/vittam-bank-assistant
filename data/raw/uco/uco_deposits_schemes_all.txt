UCO Bank — Recurring Deposit and Special Schemes

Standard Recurring Deposit:
Minimum installment Rs. 100. Tenure from 1 year to 10 years. Interest
rates match the bank's term-deposit rate table (effective 01.04.2026),
ranging from 2.90% (7-14 days equivalent) to 6.94% (above 5 years).

UCO Sowbhagya (Flexi Recurring Deposit):
Minimum Rs. 100 per month, thereafter in multiples of Rs. 100. Tenure:
minimum 12 months, maximum 60 months (5 years).

UCO Sanchayika (women-focused recurring deposit):
Minimum Rs. 2,000, thereafter in multiples of Rs. 500. Tenure: minimum
12 months, maximum 60 months.
