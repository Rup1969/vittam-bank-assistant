UCO Bank Gold Loan

UCO Bank's gold loan interest rate is 8.70% p.a. (UCO Float benchmark
8.30% + 0.40% spread, at 30% margin).

Eligibility:
All new/existing individual customers who own gold ornaments, jewelry,
or coins.

Loan-to-Value (LTV):
Up to 70% of current gold market value for personal/consumption
purposes; up to 85% for agriculture/allied activities.

Processing Fees:
- Priority-sector advances up to Rs. 50,000: nil.
- Non-priority advances up to Rs. 25,000: nil.
- Non-priority, Rs. 25,000-50,000: Rs. 250.
- Rs. 50,000-1 lakh: Rs. 250.
- Above Rs. 1 lakh to Rs. 2 lakh: Rs. 350.
- Above Rs. 5 lakh: 0.25%, maximum Rs. 4,000.
Additional appraisal fee Rs. 200-1,000 (varies by location) plus stamp
duty at actuals.

Loan Amount and Tenure:
Not stated as specific figures on this product page.
