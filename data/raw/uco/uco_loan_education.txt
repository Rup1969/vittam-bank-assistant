UCO Bank Education Loan (UCO Udaan)

UCO Bank's education loan interest rate ranges from 7.50% to 10.65%
p.a. (UCO Float benchmark 8.30%, depending on institute category and
collateral). List A institutes with 100% collateral get the best rate:
a -0.80% spread, landing at 7.50%.

UCO Udaan is specifically positioned for meritorious female students at
premier educational institutes.

Eligibility:
Indian nationals who have secured admission through an entrance test or
selection process. No minimum or maximum age criteria.

Loan Amount:
Need-based, determined by actual educational expenses.

Margin:
Nil up to Rs. 30 lakh; 15% above Rs. 30 lakh.

Collateral:
No security required, only a parent/spouse/guardian as co-borrower, for
loans up to Rs. 30 lakh at List A premier institutes. For higher
amounts or lower-category institutions, tangible collateral equal to
100% of the loan value is required along with a co-borrower.

Repayment:
Maximum 180 EMIs (15 years). Moratorium: course period + 1 year.

Fees:
Processing and documentation charges: nil.
