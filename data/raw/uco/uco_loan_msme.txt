UCO Bank MSME / Business Loan

UCO Bank's MSME loan interest rate ranges from 9.05% to 12.45% p.a.
(UCO Float benchmark 8.30%, or RLLR 8.05% as of 11.12.2025, depending on
exposure size and credit rating). The best rate — advances up to
Rs. 50,000 — carries a 1.00% spread, landing at 9.05%.

The bank's MSME business-loan page itself is a classification/
eligibility explainer rather than a product-terms page: it covers the
revised Ministry of MSME classification (Micro: investment up to Rs. 1
crore; Small: up to Rs. 10 crore; Medium: up to Rs. 50 crore, with
matching turnover limits) and Udyam Registration requirements for
manufacturing and services enterprises.

This is a summary-level entry only — specific loan-amount ranges,
collateral requirements, and CGTMSE guarantee coverage details are not
published inline on this page; confirm with the bank (toll-free
1800 8910) before relying on any figure beyond the rate range above.
