IndusInd Bank Home Loan

IndusInd Bank's home loan interest rate is linked to its external
Benchmark Rate (currently 5.25% per the official page), with the
customer's final rate depending on profile, collateral, and credit
score. Secondary sources report a fairly wide range for the actual
starting/applicable rate — figures seen include starting rates from
7.50% up to 8.25%-8.35%, with the broader applicable range commonly
cited as roughly 8.25%-10.00% p.a. (some sources cite a wider 8%-15%
band depending on the applicant's profile and prevailing base rate).
A credit score of 750 or above is generally needed to secure the
lower end of the range.

Rate Type:
Floating rate, linked to the external Benchmark Rate — reset in
response to RBI repo rate changes.

Tenure:
Up to 30 years.

Key Features:
- Wide range of home loan products (purchase, balance transfer, top-up,
  plot + construction, NRI home loans, renovation/extension)
- Doorstep service, simplified documentation
- No prepayment charges on floating-rate loans
- Access to a pre-approved project inventory

Eligibility (age / experience):
- Salaried (govt./reputed companies): 24-60 years, 3 years experience
- Self-employed professionals (Doctor/Engineer/CA/CS): 24-70 years, 3 years
- Self-employed non-professionals (ITR filers): 24-65 years, 3 years

Rate Reset Protection (per RBI Aug 2023 notification):
If the benchmark rate rises, tenure is extended first (EMI held
constant) up to a maximum of 60 months beyond the original sanctioned
tenure, with EMI increased only after that cap is reached; borrower age
at maturity may not exceed 80 years as a result of such elongation.

This is a headline reference only — the bank's own page does not
display a fixed numeric starting rate, and secondary sources disagree
on the exact figure (7.50% to 8.35% starting, depending on source and
date). Confirm the current rate and the applicant's specific quoted
spread with the bank before quoting.
