IndusInd Bank MSME / Business Loan

IndusInd Bank's MSME loan interest rates range from approximately 18%
to 26% per annum for unsecured MSME lending. Its broader unsecured
business loan product (which may overlap with the MSME offering) is
reported at a lower fixed range of roughly 13% to 22% per annum,
depending on the specific loan scheme and applicant risk profile.

Key Features:
- Maximum loan amount: up to Rs. 50 lakh
- Repayment tenure: up to 4 years
- Unsecured lending, primarily targeting rural, semi-urban, and urban
  MSME customers
- Rate depends on business profitability, turnover, credit score, and
  internal credit policy

This is a headline reference from secondary sources, not the bank's
own published rate card — MSME/business loan pricing at IndusInd is
individually risk-assessed. Confirm the current applicable rate and
scheme-specific terms with the bank before quoting.
