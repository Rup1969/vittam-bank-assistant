IndusInd Bank Savings Account — Minimum Balance and Interest Rate

IndusInd Bank offers a Zero Balance savings account variant (Indus
Delite) with no minimum balance requirement, alongside multiple premium
variants (Indus Select, Indus Exclusive, Indus Grandé, PIONEER) that
require maintaining a minimum balance — exact rupee figures for the
premium tiers should be confirmed on each variant's own product page,
since IndusInd's main savings account page describes their features and
benefits but does not list every variant's specific balance requirement
in one place.

Confirmed account variants (from the official page):
- Indus Delite (Zero Balance Savings Account): "Zero balance requirement
  subject to IP funding" — the bank's own wording for this zero-balance
  variant.
- Indus Select Savings Account: premium variant with lifetime-free
  World/Signature Select debit card and lifestyle privileges (exact MAB
  not stated on the main page).
- Indus Exclusive, Indus Grandé, Indus Grandé Care, Indus Care, PIONEER:
  premium/relationship-manager-backed tiers with escalating privileges
  (airport lounge access, dedicated RM, family banking) — exact MAB not
  stated on the main page for these either.
- Indus Multiplier Max: standard savings account with an auto-sweep
  feature that converts balance above Rs. 20,000 into a fixed deposit.

Savings Account Interest Rates (effective 1 March 2026, domestic/
NRO/NRE, daily balance basis):
- Up to Rs. 1 lakh: 2.50% p.a.
- Above Rs. 1 lakh up to Rs. 25 lakh: 3.00% p.a.
- Above Rs. 25 lakh up to Rs. 5 crore: 4.00% p.a.
- Above Rs. 5 crore up to Rs. 100 crore: 5.00% p.a. (flat rate on entire balance)
- Above Rs. 100 crore up to Rs. 150 crore: 7.05% p.a. (flat rate)
- Above Rs. 150 crore up to Rs. 1,400 crore: 7.00% p.a. (flat rate)
- Above Rs. 1,400 crore: MIBOR-linked floating rate (effective 27 March
  2026), calculated daily and paid monthly.

Interest is calculated daily on End-of-Day balance and credited
quarterly. A general AMB formula is published: AMB = Total of Everyday
Closing Balance / Total Number of Days in the Month.

For the exact minimum-balance figure of a specific non-zero-balance
variant (Select, Exclusive, Grandé, etc.), consult that account's
dedicated product page — not listed uniformly on the main savings page.

Rates and minimum-balance requirements are revised periodically; verify
against IndusInd Bank's official website before quoting to a customer as
final.
