IndusInd Bank — Deposit Scheme Variants (Recurring Deposit)

IndusInd Bank's Recurring Deposit (RD) lets customers build savings
through regular monthly instalments starting from a minimum of Rs.
1,000, with a tenure of 12 to 120 months and a rate that tracks the
bank's term deposit rate (including the senior citizen premium).

Recurring Deposit — Key Terms:
- Minimum investment: Rs. 1,000 (may vary by booking channel).
- Maximum deposit: Rs. 3 crore.
- Tenure: 12 to 120 months.
- Booking increments: multiples of Rs. 100 only.
- Interest rate: same as the equivalent-tenure term deposit rate
  (including senior citizen benefit) — see indusind_fd_rates.txt for
  the current tenure-wise rate table.
- TDS on interest: per current income tax regulations.
- Nomination facility: available.
- Premature closure: allowed.
- Funding method: fixed monthly instalment via standing order, ECS
  mandate, or regular direct remittance.
- Opening: at a branch, via the deposit account form.

Tax Saver Fixed Deposit: IndusInd Bank's 5-year Tax Saver FD is one of
the standard retail FD tenure bands (not a separate product page) —
see the "Tax Saver (5 years)" row in indusind_fd_rates.txt for its
current rate, which qualifies for a Section 80C tax deduction up to
Rs. 1.5 lakh, with a mandatory 5-year lock-in (no premature
withdrawal).

Verify current RD and Tax Saver FD rates on the bank's live rate page
before quoting to a customer, since these are revised periodically.
