IndusInd Bank Kisan Credit Card (KCC) — via Indus Kisan

Yes — IndusInd Bank offers a Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme, provided
under its "Indus Kisan" agricultural lending program: a revolving
credit facility for farmers' crop production and allied agricultural
needs, with KCC/crop loans up to Rs. 3 lakh for Small and Marginal
Farmers carrying NIL processing fees.

IndusInd Bank's agriculture loan interest rates for Q1 FY 2026-27
range from 7.00% to 15.50% per annum depending on the facility type,
offered under "Indus Kisan," a direct agri-funding product for
progressive Indian farmers covering both short-term (crop/working
capital) and long-term (investment) credit needs through a single
window.

Rate table by facility type (Q1 FY27, fixed ROI effective since
Sept'24):
- Production Loan (Cash Credit): 7.00% - 13.25% p.a. (mean 11.18%)
- Investment Loan (Term Loan): 9.10% - 10.25% p.a. (mean 9.74%)
- Overdraft Loan: 9.00% - 15.50% p.a. (mean 11.12%)

Revised Annual Percentage Rate (APR) for Q1 FY27:
- Term Loan: ROI 9.10%-10.25%, APR 9.35%-10.63%
- Cash Credit/Overdraft: ROI 7.00%-15.50%, APR 7.00%-16.65%

Product Features:
- Crop Loan: working capital for agri and agri-allied activities
- Investment Loan: long-term financing (e.g. pump sets, sprayers)
- Tenure: up to 5 years
- Bundled savings bank account, RuPay Debit Card, and internet banking
- Flexible repayment cycle aligned to crop harvest cycle
- Crop insurance facility for notified crops; Health/Life insurance also available

Availability:
Indus Kisan is presently available only at select branches in
Maharashtra, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Punjab, Haryana,
Rajasthan, and Kerala — not a pan-India product.

Charges:
- Processing Fee (fresh/renewal): up to 2% of sanctioned amount + GST
  (NIL for KCC/Crop loans up to Rs. 3 lakh for Small/Marginal Farmers)
- Penal charge: Term Loans 2% + GST per month on overdue amount (or
  Rs. 100 + GST, whichever is higher); CC/OD 24% p.a. + GST on overdue
  amount (or Rs. 100 + GST, whichever is higher)
- Prepayment/foreclosure: NIL for floating-rate loans; fixed-rate
  loans carry a 4% charge if closed within 3 years of first disbursement

Rates are periodically revised (this table is specifically for Q1
FY27); confirm the current rate and branch availability with the bank
before quoting.
