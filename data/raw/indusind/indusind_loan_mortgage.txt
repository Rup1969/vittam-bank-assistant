IndusInd Bank Loan Against Property (LAP) / Mortgage Loan

IndusInd Bank's Loan Against Property interest rate for Q1 FY27 ranges
from 8.00% to 12.50% Rate of Interest (average 9.65%), with APR ranging
from 8.00% to 16.71% (APR figures are calculated for a 10-year tenure;
actual APR varies by the specific rate and tenure taken).

Rate Table — Q1 FY27 (per official page):
- Bank ROI: Min 8.00%, Max 12.50%, Average 9.65%
- APR: Min 8.00%, Max 16.71%

Rates are linked to the External Benchmark Rate, currently 5.25%. The
bank offers attractive rates based on customer profile and the type of
collateral offered.

Rate Reset Protection (per RBI Aug 2023 circular, applicable to
individual borrowers taking LAP for personal purposes — non-individual
or business-purpose borrowers are NOT covered by this circular):
If the benchmark rate rises, the loan tenure is extended first (EMI
held constant) rather than immediately raising the EMI.

Credit Decision Turnaround Time (TAT):
- Loans up to Rs. 25 lakh (to MSME borrower units): not more than 14
  working days.
- Loans above Rs. 25 lakh (to MSME borrower units): 45 working days.
(Subject to complete documentation being submitted by the customer.)

Documentation includes: latest 6 months' bank statement of the main
CA/SA/CC/OD account, copies of all property documents for the property
to be mortgaged, and a processing fee cheque.

Rates and the benchmark are revised periodically; verify the current
Q1 FY27 (or latest quarter's) rate table with the bank before quoting
to a customer.
