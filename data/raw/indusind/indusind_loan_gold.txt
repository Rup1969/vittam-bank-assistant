IndusInd Bank Gold Loan

IndusInd Bank's gold loan interest rates range from approximately
10.06% to 15.82% per annum (secondary sources also cite a "starting"
figure around 10.38%-13.50% depending on the snapshot/date), applied
against gold jewellery pledged as collateral.

Key Features:
- Maximum loan amount: up to Rs. 10 lakh
- Tenure: 3 to 24 months
- Minimum documentation, instant disbursal
- No proof of income required for loans up to Rs. 10 lakh
- Processing fee: 1% of the loan amount
- Additional interest discounts available for women borrowers and
  those in the agricultural profession

Eligibility:
Minimum age 18 years, maximum age 70 years.

This is a headline reference — secondary sources show a wide reported
range (10.06% to 15.82%) rather than one single confirmed rate.
Confirm the current applicable rate with the bank before quoting, as
gold loan pricing also depends on gold purity/weight and prevailing
gold price at the time of the loan.
