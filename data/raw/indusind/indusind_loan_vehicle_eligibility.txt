IndusInd Bank Car / Vehicle Loan — Features, Eligibility, Documentation

IndusInd Bank's car loan (new car loan / vehicle loan) offers new car
loan, used car loan, and two-wheeler loan variants.

Key Features:
- Car loan / new car loan financing up to 100% of new car value (varies
  with vehicle cost and credit parameters).
- Repayment tenure: 1-5 years, extendable up to 7 years in some cases
  (elsewhere on the page also stated as "up to 60 months").
- Minimum credit score required: above 600.

Eligibility (by applicant type):
- Salaried Individuals: minimum income Rs. 2.5 lakh p.a.; age 23-60
  years; minimum 3 years' employment stability, at least 1 year in
  current job; residence stability 2 years in city, 1 year at current
  address (waived if own house).
- Self-Employed Individuals: minimum income Rs. 3 lakh p.a. (per latest
  ITR); age 23-65 years; minimum 3 years' business stability.
- Self-Employed — No Income Proof: 3 years' business stability; age
  23-65 years; own residence stability required.
- Self-Employed, Commercial Use (FTU): minimum 3 years' valid driving
  license with badge; age 25-60 years; mandatory guarantor with 12+
  months of commercial driving experience.

Documentation (salaried): latest Form 16/ITR, last 3 months' salary
slips, 3 months' bank statements.

Contact for loan queries: 1860 267 7777 (or 022 6857 7777 for Two
Wheeler loans specifically); email reach4loan@indusind.com.

This is a headline reference — since no fixed rate is published (rate
is set per applicant profile and vehicle at application time), confirm
the current applicable rate with the bank before quoting.
