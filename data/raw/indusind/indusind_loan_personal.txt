IndusInd Bank Personal Loan

IndusInd Bank's personal loan interest rate starts from 10.49% per
annum, determined based on the applicant's creditworthiness, income
stability, and repayment capacity. Customers with strong credit
profiles and consistent repayment histories typically qualify for
rates closer to the starting figure, while others receive relatively
higher pricing based on risk assessment.

Interest Calculation:
Uses the reducing balance method — interest is charged only on the
outstanding loan amount, not the original principal, throughout the
tenure. Early EMIs carry a higher interest component; the principal
component grows over time.

Processing Fee:
Typically 3.5% of the loan amount, reduced to 1.5%-2% during
special/festive promotional offers.

Key Factors Affecting the Rate:
- CIBIL score (higher score, lower rate)
- Income profile and employment stability
- Loan amount and tenure (longer tenures may carry marginally higher rates)
- Existing financial obligations/EMIs
- Existing relationship with IndusInd Bank (may qualify for preferential pricing)

Repayment Tenure:
Flexible tenure of 12 to 48 months.

Rates are risk-based and vary by applicant; confirm the current
starting rate and the applicant's specific quoted rate with the bank
before quoting.
