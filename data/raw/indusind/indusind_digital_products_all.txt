IndusInd Bank — Digital Banking Products

INDIE:
IndusInd Bank's flagship digital-first mobile banking app, aimed at
digitally-savvy customers seeking a fully online experience. Supports
100% digital savings account opening (zero-balance account, no
non-maintenance charges), fixed deposit booking (rates up to 7.85%
p.a. quoted on the app's own microsite — note this differs slightly
from the standard branch/web FD card rate, confirm which applies),
instant loan and credit card applications, and mutual fund investing —
all from one app.

Savings Account via INDIE:
Advertised as offering up to 6.75% p.a. return (a high-interest
savings variant), with zero non-maintenance charges.

IndusPay:
A UPI (Unified Payments Interface) application usable by both
IndusInd Bank customers and non-IndusInd Bank customers alike.

Rewards:
"INDIE Gems" — a rewards program earning points on fund transfers,
bill payments, debit card transactions, UPI transactions via the app,
and recurring payments.

Security Features:
- Numberless debit card (card number not printed on the physical card)
- Single-use virtual card for online transactions
- Dynamic ATM PIN
- "Super OTP" additional authentication layer

Other Digital Channels (per the bank's own navigation/product list):
IndusInd Bank Net Banking, WhatsApp Banking, Video Branch, BHIM
IndusPay, Bharat QR, FASTag, and e-Mandate setup for recurring
payments.

Note: the exact FD/savings rates advertised on INDIE's own microsite
may lag or differ slightly from the bank's main rate-card page
(see indusind_fd_rates.txt for the verified, extraction-cross-checked
FD table) — treat the app-specific figures as promotional headline
numbers and confirm against the main rate card before quoting.
