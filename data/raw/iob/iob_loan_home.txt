Indian Overseas Bank home loan interest rate is linked to RLLR (Repo
Linked Lending Rate), 8.10% as of 15 December 2025. Best-graded
borrowers (CIC-1) get RLLR minus 1.00%, around 7.10%. Reported rates
range 8.00% to 10.50% p.a. depending on CIBIL score and loan amount.
