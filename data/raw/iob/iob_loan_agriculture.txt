Indian Overseas Bank — Kisan Credit Card

Purpose:
A revolving credit facility for farmers' short-term production credit
and allied agricultural needs, per the standard nationwide KCC scheme
that IOB, like other public sector banks, implements.

Security Requirements:
- Existing borrowers with a satisfactory 2-year repayment track record
  at IOB: no collateral required for aggregate short-term production
  loans up to Rs. 3 lakh.
- New borrowers, loans up to Rs. 1 lakh: security via crop
  hypothecation or assets created from the loan.
- New borrowers, loans between Rs. 1 lakh and Rs. 3 lakh: suitable
  collateral may additionally be required (a stricter stance than
  existing-borrower terms above).

Interest Rate:
Not confirmed from public secondary sources for the current period —
IOB participates in the government's Interest Subvention Scheme for
short-term crop loans (like other public sector banks, typically around
7% p.a. effective rate for qualifying loans up to Rs. 3 lakh); confirm
the current effective rate with the bank.

This is a headline reference — confirm the current effective rate and
exact collateral thresholds with the bank or nearest branch.
