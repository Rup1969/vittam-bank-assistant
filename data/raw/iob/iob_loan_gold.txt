Indian Overseas Bank Gold Loan

Interest Rate:
General retail jewel loans reported starting around 8.25% p.a. Certain
agricultural gold loan schemes (for farming-related purposes) are
reported as low as 7% p.a., reflecting government interest-subvention
benefits similar to Kisan Credit Card pricing — these are a distinct,
purpose-restricted product from the general jewel loan, not a rate
available for any gold loan purpose.

Structure:
Loan secured against pledged gold ornaments/jewellery; exact rate and
eligible amount depend on gold purity, weight, and prevailing gold
valuation, and on which specific scheme (general retail vs.
agricultural) the loan is booked under.

This is a headline reference — always confirm the current rate, which
scheme applies, and the loan-to-value ratio with the bank, since gold
loan terms are sensitive to gold price movements and are reviewed
frequently.
