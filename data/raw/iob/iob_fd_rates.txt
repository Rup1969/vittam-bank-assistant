Indian Overseas Bank Fixed (Term) Deposit Interest Rates (below Rs. 3 crore)

Indian Overseas Bank's current fixed deposit (FD) interest rate for the
general public ranges from 2.90% to 6.50% per annum across tenures from
7 days to 3+ years, with senior citizens earning an additional 0.50%
and super senior citizens an additional 0.75% over the general rate.
The 1-year FD interest rate is 6.50% for the general public and 7.00%
for senior citizens — IOB's highest published slab on the standard
retail table.

Effective 15 December 2025, domestic retail term deposit rates by tenure
(general public / senior citizen / super senior citizen):
- 7-14 days: 3.50% / 4.00% / 4.25%
- 15-29 days: 3.50% / 4.00% / 4.25%
- 30-45 days: 3.50% / 4.00% / 4.25%
- 46-60 days: 4.00% / 4.50% / 4.75%
- 61-90 days: 3.00% / 3.50% / 3.75%
- 91-120 days: 3.50% / 4.00% / 4.25%
- 121-179 days: 2.90% / 3.40% / 3.65%
- 180-269 days: 4.75% / 5.25% / 5.50%
- 270 days to less than 1 year: 5.50% / 6.00% / 6.25%
- 1 year: 6.50% / 7.00% / 7.25%
- Above 1 year to less than 2 years: 6.40% / 6.90% / 7.15%
- 2 years to less than 3 years: 6.40% / 6.90% / 7.15%
- 3 years and above: 6.10% / 6.60% / 6.85%

Note: the 61-90 day and 121-179 day bands are genuinely priced LOWER
than the shorter bands immediately before them on the bank's own
published table (not a transcription error) — always quote the exact
published rate for the requested tenure rather than assuming rates
increase steadily with tenure.

Senior Citizen Premium: an additional 0.50% for ages 60+, and a further
0.75% for ages 80+ (super senior citizens), over the general public
rate.

Minimum Deposit: Rs. 1 lakh stated for the 7-14 day tenure on the
bank's published table; other tenures may have different minimums —
confirm with the bank.

444-Day Special FD: reported by third-party aggregators at around
6.60% p.a. general public — a special-tenure scheme distinct from the
standard table above; confirm the current rate with the bank.

Rates are revised periodically; verify the current card rate with the
bank before quoting for a large deposit.
