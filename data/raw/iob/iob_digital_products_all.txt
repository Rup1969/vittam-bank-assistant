Indian Overseas Bank — Digital Banking Products

IOB Connect (also listed as "IOBMobile" in some app stores):
IOB's mobile banking app (Android/iOS) — fund transfers via UPI, NEFT,
RTGS, and IMPS, balance enquiries, card management, and bill payments
covering electricity, telecom, DTH, gas, water, insurance premiums,
mutual funds, and school/institution fees. "IOB Connect" is described
as a redesigned, enhanced successor to the bank's earlier mobile app.

BHIM IOB UPI:
A dedicated UPI app — lets customers create a Virtual Payment Address
(VPA) to link their bank account and make/receive payments without
sharing full account details. UPI transfers are capped at Rs. 1,00,000
per 24 hours per the bank's stated limit.

Confirm current app names (the IOBMobile-to-IOB-Connect naming may
still be in transition across app stores) and UPI limits with the bank,
since transaction limits are periodically revised by NPCI/RBI guidance
as well as bank policy.
