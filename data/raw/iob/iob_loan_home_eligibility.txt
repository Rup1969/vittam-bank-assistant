Indian Overseas Bank Home Loan — Rate Structure

Interest Rate detail:
The bank adds its own spread (margin) to RLLR, and the final rate
resets periodically with RBI repo-rate changes.

Structure:
Floating-rate loan — an RBI repo-rate cut typically reduces either the
EMI or the tenure, and vice versa for a rate increase.

This is a headline, RLLR-linked reference — confirm the current RLLR
value and the applicant's specific quoted spread with the bank before
quoting.
