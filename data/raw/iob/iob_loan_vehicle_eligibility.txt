Indian Overseas Bank Vehicle Loans-Pushpaka — Scheme Coverage, Loan Amount, Eligibility

Scheme Coverage:
Branded "Vehicle Loans-Pushpaka" — covers new cars, old/used cars, and
two-wheelers under one product family.

Loan Amount:
Up to 90% of the vehicle's cost for new cars.

Eligibility:
Applicant must be in confirmed service with take-home pay above 50% of
gross salary (after PF/insurance deductions); minimum total monthly
income of Rs. 8,000 for car loans and Rs. 5,000 for two-wheeler loans.

This is a headline reference — confirm the current rate and eligibility
thresholds with the bank before quoting.
