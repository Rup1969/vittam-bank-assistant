Indian Overseas Bank Education Loan

Coverage:
IOB offers education loans covering a range of courses, including
technical/vocational courses at recognised training institutes and
polytechnics, alongside standard degree-course education loans that
most public sector banks offer under IBA's common education-loan-scheme
framework.

Interest Rate:
IOB's education loan rates range approximately 7.50%-11.75% p.a. across
its various schemes — per IOB's own published rate card. Representative
rates: Vidya Shresht for A-rated institutions 7.50% (the lowest
published rate); Vidya Jyothi for the general public 9.75%; IOB Scholar
above Rs. 7.50 lakh up to Rs. 60 lakh 11.30%; IOB Career Dream secured
by immovable property, above Rs. 2 lakh, 11.75% (the highest published
rate). Typical public-sector-bank structure applies (moratorium of
course duration plus one year, repayment tenure up to 15 years,
collateral required above a certain loan threshold, per the pattern
seen across other banks in this dataset) — confirm the exact scheme,
rate, and terms applicable to a specific applicant with the bank.
