Indian Overseas Bank MSME Loans

Collateral-Free Lending:
IOB's MSME Term Loan offers collateral-free credit up to Rs. 10 lakh
for businesses seeking financial assistance for expansion and growth-
related activities — in line with CGTMSE-backed collateral-free lending
norms most public sector banks, including IOB, follow for smaller MSME
loan amounts.

Policy Context:
The bank publishes a consolidated "MSME Credit Schemes at a Glance"
reference covering its various MSME product lines — specific
product-level interest rates were not found in public secondary sources
during this research pass and should be confirmed directly with the
bank.

This is a partial reference — confirm current MSME product names,
interest rates, and collateral-free thresholds above Rs. 10 lakh with
the bank before advising a customer.
