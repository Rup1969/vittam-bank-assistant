Yes, Indian Overseas Bank offers a Loan Against Property (mortgage
loan), interest rate RLLR + 0.50% (approximately 9.85% p.a. per the
bank's own FAQ page). Third-party aggregators report a somewhat wider
9.90%-10.90% p.a. range — the bank's own RLLR-linked formula should be
treated as more authoritative; confirm the current RLLR value with
the bank.

Loan Amount & Tenure:
Up to 50% of the property's market value; maximum tenure 7 years —
notably shorter than several peer banks' loan-against-property products
(some offer 15 years), worth flagging to a customer comparing options.

Security:
Equitable mortgage of the property, or alternatively liquid assets such
as LIC policies, IVPs, NSCs, or UTI units.

Processing Charges:
0.60% of the loan amount for loans up to Rs. 5 lakh; 0.75% of the loan
amount for loans above Rs. 5 lakh.

This is a headline reference — confirm the current RLLR value and the
applicant's specific quoted spread with the bank before quoting.
