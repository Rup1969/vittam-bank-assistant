Indian Overseas Bank — Deposit Scheme Variants

IOB Tax Saver Deposit:
A 5-year lock-in tax-saving fixed deposit qualifying for a Section 80C
deduction — reported interest rate around 6.10% p.a. for the general
public, plus the standard senior-citizen (+0.50%) and super-senior
(+0.75%) premiums.

Floating Rate Deposits:
A term deposit whose interest rate is market-linked (floats with a
reference benchmark) rather than fixed for the full tenure — positioned
for customers who want to capture rate increases during the deposit
term rather than lock in today's rate.

IOB Non-Callable Retail Term Deposit:
A non-callable variant (no premature withdrawal) offered as a rate
premium over the standard callable retail table.

Flexi Recurring Deposit (Flexi RD):
A recurring deposit allowing variable monthly instalment amounts within
a set range, rather than a fixed instalment every month — minimum
tenure 12 months, maximum 120 months.

Bulk Deposit:
A separate rate card applies for deposits above the retail (below
Rs. 3 crore) threshold covered in `iob_fd_rates` — confirm the bulk
deposit rate card with the bank for large-value deposits.

This is a summary of publicly listed IOB deposit scheme names — exact
current rates, minimum amounts, and eligibility should be confirmed
with the bank before advising a customer.
