Indian Overseas Bank Personal Loan

Interest Rate:
Reported starting rate varies by source — 10.25% p.a. (as of 27 January
2026) from one aggregator, 10.50% p.a. from another, with a broader
reported range up to 13.65% p.a. depending on employment profile and
credit score. Given the source disagreement even on the starting rate,
confirm the applicant's actual quoted rate directly with the bank
rather than relying on a single headline figure.

This is a headline reference — personal loan pricing is highly

and eligibility with the bank.
