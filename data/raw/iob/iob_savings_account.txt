Indian Overseas Bank Savings Account — Minimum Balance Requirements

Indian Overseas Bank's savings account minimum balance ranges from zero
(Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account) to Rs. 2,000 (Regular Savings
Account with cheque book, metro/urban), tracked as a Quarterly Average
Balance (QAB) rather than a monthly figure.

Regular SB Account — Quarterly Average Balance requirement (confirmed
directly from IOB's official page):

Accounts WITH cheque book:
- Metro & Urban: Rs. 2,000
- Rural & Semi Urban: Rs. 500
- Pensioners: Rs. 250

Accounts WITHOUT cheque book:
- Metro & Urban: Rs. 500
- Rural & Semi Urban: Rs. 100
- Pensioners: Rs. 5

Charges for non-maintenance of minimum balance: currently WAIVED, with
effect from 01.10.2025 (per IOB's official page).

Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA): "This account shall not have
the requirement of any minimum balance." Available to all as a normal
banking service. Maximum 4 withdrawals per month (including ATM),
unlimited deposits, free ATM/debit card, no charges for the account or
for non-operation of an inoperative account.

Regular SB Account eligibility: individuals, joint accounts, clubs,
associations, trusts, government bodies, societies (including
co-operatives), educational institutions; minors aged 10 and above can
apply in their own name.

IOB also offers several other savings account variants (IOB Freedom
Savings Account, SB MAX, SB Super Star, SB HNI, Ultra-HNI SB Scheme,
IOB Insta Digital Savings Account, IOB Eco Savings Account, IOB Parivaar,
Government and Institutional Savings Account) — each may carry a
different minimum-balance requirement; check the specific scheme's page
before quoting.

Verify current minimum balance requirements and the non-maintenance
charge waiver status against IOB's official website before quoting to a
customer as final, since these are periodically revised.
