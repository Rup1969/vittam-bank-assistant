RBI Complaint Process — How To File a Complaint

Two Distinct Complaint Channels:

1. Complaint AGAINST a Regulated Entity (bank, NBFC, or System
   Participant):
   File online through RBI's Complaint Management System (CMS) at
   cms.rbi.org.in. This is the correct channel for a customer's
   grievance against their own bank or any other RBI-regulated entity
   (deficient service, unresolved dispute, etc.) — this is also the
   intake point that feeds into the Reserve Bank - Integrated
   Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS) process (see rbi_grievance_ombudsman_2026
   for the substantive scheme rules — no monetary cap, escalation
   timelines, etc.).

2. Complaint AGAINST RBI Itself:
   For a grievance about an RBI department directly (not about a bank
   or other regulated entity), email crpc@rbi.org.in with: name,
   address, the specific RBI department concerned, and supporting
   documents.

Key Distinction To Get Right:
A customer complaint about their OWN BANK's service should go through
CMS (cms.rbi.org.in) and, if unresolved, escalates under the
Ombudsman Scheme — NOT to crpc@rbi.org.in, which is only for
complaints about RBI's own departments/conduct.

This is a process/channel reference — the substantive rules for what
qualifies, escalation timelines, and monetary limits are covered
separately in the Ombudsman Scheme documents, not repeated here.
