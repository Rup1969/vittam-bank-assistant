RBI Grievance Redressal — Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021 (RB-IOS 2021) [SUPERSEDED]

NOTE: This scheme was replaced by RB-IOS 2026, effective 1 July 2026. This
document is kept only as historical/reference context — do not present
this as the current scheme.

RB-IOS 2021 was launched in November 2021, unifying three earlier RBI
ombudsman schemes into a single "One Nation, One Ombudsman" framework. It
provided a cost-free, expeditious grievance redress mechanism for
customers of RBI-regulated entities.

In October 2025, coverage was extended to state and central co-operative
banks, and non-scheduled primary (urban) co-operative banks with deposits
of Rs. 50 crore or more, alongside existing coverage of commercial banks,
regional rural banks, and most NBFCs.

Under RB-IOS 2021, the scheme had different (lower) compensation limits
and procedural rules than the current 2026 version — for exact current
figures, refer to the RB-IOS 2026 document, not this one.
