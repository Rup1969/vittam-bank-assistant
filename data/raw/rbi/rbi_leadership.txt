RBI Leadership — Governor and Deputy Governors

Governor:
Shri Sanjay Malhotra took charge as Governor of the Reserve Bank of
India on 11 December 2024, appointed for a 3-year term. He is the 26th
Governor of RBI (the 15th to come from the civil services), and
succeeded Shaktikanta Das, who completed a 6-year term. Sanjay Malhotra
is a 1990-batch IAS officer of the Rajasthan cadre, and was Revenue
Secretary in the Ministry of Finance immediately before this
appointment.

Deputy Governors (as listed on RBI's official Deputy Governors page,
current as of the dates below):
- Shri Rohit Jain — took charge 4 May 2026.
- Shri Shirish Chandra Murmu — took charge 9 October 2025.
- Dr. Poonam Gupta — took charge 2 May 2025.
- Shri Swaminathan J — RBI's page listed his term as extending to
  25 June 2026 at last check.

IMPORTANT — verify before quoting: Deputy Governor terms are fixed and
this is exactly the kind of fact that goes stale. Shri Swaminathan J's
listed term end-date (25 June 2026) may already have passed depending
on today's date — there may be a new 4th Deputy Governor not reflected
here. Always cross-check the live list at
rbi.org.in/scripts/deputygovernors.aspx before stating who currently
holds all four Deputy Governor posts.
