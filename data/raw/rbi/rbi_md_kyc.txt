RBI Master Direction — Know Your Customer (KYC), Commercial Banks

Issued 28 November 2025 as one of RBI's sector-specific KYC Directions
(10 institution types covered, including commercial banks, NBFCs,
payments banks, and cooperative banks, each under its own Direction).

Risk Categorisation:
Customers must be categorised as Low, Medium, or High risk based on
identity, business/occupation, geography, and transaction-type factors.

Periodic KYC Update Frequency (by risk category):
- High-risk customers: full KYC updation at least once every 2 years.
- Medium-risk customers: once every 8 years.
- Low-risk customers: once every 10 years.

June 2025 Amendment:
The Reserve Bank of India (KYC) (Amendment) Directions, 2025 (effective
immediately from 12 June 2025) gave low-risk individual customers extra
time to complete a due periodic KYC update — up to one year after the
due date, or up to 30 June 2026, whichever is later.

This is a binding regulatory rule, not guidance — banks are required to
enforce these periodicities, not merely recommend them to customers.
Given the amendment history, always check whether a further amendment
has superseded these specific timelines before treating them as final.
