RBI Master Direction — Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) and Statutory Liquidity
Ratio (SLR), Commercial Banks

Consolidates all commercial-bank reserve-requirement rules into a
single Direction, issued 28 November 2025.

CRR Phased Schedule (as of the 2025 Directions):
A phased reduction was scheduled: 3.75% -> 3.5% -> 3.25% -> 3.00% of
Net Demand and Time Liabilities (NDTL), effective from the reporting
fortnights beginning 6 September, 4 October, 1 November, and 29
November 2025 respectively. Current CRR: 3.00% (consistent with the
rate confirmed in rbi_current_policy_rates for December 2025).

SLR Requirement:
Minimum 18.00% of NDTL, held in specified liquid assets (cash,
approved government securities, gold).

Minimum Daily CRR Maintenance:
Banks must maintain at least 90% of the prescribed average CRR on any
given day within the reporting fortnight (not just meet the average
over the full period).

NDTL Computation Notes:
The Direction clarifies treatment of inter-bank items, foreign
currency deposits, and Upper Tier II instruments, and lists specific
exclusions from NDTL (paid-up capital, certain refinance amounts, tax
provisions, specified government/agency receipts).

Since CRR/SLR are reviewed at RBI's bi-monthly Monetary Policy
Committee meetings (same cadence as the repo rate — see
rbi_current_policy_rates), always confirm the currently-effective
percentage before quoting it as final.
