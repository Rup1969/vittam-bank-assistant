RBI Grievance Redressal — Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026 (RB-IOS 2026)

RB-IOS 2026 came into force on 1 July 2026 (issued 16 January 2026),
replacing the Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021 (RB-IOS
2021). This is the current scheme in effect.

If a customer has a complaint about deficiency in service by a bank or
other RBI-regulated entity, and it is not resolved to their satisfaction
or not replied to within the applicable time limit, they can escalate it
under RB-IOS 2026.

Key points of the current scheme:
- No monetary limit on the amount in dispute that can be brought before
  the RBI Ombudsman.
- The RBI Ombudsman can award compensation up to Rs. 30 lakh for
  consequential loss, plus up to Rs. 3 lakh separately for the
  complainant's time, expenses, and mental anguish/harassment.
- Complaints can be filed online via the Complaint Management System at
  cms.rbi.org.in, or by email/physical mode.
- Coverage includes all Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks, State and
  Central Co-operative Banks (subject to deposit-size thresholds), NBFCs,
  payment system operators, and Credit Information Companies.
- A Regulated Entity or a complainant can appeal an Ombudsman's Award to
  the Appellate Authority within 30 days of receiving it.
- Complaints received before 1 July 2026, and appeals/execution of
  earlier Awards, continue to be governed by the older RB-IOS 2021 rules.

Within the bank, the Principal Nodal Officer (typically a General
Manager-level official) represents the bank and provides information for
complaints filed against it.

This scheme replaced RB-IOS 2021 — if you encounter older material citing
the 2021 scheme's monetary caps or procedures, treat this 2026 version as
current and the 2021 material as superseded.
