RBI Master Direction — Branch Authorisation (Commercial Banks)

Consolidates rules on opening, shifting, merging, and closing banking
outlets and related off-site units (ATMs, Business Correspondent
points, Doorstep Banking arrangements). Applies to all commercial
banks except Small Finance Banks, Payments Banks, and Local Area
Banks. In force with immediate effect from 28 November 2025.

Board Responsibility:
Banks' Boards must adopt a board-approved policy for branch-network
decisions, and for Digital Banking Units (DBUs), Business
Correspondents (BCs), Mobile/Off-site ATMs, and Doorstep Banking —
with clearly assigned oversight duties, not left to branch-level
discretion.

Opening Outlets WITHOUT Prior RBI Permission:
Scheduled commercial banks may open new banking outlets without RBI's
prior permission, PROVIDED at least 25% of the outlets opened in a
period are in Unbanked Rural Centres (URCs) — including rural,
Left-Wing-Extremism (LWE) affected districts, or North-Eastern states.
Opening MORE than the required 25% in a given period earns a carried-
forward credit usable for up to 2 subsequent years.

When Prior RBI Approval IS Required:
- Opening at sensitive locations (as defined by RBI/security
  considerations).
- Branches conducting government business.
- Any bank whose general branch-opening permission has been withdrawn
  by RBI.
Such requests go through the PRAVAAH portal and, once granted, remain
valid for one year.

Minimum Service Standards:
The Direction also prescribes minimum service standards for fixed-
point service delivery at a banking outlet (not detailed further here
— check the primary Direction for the specific standards).
