RBI Citizen's Charter — Service Delivery Commitments

RBI's Citizen's Charter lists roughly 213 distinct services across 9
RBI departments, each with a committed processing timeline.

Departments Covered:
- Currency Management — currency chest applications, note exchange.
- Government & Bank Accounts — challans, drafts, cash transactions,
  refinancing.
- Payment Systems — certificate authorisation, membership applications.
- Regulation — licensing/approvals for banks, NBFCs, Asset
  Reconstruction Companies (ARCs), and other financial institutions.
- Supervision — statutory auditor appointment approvals.
- Enforcement — compounding of contraventions (regulatory violations).
- Financial Markets — benchmark administration, trading platform
  authorisation.
- Foreign Exchange — Authorised Dealer (AD) authorisations, External
  Commercial Borrowing (ECB) approvals, investment transaction
  approvals.
- Internal Debt Management — primary dealer licensing, government
  securities account services.

Service Timelines:
Committed processing windows range from as short as 3 days to as long
as 270 days, with most falling between 15 and 90 calendar days. The
timeline clock starts only once RBI has received a complete
application (all required documents/information submitted) —
incomplete submissions do not start the countdown.

Application Channel:
Most applications for RBI services under this Charter are submitted
through the PRAVAAH platform (RBI's centralised regulatory application
portal).

This is a general service-standard reference — the exact timeline for
a specific service should be checked against the current published
Charter, since individual service timelines can be revised.
