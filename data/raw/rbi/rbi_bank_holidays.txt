RBI Bank Holidays — How Bank Holidays Are Determined

RBI publishes a Bank Holiday matrix, filterable by RBI regional office
(covering all major cities from Agartala to Vijayawada), month, and
year, showing when banks in that region will be closed.

Two Categories of Closure:
1. Negotiable Instruments Act (NI Act) Holidays — statutory closures
   declared under the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881. These are
   public holidays on which cheques/negotiable instruments cannot be
   presented, and banks are closed.
2. Banks' Closing of Accounts — additional closure periods relating to
   half-yearly/annual account-closing activity, distinct from NI Act
   holidays.

General Rule: All scheduled and non-scheduled banks observe a public
holiday on the second and fourth Saturday of every month, in addition
to the region-specific NI Act holidays (which vary by state/region —
e.g. a regional festival observed as a bank holiday in one RBI regional
office's jurisdiction may not be a holiday elsewhere in the country).

How To Use This For A Specific Date:
The actual list of holiday dates is region- and year-specific and
changes annually — this document intentionally does NOT reproduce a
dated list, since it would go stale immediately. For a specific date,
region, and year, check RBI's live Holiday Matrix directly, filtering
by the relevant RBI regional office.

This matters for banking staff/customers when planning cheque clearing,
RTGS/NEFT cut-offs, or any date-sensitive transaction around a
potential regional holiday.
