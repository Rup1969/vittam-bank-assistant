RBI Regulated Entities — How To Verify a Financial Institution is Genuine

RBI maintains a public directory/search tool letting anyone verify
whether a financial institution is genuinely regulated by RBI, before
depositing money or doing business with it — an important fraud-
prevention check.

Categories Searchable in RBI's Directory:
- Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs) and their branches.
- Non-Banking Finance Companies (NBFCs).
- Foreign bank branches and representative offices operating in India.
- Full-Fledged Money Changers and Authorised Dealers (forex).
- Credit Information Companies.
- Primary Dealers (government securities market).
- Payment System Operators (payment gateways, wallets, etc.).
- Mobile banking and prepaid payment instrument (PPI) issuers.
- Banks authorised to import gold/silver.
- Agency banks handling government business (tax collection, pension
  disbursement, etc.).

Why This Matters:
A common fraud pattern is an entity claiming RBI approval/registration
it does not actually have (e.g. a fake NBFC or unauthorised digital
lending app). Checking the entity's name against RBI's official list
in the relevant category is the direct way to confirm genuine
regulatory status before a customer commits funds — this is also
relevant to RBI's Digital Lending Apps (DLA) list published separately
by regulated entities, which serves the same verification purpose for
lending apps specifically.

This is a lookup tool, not a static list — the current, authoritative
answer for any specific entity's status should always be checked live
on RBI's site rather than assumed from memory.
