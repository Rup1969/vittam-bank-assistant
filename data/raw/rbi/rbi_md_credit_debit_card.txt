RBI Master Direction — Credit Cards and Debit Cards: Issuance and Conduct (Commercial Banks)

Applies immediately to all commercial banks, including SBI and other
new banks (a consolidation of prior circulars into a single Direction).

Board-Approved Policy Required, Covering:
Interest rates, fees, credit limits, customer consent procedures,
grievance redressal, and co-branding arrangements (co-branded cards
issued jointly with a non-bank partner).

Consent and Issuance Rules:
- Explicit customer consent is required before issuing a card.
- Unsolicited or unilateral credit-limit upgrades are prohibited — a
  bank cannot raise a customer's limit without the customer's
  agreement.
- Charges and Annual Percentage Rate (APR) must be clearly disclosed
  upfront, not buried in fine print.

Billing, Refunds, and Reviews:
Strict adherence to billing-cycle and refund-processing procedures is
required. Card-issuers must review their card operations semi-annually
through their Audit Committee, actively monitor credit risk on the
card portfolio, and ensure EMI-conversion offers on card spends are
transparent (clear disclosure of the effective interest rate, not just
the EMI amount).

This Direction is the regulatory basis for card-related customer
protections — e.g. why a bank cannot silently raise a credit limit, or
must clearly disclose the APR on a card before issuance.
