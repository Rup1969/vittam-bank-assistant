RBI Master Direction — Governance (Commercial Banks)

Board Committee Structure:
- Audit Committee of the Board (ACB): must comprise only Non-Executive
  Directors, with financial expertise required among members.
- Risk Management Committee of the Board (RMCB): must have a majority
  of Non-Executive Directors.
- Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC): handles director
  appointments and compensation policy.

Fit-and-Proper Director Criteria:
- Age: 35-67 years for Public Sector Banks (PSBs); up to 75 years for
  Private Banks (PVBs) — a materially different upper limit between
  the two.
- Minimum qualification: a graduate degree, plus expertise in banking,
  finance, law, IT, risk management, or agriculture.
- Disqualifications include holding a political position, involvement
  in para-banking activities, or a conflict of interest.

Board's Strategic Focus Areas (7 themes RBI expects boards to
prioritise):
1. Business Strategy — product innovation, competitiveness.
2. Risk Management — credit, market, liquidity risk policy oversight.
3. Financial Integrity — scrutiny of NPAs and provisioning adequacy.
4. Compliance — adherence to RBI and SEBI norms.
5. Customer Protection — preventing mis-selling, grievance resolution.
6. Financial Inclusion — priority sector lending, microfinance.
7. Human Resources — training, incentive structures, internal
   governance of staff.

This Direction governs how a bank's own Board must be structured and
what it must actively oversee — relevant context for understanding why
a bank's decision-making process looks the way it does, not a
customer-facing rule.
