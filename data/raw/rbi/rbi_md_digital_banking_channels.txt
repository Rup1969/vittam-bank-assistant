RBI Master Direction — Digital Banking Channels Authorisation (Commercial Banks)

Issued 28 November 2025, effective from 1 January 2026 — a
consolidated regulatory framework for how commercial banks are allowed
to launch and operate digital banking services.

Scope: applies to commercial banking companies (excluding Small
Finance Banks, Payments Banks, Local Area Banks), corresponding new
banks, and SBI, as defined under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.

Channels Covered:
Internet banking, mobile banking apps, USSD (Unstructured Supplementary
Service Data — e.g. the *99# banking service), SMS-based banking, and
other electronic platforms. The Direction distinguishes View-Only
facilities (balance/statement checking only) from Transactional
facilities (actual fund transfers/payments).

Approval Requirement:
Prior RBI approval is required specifically before launching a
TRANSACTIONAL digital banking facility (not for view-only facilities).
Banks apply to their Regional Office of RBI through the PRAVAAH portal,
subject to meeting prudential eligibility criteria, and must submit a
Board resolution plus supporting documents alongside the application.

This is the regulatory basis for why launching a new transactional
mobile/internet banking feature requires RBI sign-off, while a
read-only balance-check feature generally does not.
