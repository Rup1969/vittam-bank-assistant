RBI Master Direction — Interest Rate on Deposits (Commercial Banks)

Consolidates and replaces the previous 2016-era Master Directions on
deposit interest rates, effective 1 April 2025, applicable to all
banks in India (not commercial banks alone).

Premature Withdrawal:
- Banks may choose to offer term deposits WITHOUT a premature
  withdrawal option, EXCEPT that all term deposits from individuals
  (singly or jointly held) of Rs. 1 crore and below MUST carry a
  premature withdrawal facility — this is a floor protection for
  retail depositors, not optional for the bank.
- A prematurely withdrawn deposit earns interest at the rate
  applicable to the amount/period actually held, NOT the originally
  contracted rate for the full tenor.
- No interest at all is payable if premature withdrawal happens before
  completing the minimum period of 7 days.

Senior Citizens / Retired Staff:
Banks may offer additional interest to specified categories including
senior citizens and retired staff — this is permitted, not a fixed
mandated premium (compare to the specific rate a given bank actually
publishes, e.g. sbi_rates_deposit_structured, indianbank rates).

General Rules Banks Must Follow:
- Must adopt a Board-approved policy covering the interest-rate
  schedule, premature-withdrawal penalties, and specific rules for
  NRE/NRO/FCNR(B) deposits.
- Interest rates must be UNIFORM across all branches and customers for
  comparable deposits accepted on the same date — negotiating an
  individual customer's rate is prohibited.

This is the regulatory floor/framework — an individual bank's actual
published rates (e.g. SBI's or Indian Bank's FD tables) must comply
with this Direction but are set by the bank itself within it.
