Canara Bank home loan interest rate ranges from 7.15% to 10.00% p.a.
(mean approximately 8.06%), linked to the Repo Linked Lending Rate
(RLLR) at 8.00%. Maximum tenure 30 years, loan-to-value up to 90%.
