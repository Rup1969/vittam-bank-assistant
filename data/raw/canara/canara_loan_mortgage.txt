Canara Bank Mortgage Loan (Loan Against Property)

Interest Rate:
Ranges from 9.05% to 11.55% p.a. (mean rate approximately 9.98%),
effective 12 December 2025.

Related Product — Rental Loan:
A separate specialised product for borrowers with rental income to
pledge/service the loan against, ranging 9.25%-9.80% p.a. (mean
~9.43%) — distinct from the general Mortgage Loan product, similar in
concept to Bank of Baroda's "Loan Against Future Rent Receivables".

Purpose: loan against a residential/commercial property already
owned, for purposes other than purchasing that specific property
(compare to Housing Loan, which finances the property purchase
itself).

For eligibility, loan amount, tenure, and documentation, check the
specific product's own page or a branch, since Mortgage Loan and
Rental Loan have different eligibility bases (property value vs.
rental income).
