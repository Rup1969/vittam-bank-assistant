Canara Bank — Structured Lending Rate Table

Benchmark: RLLR (Repo Linked Lending Rate) = 8.00% effective 12 March
2026. Most retail loan rates below are quoted as a range effective 12
December 2025 (mean rate in parentheses) and are ultimately anchored
to RLLR.

Housing Loan: 7.15% - 10.00% (mean 8.06%)
Vehicle Loan: 7.45% - 15.00% (mean 9.09%)
Mortgage Loan (Loan Against Property): 9.05% - 11.55% (mean 9.98%)
Rental Loan: 9.25% - 9.80% (mean 9.43%)
Budget Loan (personal loan category): 9.70% - 14.70% (mean 11.86%)
Vidya Turant Education Loan: 6.85% starting, 8.60% - 11.35% range by
  institution tier
Swarna Gold Loan: ~8.75% - 9.60% (Swarna-branded: RLLR + 0.95% as of
  12 March 2026)
Kisan Credit Card (Agriculture): mean 9.20%, base 7%, subvented to as
  low as 4% for eligible prompt-repaying farmers
Loan Against Fixed Deposit: 1%-2% over the underlying FD rate

All rates above are headline ranges — the rate actually offered to an
individual applicant depends on credit profile, loan amount, tenure,
and scheme chosen. Reverify RLLR and any spread before quoting, since
it moves with RBI's repo rate.
