Canara Bank Loan / Overdraft Against Fixed Deposit

Interest Rate:
Typically 1% to 2% above the interest rate being earned on the
underlying fixed deposit — broadly 2 to 2.5 percentage points cheaper
than a standard personal loan, since the FD itself secures the
facility.

Loan Amount:
Up to 90% of the fixed deposit's value, available as an overdraft
facility.

Note on Overdraft Pricing:
There is no single standard published overdraft rate — the applicable
rate depends on the borrower's profile and relationship history with
the bank, in addition to the underlying FD rate. Confirm the exact
effective rate with a branch before quoting to a customer.

This product lets a depositor access liquidity without breaking the
FD, so the deposit continues earning its own interest throughout.
