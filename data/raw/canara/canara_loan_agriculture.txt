Canara Bank Agriculture Loans

Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme:
Mean interest rate 9.20% (as updated to 15 February 2025). However,
the base KCC interest rate is 7% p.a., and under the Government's
Interest Subvention Scheme, the effective rate to the farmer can be as
low as 4% p.a. for prompt repayers — a significant government-backed
reduction from the headline rate.

Purpose: easy access to credit for cultivation or household
requirements of the agricultural community, under a single-window KCC
facility (same broad mechanism as SBI's and Bank of Baroda's KCC
products).

Repayment: half-yearly or annual instalments, over a period of up to 5
years.

Related Schemes:
Canara Bank's Agriculture and Rural Credit Schemes page lists
additional agriculture/rural financing products beyond KCC — check
that page for specific allied-activity loans (e.g. animal husbandry,
horticulture) if KCC itself doesn't fit the customer's need.

Given the Interest Subvention Scheme materially changes the effective
rate for eligible, prompt-repaying farmers, always clarify which rate
(headline mean 9.20%, base 7%, or subvented ~4%) applies to the
specific customer's situation before quoting a single figure.
