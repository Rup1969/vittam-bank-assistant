Canara Bank Fixed Deposit Interest Rates (below Rs. 3 crore)

<!--AUTO:START-->
Effective 2 November 2026 (callable deposits), General Public range:
3.00% to 6.75% p.a. across tenors from 7 days to 10 years. Senior
citizens: 3.00% to 6.90% p.a. across the same tenor range (roughly a
0.50% premium on most tenors, as per the bank's general senior-citizen
policy).

Selected specific tenors reported:
- 7-45 days: 3.00% (general public)
- 46-90 days: 4.00% (general public)
- 1 year: 6.40% (general public)

Special-Tenor Deposits:
- 444 Days Deposit and 555 Days Deposit — special-tenor schemes with
  their own rates; the 555-day scheme offers 6.85% p.a. for senior
  citizens, with an additional 0.60% for super senior citizens.
<!--AUTO:END-->

Senior Citizen Premium: an additional 0.50% on deposits below Rs. 2
crore with a tenor of 180 days or more; super senior citizens receive
a further 0.60% on select special-tenor (444/555 day) schemes.

Savings Bank Account: 2.90% to 4.00% p.a. depending on balance slab;
all savings accounts are zero-minimum-balance effective June 2026.

This is a headline range, not a complete tenor-by-tenor table — the
bank's detailed rate page and downloadable rate document should be
checked for the exact rate at a specific tenor before quoting, since
rates are revised periodically.
