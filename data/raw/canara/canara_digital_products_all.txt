Canara Bank — Digital Products Overview

Canara ai1 (Mobile Banking Super App)
Canara Bank's primary mobile banking app, marketed as a "Super App"
with 300+ features under a "One Bank, One App" approach. Supports fund
transfer via IMPS, UPI, RTGS, and NEFT; a Scan & Pay facility available
even from the pre-login screen. Also used to invest in Fixed Deposits,
and to access Public Provident Fund (PPF), Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana,
and Senior Citizen Savings Scheme investment products. Additional
features: FD/RD opening, FASTag recharge, loan/EMI payment, locker
management, insurance, scheduled payments, nomination, and integrated
bill pay.

Canara ai1Pe (Unified UPI App)
A dedicated UPI app, separate from the main ai1 super app — allows
users of ANY bank (not just Canara Bank customers) to link accounts
for transfers, QR-code payments, bill payments, and set up auto-debit
mandates for recurring payments.

Canara ai1 Merchant Application
A UPI/BHIM QR merchant-facing app, available to onboarded merchant
customers holding a satisfactorily-running Savings/Current/OD/OCC
account with the bank — used to accept customer payments via QR code.

Corporate ai1 Mobile App
A separate app for Corporate Internet Banking users, letting business
customers transact via mobile without needing a desktop/laptop.

Net Banking
Standard browser-based internet banking portal, separate from the
mobile apps, for customers who prefer desktop access to the same core
banking services.

For product-specific transaction limits and eligibility, check Canara
Bank's current digital-banking help pages, since these limits are
revised periodically.
