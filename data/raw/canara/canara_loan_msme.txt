Canara Bank MSME / Business Loans

Pricing Basis:
MSME loans are typically priced on a floating rate linked to RLLR
(Repo Linked Lending Rate), with the exact spread depending on loan
amount, repayment tenure, nature of business, the enterprise's
financial history and credit score, and the value of
security/collateral pledged plus the borrower's internal/external risk
rating. Loan amounts available up to Rs. 10 crore across MSME products
(working capital finance, term loans, export finance, infrastructure
financing, and other schemes).

Special Interest Concessions:
- 0.50% reduction in applicable interest rate on new loans to Women
  Entrepreneurs under Micro & Small Enterprises (manufacturing and
  services).
- 0.25% concession in applicable interest rate on CGTMSE-covered
  accounts.

CGTMSE (Collateral-Free Loan Coverage):
Canara Bank participates in the CGTMSE scheme (Credit Guarantee Fund
Scheme for Micro and Small Enterprises), the same government-backed
collateral-free credit mechanism SBI and Bank of Baroda also
participate in — offers quick approval, flexible repayment, and
collateral-free funding for small businesses.

Area-Specific MSME Financing Schemes:
Canara Bank also offers financing schemes tailored to specific
geographic/industry clusters — check the area-specific scheme page for
whether one applies to a given business's location/sector.

For a specific MSME product's exact rate, eligibility, and
documentation, check that product's own page or a branch.
