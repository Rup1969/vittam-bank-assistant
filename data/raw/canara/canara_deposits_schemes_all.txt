Canara Bank — Deposit Schemes Overview

SECTION A: SAVINGS BANK ACCOUNT
Interest rate range: 2.90% to 4.00% p.a. depending on balance slab,
calculated on a daily-product basis and credited quarterly. Effective
June 2026, Canara Bank made all savings accounts zero-balance accounts
— the earlier minimum balance requirement (Rs. 500 rural / Rs. 1,000
semi-urban-urban-metro) no longer applies to any savings account.

SECTION B: TERM / FIXED DEPOSITS
See canara_fd_rates / canara_rates_deposit_structured for the full
tenor-wise table. Interest is compounded quarterly. Senior citizens get
an additional 0.50% on deposits below Rs. 2 crore with tenor of 180
days or more; super senior citizens get an extra 0.60% on select
special-tenor schemes.

SECTION C: SPECIAL / NAMED DEPOSIT SCHEMES
- Kamadhenu Deposit — a term deposit scheme (specific terms on its own
  page).
- Canara Tax Saver Scheme — 5-year lock-in term deposit qualifying for
  Section 80C tax deduction, same general structure as other banks'
  tax-saver FDs.
- Canara Dhanvarsha — a term deposit product (specific terms on its
  own page).
- Nitya Nidhi Deposit Scheme — a recurring/systematic deposit-style
  product.
- 444 Days Deposit and 555 Days Deposit — special-tenor FDs (rates in
  canara_fd_rates); the 555-day scheme currently offers 7.10% p.a. for
  senior citizens, with an extra 0.60% for super senior citizens on
  select special-tenor schemes (444/555 days).
- Canara Green Deposit — an environmentally-themed term deposit,
  matching the "green deposit" pattern seen at SBI and Bank of Baroda.

SECTION D: NON-RESIDENT ACCOUNTS
NRO/NRE Savings and Term Deposits, and FCNR(B) accounts, are priced on
a separate NRI-specific rate table — do not quote a domestic rate to
an NRI customer or vice versa.

Verify all rates against Canara Bank's live interest-rates page before
quoting, since both standard and special-tenor deposit rates are
revised periodically.
