Canara Bank Gold Loan (Swarna Schemes)

Interest Rate:
Starting from approximately 8.75%-8.90% p.a., typically ranging
8.85%-9.60% p.a. Swarna-branded products specifically priced at RLLR +
0.95% effective 12 March 2026.

Loan-to-Value (LTV):
Capped at 75%, per RBI's regulatory LTV limit for gold loans (Canara
Bank adheres to this cap, does not exceed it).

Scheme Variants:
- Swarna Loan (standard) — repaid within 12 months from sanction as a
  bullet payment (principal + interest together at the end of term).
- Swarna Express — a faster/instant-disbursal gold loan variant.
- Swarna Overdraft — an overdraft facility against pledged gold,
  rather than a standard term loan.
- Swarna Monthly Interest Loan — interest serviced monthly rather than
  at bullet maturity.

Loan Amount:
Up to Rs. 35 lakh, depending on gold valuation, purity assessment, and
the applicable LTV limit.

For eligibility and documentation specifics, check the relevant Swarna
scheme's own page or a branch, since bullet-repayment, overdraft, and
monthly-interest variants have different repayment structures.
