Canara Bank — Structured Deposit Rate Table

Savings Bank Account: 2.90% to 4.00% p.a. depending on balance slab;
all savings accounts are zero-minimum-balance effective June 2026.

Fixed Deposits (below Rs. 3 crore), effective 1 December 2024:
- General Public range: 4.00% to 7.40% p.a. (7 days to 10 years)
- Senior Citizen range: 4.00% to 7.90% p.a. (7 days to 10 years)
- 7-45 days: 4.00% (general public)
- 46-90 days: 5.25% (general public)
- 1 year: 6.85% (general public)
- 444 Days / 555 Days special-tenor schemes: 555-day senior citizen
  rate 7.10%, with an additional 0.60% for super senior citizens on
  select special-tenor schemes.

rates pool has a single structured reference table per bank, matching
the convention used for SBI, Bank of Baroda, and Indian Bank.)

Senior Citizen Premium: additional 0.50% on deposits below Rs. 2 crore
with tenor 180 days or more.
