Canara Bank Personal Loan

Interest Rate:
Starts from approximately 9.70% p.a. (headline "Budget Loan" category
range: 9.70%-14.70%, mean ~11.86%, per the bank's retail loan interest
rate range page dated 12 December 2025). The actual rate depends
heavily on the applicant's credit profile.

Canara Ready Cash (pre-approved personal loan for existing salaried
Canara Bank customers):
- Eligibility: existing salaried Canara Bank customers, age 21-55
  years, minimum monthly income Rs. 50,000, minimum credit score 700.
- EMI/Net Monthly Income (NMI) ratio: minimum 25%, or Rs. 1,000/month,
  after accounting for existing and proposed EMIs.
- Loan Amount: Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 10 lakh.
- Tenure: 1 to 5 years.
- Purpose: personal needs, other than speculative purposes.

Other Personal Loan Variants:
- Canara My Money — a separate personal loan scheme (own terms, check
  its own page).
- Canara Budget — a separate personal loan scheme, appears to
  correspond to the "Budget Loan" rate category (9.70%-14.70%).

For eligibility, exact amount, and documentation for Canara My Money
or Canara Budget specifically, check that scheme's own page or a
branch, since Canara Ready Cash's eligibility (existing salaried
customers only) does not apply to all personal loan variants.
