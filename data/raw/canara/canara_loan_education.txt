Canara Bank Education Loan

Vidya Turant Scheme:
Interest rate starts from 6.85%, ranging 8.60%-11.35% depending on the
institution's category. Collateral-free and guarantor-free regardless
of loan amount.

Eligibility:
Any Indian student who has cleared the previous qualifying exam and
secured admission via entrance test/merit at an eligible institution
under the scheme, including premier institutions: IITs, IIMs, NITs,
IISc, ISB (Hyderabad & Mohali), and other recognised institutes.

Loan Amount (by institution tier):
- ISB (Hyderabad & Mohali): up to Rs. 50 lakh.
- Select premier institutes: up to Rs. 40 lakh.
- Other approved institutions: up to Rs. 30 lakh.

Repayment Tenure:
Up to 15 years, not counting the moratorium period.

Processing Fee:
None (no processing fee under this scheme).

IBA Model Education Loan Scheme:
A separate, standardised education loan scheme following the Indian
Banks' Association (IBA) model template — check this scheme's own
page for terms, since it differs from Vidya Turant (which is
specifically restricted to premier-institution admissions).
