Canara Bank Vehicle Loan (Canara Vehicle)

Interest Rate:
Ranges from 7.45% to 15.00% p.a. (mean rate approximately 9.09%),
effective 12 December 2025.

Products:
- Canara Vehicle — the standard car/vehicle loan product.
- Canara Green Wheels — a dedicated electric-vehicle financing scheme,
  matching the "green auto loan" pattern seen at other banks (e.g.
  SBI's Green Car Loan, Bank of Baroda's not having a direct
  equivalent named product).

For eligibility, exact loan amount, tenure, margin/down-payment, and
documents required, check the specific product's own page (Canara
Vehicle vs. Canara Green Wheels have separate terms) or a branch.
