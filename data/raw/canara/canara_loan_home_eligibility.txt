Canara Bank Housing Loan — Eligibility, LTV, and Related Products

Eligibility:
- Eligible loan amount is generally up to 4 times gross annual income
  (up to 5 years' gross income permitted in select cases).
- A CIBIL score of 750 or above is recommended to improve approval
  chances, though the bank's own minimum threshold may differ.

Loan-to-Value (LTV):
Up to 90% — meaning a lower upfront down payment is required compared
to banks offering a lower LTV cap, depending on loan amount and
eligibility.

Tenure:
Up to 30 years.

Related Product — Canara Apna Ghar:
A specific housing loan scheme under the Canara Bank umbrella (own
distinct terms from the general Housing Loan product) — check its own
page for scheme-specific eligibility and rate.

This is a headline/summary reference — confirm the exact rate slab,
eligible amount, and LTV for a specific applicant via a branch or
Canara Bank's loan calculator before quoting.
