Bank of Baroda Loan / Overdraft Against Fixed Deposit

Domestic Fixed Deposit:
Customers can borrow up to 90% of their fixed deposit's value via an
overdraft facility against it. The specific overdraft interest rate
for domestic rupee FDs is not published on the general product page —
confirm the current rate with a branch (typically priced as a small
spread over the underlying FD's own rate, similar to other banks'
loan-against-FD products).

Loan Against FCNR(B) Deposits (foreign currency, for NRI customers):
- In foreign currency: 1.5% p.a. over the 6-month LIBOR rate as on the
  date of the loan, OR 1.50% over the FCNR(B) deposit's own rate,
  whichever is higher.
- In Indian Rupees: MCLR + 100 basis points (1.00%).

Related Products:
- Loans/Overdrafts against Non-Resident (Rupee) Fixed Deposits — a
  separate NRI-specific product line from the domestic FD overdraft.
- Loan/Overdraft Against Bank's Own Deposit (LABOD/ODBOD) — an
  agriculture/rural-banking-linked variant of the same concept.

Given the domestic rate isn't published, confirm the exact effective
rate with a branch before quoting to a customer, rather than assuming
parity with another bank's loan-against-FD pricing.
