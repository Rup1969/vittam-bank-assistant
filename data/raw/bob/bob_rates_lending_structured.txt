Bank of Baroda — Structured Lending Rate Table

Benchmark: BRLLR (Baroda Repo Linked Lending Rate) = 7.90% as of 6
December 2025 (RBI Repo Rate + Mark-Up + Strategic Premium). Most
retail/MSME loan rates below are quoted as BRLLR plus a spread — the
final rate to an individual customer also includes a borrower-specific
Strategic Premium (S.P.) based on credit profile.

Home Loan: BRLLR - 0.70% to BRLLR + 1.05% (floating); BRLLR + 1.00% to
  BRLLR + 2.05% (fixed)
Personal Loan: BRLLR + S.P. + 2.00% to BRLLR + S.P. + 7.35%
  (~10.15%-15.50%)
Digital Personal Loan: BRLLR + S.P. + 3.50% to BRLLR + S.P. + 8.85%
  (~11.65%-17.00%)
Loan to Pensioners: 8.75%-11.40%
Education Loan (PM Vidyalaxmi): BRLLR - 1.05% to BRLLR + 0.50%
Education Loan (Baroda Scholar): BRLLR + 0.55% to BRLLR + 4.55%
Education Loan (Baroda Gyan): BRLLR + 2.00% to BRLLR + 1.90%
Car Loan (New): BRLLR - 0.30% to BRLLR + S.P. + 3.15% (~7.60%-11.30%)
Digital Car Loan: BRLLR - 0.30% to BRLLR + S.P. + 0.60% (~7.60%-8.75%)
Two-Wheeler Loan: BRLLR + S.P. + 4.25% (~12.40%)
Gold Loan (EMI): BRLLR + S.P. + 1.00% (~9.15%)
Gold Loan (Overdraft): BRLLR + S.P. + 1.10% (~9.25%)
Mortgage Loan (Loan Against Property): BRLLR + S.P. + 0.75% to BRLLR +
  S.P. + 3.35% (~8.90%-11.50%)
Loan Against FCNR(B) Deposit (foreign currency): 1.5% over 6-month
  LIBOR, or 1.5% over the FCNR(B) rate, whichever is higher
Loan Against FCNR(B) Deposit (in INR): MCLR + 1.00%

All rates above are headline "starting from"/benchmark-plus-spread
figures — the rate actually offered to an individual applicant depends
on credit profile, loan amount, tenure, and scheme chosen. Reverify
BRLLR and any spread before quoting, since it moves with RBI's repo
rate.
