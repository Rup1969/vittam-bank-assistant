Bank of Baroda Agriculture Loans

Baroda Kisan Credit Card (BKCC):
Provides adequate and timely credit to farmers under a single window,
for cultivation and other farming needs, including short-term credit
for crops (including fodder crops).

Eligibility (tenant farmers/share croppers specifically):
Individual tenant farmers/share-croppers cultivating on an oral-lease
basis, resident in the village for at least 3 continuous years, and
cultivating the land for a reasonably long period (not less than 3
years), can be issued a KCC with a farm credit limit up to Rs. 10,000
under this specific route. (Landowning farmers have their own,
separate eligibility route not detailed here — check the BKCC product
page.)

Processing Charges:
- Nil for aggregate loans up to Rs. 3 lakh.
- Above Rs. 3 lakh to Rs. 10 lakh: Rs. 250 per lakh (or part thereof)
  plus GST.

Related Schemes:
- Baroda Animal Husbandry and Fisheries Kisan Credit Card — a KCC
  variant specifically for livestock/fisheries-based income, not crop
  cultivation.
- Agricultural Land Development Loan.
- Horticulture Loan Scheme (Scheme for Development of Horticulture).
- Lending to Agriculture — Other Priority Sectors (demand loan against
  specified security).

This is a summary-level entry only — for a specific agriculture loan
(standard KCC for landowning farmers, allied-activity loans, etc.),
check that scheme's own page or a branch, since eligibility and terms
vary significantly by scheme.
