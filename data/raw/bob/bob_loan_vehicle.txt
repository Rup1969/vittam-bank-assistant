Bank of Baroda Auto Loan (Car / Two-Wheeler)

Interest Rate (linked to BRLLR, 7.90% as of 6 December 2025, plus a
Strategic Premium (S.P.) that varies by applicant):
- Baroda Car Loan (New): BRLLR - 0.30% to BRLLR + S.P. + 3.15%
  (approximately 7.60%-11.30%).
- Digital Car Loan: BRLLR - 0.30% to BRLLR + S.P. + 0.60%
  (approximately 7.60%-8.75%) — narrower, generally cheaper band than
  the standard branch-assisted car loan, for the fully-digital
  application route.
- Baroda Two-Wheeler Loan: BRLLR + S.P. + 4.25% (approximately
  12.40%).

Purpose: financing new car purchases (Baroda Car Loan / Digital Car
Loan) and two-wheeler purchases.

For eligibility, loan amount, tenure, and documents, check the
specific product page or a branch, since the Digital Car Loan and
standard Baroda Car Loan have different application processes and
eligibility criteria despite overlapping rate bands.
