Bank of Baroda Fixed Deposit Interest Rates (Callable, below Rs. 3 crore)

Effective 12 June 2026, General Public | Senior Citizen:
- 7-14 days:                3.50% | 4.00%
- 15-45 days:               3.50% | 4.00%
- 46-90 days:               5.00% | 5.50%
- 91-180 days:              5.00% | 5.50%
- 181-210 days:             5.50% | 6.00%
- 211-270 days:             5.75% | 6.25%
- 271 days to <1 year:      6.00% | 6.50%
- 1 year:                   6.25% | 6.75%
- >1 year to 400 days:      6.25% | 6.75%
- >2-3 years:                6.25% | 6.75%
- >3-5 years:                6.30% | 6.90%
- >5-10 years:               6.00% | 7.00%

Special-Tenor Deposits:
- bob Square Drive (444 days): 6.45% | 6.95%
- bob Golden Goal (555 days):  6.75% | 7.25%

Senior citizens generally receive a 0.50% premium over the general
public rate, though this varies slightly by tenor (e.g. the >5-10 year
and special-tenor slabs).

These are callable (premature-withdrawal-permitted) rates for deposits
below Rs. 3 crore — bulk deposits (Rs. 3 crore+) and non-callable
variants are priced on a separate table. Verify the current rate
against Bank of Baroda's live page before quoting, since FD rates are
revised periodically.
