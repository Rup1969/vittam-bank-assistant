Bank of Baroda — Digital Products Overview

bob World (Mobile Banking App)
Bank of Baroda's primary mobile banking app — offers 190+ to 240+
banking services from one place, from simple balance enquiry to
cardless cash withdrawal. Supports opening a fully digital savings
account end-to-end, including Video KYC — no branch visit required.
Also used for FD/RD opening, bill payments, FASTag recharge, and debit
card management.

bob World Benefits (Rewards Program)
A loyalty points program layered on top of bob World — earns points
for activities like fresh bob World activation/login, UPI activation
and transactions, third-party transfers, bill payments, recharges,
travel bookings (flight/bus/hotel), locker applications, and credit
report generation. Points are redeemable within the program.

bob इ Pay / bob e-Pay (Dedicated UPI App)
A UPI-focused app separate from bob World — supports payments via
VPA, Account+IFSC, UPI Number, and Tap & Pay; mobile/DTH recharge;
bill payments; IPO bid acceptance (ASBA); Mandate functions; QR-code
ATM cash withdrawal; and person-to-person/retail-store payments.

bob World Tab
Tablet-based digital account-opening tool, used for instant Savings
Account opening for Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and similar
doorstep/assisted onboarding scenarios.

bob World Business
A dedicated current-account/business-banking variant of the bob World
app, for business customers rather than retail savings customers.

FASTag
RFID-based electronic toll payment, integrated into bob World —
rechargeable via net banking, debit/credit card, or UPI; FASTag
purchase and recharge can also earn bob World Benefits reward points.

Internet Banking (bob World Internet Banking)
Standard net-banking portal, separate from the mobile app, for
customers who prefer browser-based access to the same core banking
services.

For product-specific transaction limits and eligibility, check Bank of
Baroda's current digital-banking help pages, since these limits are
revised periodically.
