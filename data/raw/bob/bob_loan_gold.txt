Bank of Baroda Retail Gold Loan

Interest Rate (linked to BRLLR, 7.90% as of 6 December 2025, plus a
Strategic Premium (S.P.)):
- Demand/EMI-based Gold Loan: BRLLR + S.P. + 1.00% (approximately
  9.15%).
- Overdraft Gold Loan: BRLLR + S.P. + 1.10% (approximately 9.25%).

Purpose: loan against pledged gold ornaments/jewellery, available as
either a standard EMI-repayment loan or an overdraft facility against
the pledged gold.

Details on eligibility, maximum loan amount, loan-to-value ratio
against the gold pledged, and documentation are not detailed on the
general rates page — check with a branch or Bank of Baroda's dedicated
gold loan product page for a firm quote.
