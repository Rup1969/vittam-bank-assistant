Bank of Baroda Home Loan

Interest Rate (linked to BRLLR — Baroda Repo Linked Lending Rate,
7.90% as of 6 December 2025):
- Baroda Home Loan: BRLLR - 0.70% to BRLLR + 1.05% (floating); BRLLR +
  1.00% to BRLLR + 2.05% (fixed).
- Baroda Home Improvement Loan: same rate band as the standard Home
  Loan.
- Baroda Max Savings Home Loan: tiered rate depending on loan amount.
- Baroda CRE (Commercial Real Estate) Home Loan: 0.25%-0.50% premium
  over the standard Home Loan rate.

Eligibility:
- Minimum age 21 years for the primary applicant; a co-applicant may
  be added from age 18.
- Minimum CIBIL/credit score of 701 required.
- Eligibility also weighs financial position (salaried/self-employed),
  credit history, and existing liabilities (EMIs, credit card debt).

Loan Amount:
No fixed cap stated on the general product page — the eligible amount
is determined via Bank of Baroda's Home Loan Eligibility Calculator,
based on income, repayment capacity, and credit history. Use the
calculator or a branch for a specific applicant's eligible amount.

Documents Required:
- Completed application form.
- Identity proof and address proof.
- Last 3 months' salary slips (salaried applicants).
- Last 3 years' Form 16 (salaried applicants).
- Last 6 months' bank statement showing salary credit and any existing
  loan repayments.
- Property ownership papers.

This is a headline/summary reference — confirm the exact rate slab and
eligible amount for a specific applicant via Bank of Baroda's
calculator or a branch before quoting.
