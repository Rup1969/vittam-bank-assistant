Bank of Baroda — Deposit Schemes Overview

SECTION A: SAVINGS BANK ACCOUNT
Interest rate: 2.50% p.a. for retail balances (up to Rs. 10 crore),
effective 22 August 2025. Higher tiers (2.75% up to 3.50%/4.50%/4.75%)
apply only to very large institutional-scale balances (Rs. 50 crore
and above), not relevant for retail customers. Interest is calculated
at quarterly compounding intervals per Indian Banks' Association
guidelines.

SECTION B: SAVINGS ACCOUNT VARIANTS (bob-branded)
- bob LITE Savings Account — lifetime free zero-balance account, free
  RuPay Platinum debit card, no minimum balance charge.
- bob Advantage Saving Account — general-purpose flexible savings.
- bob Super Savings Account — for metro/urban high-value customers;
  immediate credit of outstation cheques up to Rs. 25,000.
- bob Masterstroke Saving Bank Account — high-income professionals;
  MAB requirement Rs. 10-50 lakh; lifetime-free Visa Opulence debit
  card; Rs. 5 lakh daily ATM limit.
- bob Masterstroke LITE — accident insurance up to Rs. 50 lakh, free
  banking services, aimed at customers wanting premium benefits more
  affordably than the full Masterstroke account.
- bob Platinum Saving Bank Account — for HNIs; free Visa Platinum
  debit card, Rs. 1 lakh daily withdrawal limit, unlimited cheque
  books.
- bob Professional Saving Bank Account — for doctors, CAs, CS,
  engineers, advocates; free RuPay Select debit card (year one),
  waived SMS alerts, demat AMC waiver.
- bob Senior Citizen Vishesh Samman SB — for 60+; free online doctor
  consultations, up to 15% concession on online medicine purchase,
  doorstep banking.
- bob Senior Citizen Privilege Scheme — free BOB Prime Credit Card
  with 1% cashback, 50% waiver on reverse mortgage processing fees.
- bob Pensioners Savings Bank Account — opened at Rs. 5 only;
  overdraft facility up to 2 months' pension amount.
- bob Women Sapphire Savings Account — critical illness cover of
  Rs. 10 lakh, healthcare concessions, RuPay-linked benefits.
- bob Mahila Shakti Saving Account — loan processing fee discounts,
  50% locker rental waiver, for women.
- bob BRO Saving Account — students aged 16-25; zero balance,
  lifestyle-focused benefits.
- bob Champ Account — minors 0-18 years; no minimum balance, themed
  debit card available from age 10+.
- bob Family Savings Accounts — links multiple family members' SB
  accounts so the zero-balance requirement is assessed collectively
  across the group.
- bob Jeevan Suraksha Saving Account — bundles life insurance with
  the SB account (single or joint), auto-sweep facility to a term
  deposit.
- bob SB Self Help Group — for SHGs; SMS alerts, outstation cheque
  collection.
- bob Bank Mitra Bachat Khata — opened only at Business Correspondent
  (BC) points, for rural/underbanked populations; Aadhaar seeding and
  government-scheme enrollment support.
- Govt. Bodies SB Account — for government departments/agencies;
  account transferability, unlimited free cash deposit at the home
  branch.

SECTION C: FIXED DEPOSITS
See bob_fd_rates / bob_rates_deposit_structured for the full tenor-wise
table. Headline products: standard callable FD (7 days to 10 years),
"bob Square Drive" (444-day special tenor), "bob Golden Goal" (555-day
special tenor), plus a Tax-Saving FD variant and "bob earth" Green Term
Deposits (callable/non-callable, up to Rs. 10 crore) for
environmentally-themed deposits.

SECTION D: NRI / FOREIGN CURRENCY DEPOSITS
Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Bank) [FCNR(B)] deposits are offered
separately from domestic retail FDs, with their own rate table — do
not quote a domestic FD rate to an NRI customer or vice versa.

Verify all rates against Bank of Baroda's live interest-rates page
before quoting, since both standard and special-tenor deposit rates
are revised periodically.
