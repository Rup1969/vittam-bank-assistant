Bank of Baroda Personal Loan

Interest Rate (linked to BRLLR, 7.90% as of 6 December 2025, plus a
Strategic Premium (S.P.) that varies by applicant):
- Baroda Personal Loan: BRLLR + S.P. + 2.00% to BRLLR + S.P. + 7.35%
  (approximately 10.15%-15.50% overall), linked to the applicant's
  CIBIL score — a better score gets a lower spread.
- Baroda Digital Personal Loan: BRLLR + S.P. + 3.50% to BRLLR + S.P. +
  8.85% (approximately 11.65%-17.00%) — the fully-digital, faster
  variant carries a somewhat higher rate band than the standard
  branch-assisted product.
- Baroda Loan to Pensioners: 8.75%-11.40% depending on pensioner
  category.

Key Point: the exact rate depends heavily on the applicant's CIBIL
score — quote the range, not a single figure, unless the applicant's
score is known.

For eligibility, loan amount, tenure, and documentation specifics,
check the relevant product page (Baroda Personal Loan vs. Baroda
Digital Personal Loan vs. Loan to Pensioners each has its own detail
page) or a branch, since terms differ meaningfully between these
variants.
