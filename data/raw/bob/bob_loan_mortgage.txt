Bank of Baroda Mortgage Loan (Loan Against Property)

Interest Rate (linked to BRLLR, 7.90% as of 6 December 2025, plus a
Strategic Premium (S.P.)):
- Baroda Mortgage Loan (Individuals): BRLLR + S.P. + 0.75% to BRLLR +
  S.P. + 3.35% (approximately 8.90%-11.50%).
- Baroda Loan Against Future Rent Receivables: BRLLR + S.P. + 2.00%
  floating (approximately 10.15%), or a 4.00% fixed spread variant —
  a specialised product for borrowers with rental income to pledge
  against, not a general-purpose mortgage loan.

Purpose: loan against a residential/commercial property already
owned, for purposes other than purchasing that specific property
(compare to Home Loan, which finances the property purchase itself).

Documents commonly required (per Bank of Baroda's general Loan Against
Property checklist): property title documents, identity and address
proof, income proof (salary slips/ITR depending on employment type),
and bank statements — exact requirements vary by applicant profile and
should be confirmed with a branch.
