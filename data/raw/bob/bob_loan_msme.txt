Bank of Baroda MSME / Business Loans

Pricing Basis:
MSME loans are priced off BRLLR (Baroda Repo Linked Lending Rate),
7.90% as of 6 December 2025 (derived from RBI's Repo Rate plus a
Mark-Up and a Strategic Premium), plus a product/borrower-specific
spread.

Digital MSME Loan:
A fully-digital working-capital loan, ranging from Rs. 10 lakh to
Rs. 5 crore. Purpose includes acquisition of factory/land, building
construction, purchase of plant and machinery, and working-capital
needs (raw materials, finished goods financing).

Baroda SME Loan Pack:
A bundled business-loan product for SME financing needs beyond the
digital working-capital product — check the specific pack's terms for
a given business size/sector.

CGTMSE (Collateral-Free Loan Coverage):
Bank of Baroda participates in the CGTMSE scheme (Credit Guarantee
Fund Scheme for Micro and Small Enterprises), allowing eligible MSE
borrowers to access collateral-free credit — same underlying
government scheme SBI participates in (see sbi_loan_msme for the
scheme's general mechanics: 85% guarantee coverage, Udyam Registration
required).

For a specific MSME product's exact rate, eligibility, and
documentation, check that product's own page or a branch, since rates
and terms vary by loan size, sector, and whether CGTMSE cover applies.
