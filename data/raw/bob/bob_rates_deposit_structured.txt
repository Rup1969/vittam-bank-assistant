Bank of Baroda — Structured Deposit Rate Table

Savings Bank Account: 2.50% p.a. for retail balances up to Rs. 10
crore (effective 22 August 2025).

Fixed Deposits (callable, below Rs. 3 crore), rates effective 12 June
2026, General Public | Senior Citizen:
- 7-14 days:            3.50% | 4.00%
- 46-90 days:           5.00% | 5.50%
- 181-210 days:         5.50% | 6.00%
- 271 days to <1 year:  6.00% | 6.50%
- 1 year:               6.25% | 6.75%
- 2-3 years:            6.25% | 6.75%
- 3-5 years:            6.30% | 6.90%
- 5-10 years:           6.00% | 7.00%
- bob Square Drive (444 days): 6.45% | 6.95%
- bob Golden Goal (555 days):  6.75% | 7.25%

(This mirrors bob_fd_rates.txt — kept here too so the consolidated
rates pool has a single structured reference table per bank, matching
the convention used for SBI and Indian Bank.)

Bulk deposits (Rs. 3 crore and above) and non-callable variants are
priced separately — check Bank of Baroda's dedicated pages for those
tables.

Recurring Deposit: not separately rate-tabled on Bank of Baroda's
public pages at the time of writing — generally tracks the equivalent-
tenor FD rate, consistent with how other banks price RDs; confirm with
a branch for an exact current RD rate.
