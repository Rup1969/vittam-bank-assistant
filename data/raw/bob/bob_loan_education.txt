Bank of Baroda Education Loan

Interest Rate (linked to BRLLR, 7.90% as of 6 December 2025):
- PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme: BRLLR - 1.05% (top institutions) to BRLLR +
  0.50% (category C institutions) — a government-backed scheme with
  the most favourable rates, scaled by institution ranking.
- Baroda Scholar: BRLLR + 0.55% to BRLLR + 4.55%, depending on
  institution.
- Baroda Gyan: BRLLR + 2.00% to BRLLR + 1.90% (general education loan
  scheme).

Key Point: rate depends heavily on which specific scheme applies and
the ranking/category of the educational institution — PM Vidyalaxmi
offers the best rates but only for eligible institutions under that
government scheme.

For eligibility, loan amount, moratorium, and documentation, check the
specific scheme's own page (PM Vidyalaxmi vs. Baroda Scholar vs.
Baroda Gyan each has different terms) or a branch.
