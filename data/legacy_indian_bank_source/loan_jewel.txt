IB JEWEL LOAN

Purpose:
Instant loan against pledge of gold jewellery/ornaments for any personal or business financial need.

Target Group:
- Resident individuals
- Agriculturists (separate agricultural jewel loan product)
- Business owners needing quick working capital

Eligibility:
- Any resident individual owning gold jewellery
- Minimum age: 18 years
- KYC compliance mandatory

Loan Amount:
- Based on gold weight, purity, and prevailing RBI-prescribed LTV ratio
- LTV: up to 75% of gold value (as per RBI guidelines)
- Minimum: Rs.5,000; Maximum: as per bank norms

Types:
- Demand Loan (lump sum with bullet repayment)
- Overdraft (flexible withdrawal within limit)
- Agricultural Jewel Loan (for farming purposes — lower rate)

Repayment:
- Demand Loan: 12 months (renewable)
- OD: Annual renewal
- Interest can be paid monthly/quarterly or on maturity

Interest Rate:
- Competitive rate linked to bank's base rate
- Agricultural purpose: lower concessional rate

Security:
- Gold jewellery pledged with bank (minimum 18 carat purity)
- Bank assays and values the jewellery at time of pledge

Online Appointment:
- Book jewel loan appointment online at indianbank.bank.in
- Walk-in also accepted at all branches
