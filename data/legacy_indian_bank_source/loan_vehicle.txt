IB VEHICLE LOAN

Interest Rate:
- IB Vehicle Loan (new car): 7.55%-8.85% p.a.
- IB Vehicle Loan (Eco Vahan, electric vehicles): 7.50%-8.75% p.a. —
  the lowest published rate among Indian Bank's vehicle loan schemes.
- IB Vehicle Loan (Used Car): 9.55%-11.65% p.a.
- 2-Wheeler: 8.90%-10.40% p.a.
- Van/Minibus/Bus/Ambulance (institutional): 8.75%-9.40% p.a.
- Rates are linked to RLLR (Repo-Linked Lending Rate) — floating, so the
  above ranges move with RBI's repo rate over the loan's life. A fixed
  ROI option is also available.

Purpose:
Purchase of new or used vehicles — two-wheelers, four-wheelers (cars/SUVs), and commercial vehicles.

Target Group:
- Salaried individuals
- Professionals and self-employed
- Businessmen with regular income

Eligibility:
- Resident individuals with stable income
- Minimum age: 21 years
- Both new and used vehicles covered

Loan Amount:
- New vehicles: up to 85%–90% of on-road price
- Used vehicles: based on valuation report and age of vehicle

Repayment:
- New cars: up to 84 months (7 years)
- Two-wheelers: up to 60 months (5 years)
- Used vehicles: shorter tenure based on vehicle age

Security:
- Hypothecation of the vehicle purchased

Documents Required:
- Income proof (salary slips / ITR)
- KYC documents
- Vehicle quotation / proforma invoice
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
