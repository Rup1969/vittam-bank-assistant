LOAN / OVERDRAFT / CREDIT CARD AGAINST DEPOSIT

Purpose:
Avail instant liquidity against your existing Fixed Deposit without breaking it — your deposit continues to earn interest.

Variants:
1. Demand Loan against FD
2. Overdraft (OD) against FD
3. Credit Card against FD (secured credit card)

Eligibility:
- Holders of FD/Term Deposit/MMD/RD with Indian Bank
- Both resident and NRI deposits (conditions apply for NRE deposits)
- Joint deposit holders — with consent of all holders

Loan Amount:
- Up to 90% of deposit value
- Minimum: Rs.5,000

Interest Rate:
- 1% to 2% above the deposit interest rate
- Very competitive since deposit continues to earn

Repayment:
- Loan: Bullet repayment at deposit maturity
- OD: Renewable annually
- Interest can be serviced monthly

Security:
- The FD itself is the collateral (lien marked on deposit)
- No additional security required

Key Benefit:
- No need to break FD and lose interest
- Instant sanction — same day processing
- No income proof required
- No credit score check required
