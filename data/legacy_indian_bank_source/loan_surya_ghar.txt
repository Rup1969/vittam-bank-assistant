PM SURYA GHAR: MUFT BIJLI YOJANA — SOLAR ROOFTOP LOAN

Purpose:
Loan for installation of rooftop solar panels under the Central Government's PM Surya Ghar scheme — to help households generate their own electricity and receive up to 300 units free per month.

Target Group:
- Residential households planning rooftop solar installation
- All income groups eligible

Eligibility:
- Indian resident individual
- Own residential property
- Property suitable for solar panel installation

Loan Amount:
- Based on installation cost and subsidy
- Central subsidy: Rs.30,000 per kW up to 2 kW; Rs.18,000/kW for 2–3 kW

Repayment:
- Up to 10 years

Interest Rate:
- Concessional rate as per scheme guidelines

Subsidy:
- Direct subsidy credited to bank account by Government of India
- Net loan = Installation cost minus subsidy amount

How to Apply:
- Register on pmsuryaghar.gov.in portal
- Choose empanelled vendor
- Apply for loan at Indian Bank branch or online

Helpline: 1800 1700
