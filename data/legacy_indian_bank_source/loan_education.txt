IB EDUCATION LOAN

Interest Rate:
- Education Loan (general): 7.05%-10.15% p.a., depending on loan amount
  and applicant profile — the lowest published rate among Indian Bank's
  education loan schemes.
- PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme: 7.25%-8.45% p.a. (government scheme, linked to
  institute category).
- IB SKILL Loan (vocational/skill courses): 9.45% p.a. flat.
- Rates are linked to RLLR (Repo-Linked Lending Rate) — floating, so the
  above ranges move with RBI's repo rate over the loan's life.
- Interest subsidy schemes available for eligible students.

Purpose:
Finance higher education in India and abroad — covers tuition fees, hostel charges, books, equipment, travel expenses for studies abroad.

Target Group:
- Indian nationals who have secured admission to recognised institutions
- Courses covered: Graduate, Post-graduate, Professional (engineering, medical, MBA, law, CA, etc.)
- Both Indian and overseas institutions covered

Eligibility:
- Student must be an Indian national
- Admission to recognised institution through merit/entrance test
- Co-borrower: Parent or guardian mandatory

Loan Amount:
- Studies in India: up to Rs.10 lakh (higher amounts based on merit)
- Studies abroad: up to Rs.20 lakh (higher amounts available)
- Premier institution list: higher quantum available

Repayment:
- Moratorium: Course period + 1 year (or 6 months after getting job, whichever is earlier)
- Repayment: up to 15 years after moratorium

Collateral:
- Up to Rs.7.5 lakh: No collateral (parents as co-borrower)
- Above Rs.7.5 lakh: Tangible collateral required

Government Schemes Linked:
- Dr. Ambedkar Central Sector Scheme (interest subsidy for OBC/EBC)
- Padho Pardesh scheme (for minority students abroad)
