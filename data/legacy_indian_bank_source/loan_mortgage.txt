MORTGAGE LOAN — LOAN AGAINST PROPERTY

Purpose:
Multipurpose loan against mortgage of owned residential or commercial property for business expansion, working capital, medical emergencies, marriage, education, or any personal need.

Target Group:
- Resident individuals owning property
- Business entities owning commercial/residential property
- Professionals

Eligibility:
- Clear and marketable title of property
- Property in applicant's name or close family member
- Stable income — salaried or self-employed

Loan Amount:
- Up to 60%–70% of property market value (LTV ratio)
- Higher amounts for commercial properties with strong income

Repayment:
- Up to 15 years — EMI based
- Overdraft facility also available

Interest Rate:
- Linked to RLLR — floating rate
- Fixed ROI option available

Security:
- Equitable/registered mortgage of property
- Legal and valuation reports mandatory

Documents Required:
- Title deeds, sale deed, encumbrance certificate
- Property tax receipts
- Income proof
- KYC documents
- Building plan approval (if applicable)
