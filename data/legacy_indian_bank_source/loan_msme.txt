MSME LOANS — MICRO, SMALL AND MEDIUM ENTERPRISES

Purpose:
Working capital and term loan financing for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises across manufacturing and service sectors.

Target Group:
- Micro enterprises (investment up to Rs.1 Cr, turnover up to Rs.5 Cr)
- Small enterprises (investment up to Rs.10 Cr, turnover up to Rs.50 Cr)
- Medium enterprises (investment up to Rs.50 Cr, turnover up to Rs.250 Cr)

Products Available:
1. Working Capital — Cash Credit / Overdraft
2. Term Loan for plant, machinery, equipment
3. Letter of Credit (LC) for trade
4. Bank Guarantee
5. MUDRA Loans (under PMMY): Shishu (up to Rs.50,000), Kishore (Rs.50,000–5L), Tarun (Rs.5L–10L)
6. Stand-Up India scheme (for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs)
7. 59 Minutes Loan — in-principle approval within 59 minutes

In-Principle Approval in 59 Minutes:
- Visit psbloansin59minutes.com/indianbank
- For loans up to Rs.5 Crore
- GST, IT returns, bank statement analysis done digitally

Turnaround Time (TAT):
- Published on bank website as per RBI guidelines

Documents Required:
- Business registration / MSME Udyam certificate
- GST returns, ITR for last 2–3 years
- Bank statements (12 months)
- Project report for term loans
- KYC of proprietor/partners/directors
