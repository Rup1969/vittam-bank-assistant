IB HOME LOAN

Interest Rate:
- IB Home Loan / IB Home Loan (NRI): 7.15%-8.55% p.a. — the lowest
  published rate among Indian Bank's home loan family.
- Home Loan to Corporate Entity: 7.60%-9.10% p.a.
- IB Home Loan Plus: 7.65%-8.75% p.a.
- IB Home Loan (CRE): 7.65%-9.05% p.a.
- Home Loan for EWS, LIG & MIG Individuals (Urban Areas): 7.75%-9.15% p.a.
- Plot Loan / Plot Loan (NRI): 8.15%-9.55% p.a.
- IB Home Improve: 8.15%-9.55% p.a.
- IB Home Enrich: 8.15%-8.70% p.a.
- Rates are linked to RLLR (Repo Linked Lending Rate) — floating; a
  Fixed ROI option is also available under Fixed ROI for Retail Loans.

Purpose:
Purchase or construction of house/flat, purchase of plot and construction thereon, renovation/repair/extension of existing house.

Target Group:
- Salaried class employees (Govt, PSU, reputed private companies)
- Professionals, self-employed, businessmen
- NRIs (separate NRI Home Loan product available)
- Corporate entities (separate product available)

Eligibility:
- Resident individuals with regular source of income
- Minimum age: 18 years; Maximum age: 70 years at loan maturity
- Joint applicants permitted; co-obligant income can be clubbed

Loan Amount:
- No upper ceiling — based on repayment capacity and property value
- Rural/Semi-urban areas: special consideration
- Maximum LTV ratio: 75% to 90% depending on loan amount

Repayment:
- Maximum tenure: 30 years (including moratorium)
- EMI-based repayment
- Moratorium period: up to 18–36 months during construction

Special Schemes under Home Loan:
1. IB Plot Loan — for purchase of residential plot
2. NRI Home Loan — for Non-Resident Indians
3. NRI Plot Loan — for NRIs buying residential plots
4. Home Loan to Corporate Entity — for companies/LLPs/firms
5. IB Home Loan Flexi — for existing and prospective home loan customers
6. IB Home Improve — renovation/repair for existing home loan borrowers
7. IB Home Enrich — for individuals owning residential property
8. Home Loan for EWS/LIG/MIG — for economically weaker sections
9. PMAY Urban 2.0 (ISS) — interest subsidy scheme for eligible borrowers

Security:
- Equitable/registered mortgage of the property being financed

Processing Fee:
- As per bank's schedule of charges (visit indianbank.bank.in/service-charges-forex-rates/)

Online Apply:
