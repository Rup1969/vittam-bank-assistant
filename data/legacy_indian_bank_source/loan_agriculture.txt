AGRICULTURE LOANS — KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC)

Yes — Indian Bank offers a Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme, a revolving
credit facility for farmers to meet crop production, post-harvest
expenses, and allied agricultural activities, under the nationwide
Kisan Credit Card scheme.

Purpose:
Comprehensive credit support for farming, allied activities, and rural livelihoods.

Products:

1. KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC)
   - For crop production, post-harvest expenses, allied activities
   - Revolving credit — withdraw and repay as per crop cycle
   - Interest subvention available from Government of India
   - ATM card provided for easy withdrawal

2. AGRICULTURAL TERM LOAN
   - For land development, farm mechanisation, irrigation
   - Repayment linked to crop harvest cycles

3. JEWEL LOAN (AGRICULTURE)
   - Against gold jewellery for agricultural purposes
   - Lower interest rate than regular jewel loan
   - Concessional rate under interest subvention scheme

4. SHG LOANS (Self Help Group)
   - For groups of 10–20 women/individuals
   - Credit linkage after 6 months of group savings

5. JLG LOANS (Joint Liability Group)
   - For tenant farmers, oral lessees, share croppers
   - No collateral required

6. DAIRY / ANIMAL HUSBANDRY LOANS
   - For purchase of milch animals, poultry, fishery

7. MUDRA LOANS (PMMY)
   - Shishu: up to Rs.50,000
   - Kishore: Rs.50,001 to Rs.5 Lakh
   - Tarun: Rs.5 Lakh to Rs.10 Lakh

Interest:
- Crop loans up to Rs.3 lakh: 7% p.a. with Govt interest subvention
- Prompt repayment incentive: effective rate as low as 4% p.a.

KCC Sanction Rules (from Indian Bank's own official KCC page):

Eligibility: All Farmers — Individuals/Joint Borrowers who are owner
cultivators, plus tenant farmers, oral lessees, share croppers, and
groups (Self Help Groups / Joint Liability Groups) of farmers.

Credit Limit: Scale of finance for the crop (as decided by the
District Level Technical Committee) x extent of area cultivated, plus
10% toward post-harvest/household/consumption needs, plus 20% for
farm asset repairs. An additional 10% increase in the Crop Loan
Component applies for every successive year.

Margin: No Margin — explicitly stated as a salient feature; farmers
need not contribute equity.

Security:
- Up to Rs.2 lakh, or up to Rs.3 lakh under a tie-up arrangement:
  hypothecation of crops only.
- Above Rs.2 lakh (without tie-up): hypothecation of crops plus
  pledge of jewels, or deposit receipts/LIC/NSC assignment, or
  collateral security by way of MOD (Memorandum of Deposit of title
  deeds) / charge creation.

Validity: KCC is valid for 5 years. Short-term withdrawals must be
liquidated within 12 months; the term-loan component follows Indian
Bank's existing investment-credit guidelines.
