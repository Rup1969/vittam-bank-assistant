SECTION C: TERM DEPOSIT SCHEMES
========================================

FD Interest Rates (w.e.f. 04.02.2026, less than Rs.3 Crore):
- 7-14 days: 2.80%
- 30-45 days: 3.00%
- 46-90 days: 3.25%
- 91-120 days: 3.50%
- 121-180 days: 3.85%
- 181 days to <9 months: 4.50%
- 9 months to <1 year: 4.75%
- 1 year: 6.10%
- Above 1 year to <2 years: 6.20%
- 2 years to <3 years: 6.15%
- 3 years to <5 years: 6.05%
- 5 years and above: 6.00%
Senior Citizens: Additional 0.50% on all above tenures
Super Senior Citizens (80+ yrs): Additional 0.75% on all above

--------------------------------------------
1. FIXED DEPOSIT (FD)
--------------------------------------------
Min Amount: Rs.1,000 (no maximum)
Tenure: 7 days to 10 years
Interest: Quarterly or monthly (monthly paid at discounted rate)
Premature Withdrawal: Allowed with penalty (1% below card rate)
No penalty: If held 181+ days for deposits up to Rs.5 lakh
Auto-Renewal: Available on maturity

--------------------------------------------
2. RECURRING DEPOSIT (RD)
--------------------------------------------
Purpose: Fixed monthly savings building into large corpus
Interest: Quarterly compounding
Ideal For: Salaried/regular income earners
Tenure: 6 months to 120 months (in multiples of 3 months)
Senior Citizen Benefit: Available

--------------------------------------------
3. VARIABLE RECURRING DEPOSIT (VRD)
--------------------------------------------
Special Feature: Monthly deposit amount can vary — flexible
Min Amount: Rs.500 or multiples of Rs.100
Max Amount: Rs.1 lakh per month
Interest: Quarterly compounding; paid at maturity or pre-closure
Benefit: No need to open new RD each time amount changes

--------------------------------------------
4. MONEY MULTIPLIER DEPOSIT (MMD) — REINVESTMENT PLAN
--------------------------------------------
Special Feature: Interest compounded and reinvested — earns
                 interest on interest (cumulative)
Min Amount: Rs.1,000 and multiples of Rs.100
Interest: Quarterly compounding, paid at maturity
Best For: Long-term wealth building without periodic payouts

--------------------------------------------
5. SHORT TERM DEPOSITS
--------------------------------------------
Purpose: Park funds for very short durations (weeks to months)
Interest: As per card rate for actual period

--------------------------------------------
6. IB TAX SAVER SCHEME
--------------------------------------------
Tax Benefit: Deduction under Section 80C of Income Tax Act
Lock-in Period: 5 years (mandatory — cannot withdraw early)
Max Deduction: Rs.1.5 lakh per year under 80C
Interest: Quarterly or monthly or on maturity
Note: Rate same as regular FD for respective tenure

--------------------------------------------
7. IB GOLDEN AGER — SUPER SENIOR CITIZEN FD
--------------------------------------------
Target: Super Senior Citizens aged 80 years and above
Special Rate: Additional 0.25% over and above the normal
              senior citizen additional rate
Example: Regular FD 6.10% + Senior 0.50% + Golden Ager 0.25% = 6.85%

--------------------------------------------
8. IND NON-CALLABLE DEPOSIT
--------------------------------------------
Special Feature: Premature withdrawal NOT permitted
Reward: Higher interest rate than regular callable FD
Amount: Rs.1,000 to less than Rs.3 crore
Best For: Investors who can commit funds for full tenure

SECTION D: NRI ACCOUNTS
========================================

--------------------------------------------
NRE ACCOUNT (Non-Resident External)
--------------------------------------------
Currency: Indian Rupees
Repatriability: Fully repatriable (principal + interest)
Tax: Interest tax-free in India
Use: For foreign income/savings brought to India
Deposits: Savings, Current, FD, RD available
FD Rates: Same as domestic retail term deposit rates
Min FD Tenure: 1 year; No interest if closed before 1 year

--------------------------------------------
NRO ACCOUNT (Non-Resident Ordinary)
--------------------------------------------
Currency: Indian Rupees
Repatriability: Limited — up to USD 1 million per year
Tax: Interest taxable in India (TDS applicable)
Use: For income earned in India (rent, pension, dividends)
Deposits: Savings, Current, FD, RD available

--------------------------------------------
FCNR(B) ACCOUNT (Foreign Currency Non-Resident Bank)
--------------------------------------------
Currency: Held in foreign currency (USD, GBP, EUR, etc.)
Repatriability: Fully repatriable
Tax: Interest tax-free in India
Exchange Risk: None on principal (stays in foreign currency)
Tenure: 1 year to 5 years
Rates: Separate FCNR rates in respective currencies
