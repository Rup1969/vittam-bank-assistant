INDIAN BANK — COMPLETE DIGITAL PRODUCTS & SERVICES

========================================
SECTION A: MOBILE BANKING
========================================

--------------------------------------------
1. IndSMART — RETAIL MOBILE BANKING APP
--------------------------------------------
For: Savings Bank and Current Account holders
Platform: Android and iOS
Key Features:
- Account balance enquiry and mini statement
- Fund transfers: NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI
- Bill payments (electricity, gas, water, mobile recharge)
- Fixed Deposit and Recurring Deposit opening
- Account statement download
- Cheque stop payment request
- Debit card block/unblock
- Credit card payment
- FASTag recharge
- Loan EMI payment
- UPI payments via QR code
How to Register: Download from Play Store/App Store,
                 register with account number and mobile number

--------------------------------------------
2. IndSMART BIZ — CORPORATE MOBILE BANKING
--------------------------------------------
For: Corporate and business account holders
Key Features:
- Bulk payment processing
- Multi-user access with maker-checker workflow
- Corporate fund management
- Business-specific transaction reporting
- High-value RTGS/NEFT transfers

========================================
SECTION B: INTERNET BANKING
========================================

--------------------------------------------
3. IND NET BANKING
--------------------------------------------
For: SB and Current Account holders
Access: indianbank.bank.in → Internet Banking
Key Features:
- Complete fund transfers (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS)
- Account statement for any period
- FD/RD opening and closing online
- TDS certificate download
- Interest certificate download
- Cheque book request
- Stop payment of cheques
- Tax payment (direct and indirect)
- Utility bill payments
- Loan account details
How to Register: Visit branch or register online with debit card

SECTION C: UPI & PAYMENTS
========================================

--------------------------------------------
4. WHATSAPP BANKING
--------------------------------------------
Service: Banking services via WhatsApp messenger
Available Services:
- Account balance enquiry
- Mini statement (last 5 transactions)
- Account details
- Loan account details
- FD details
How to Use: Save Indian Bank WhatsApp number and send "Hi"
Access: indianbank.bank.in/departments/whatsapp-banking/

--------------------------------------------
5. SMS BANKING / MISSED CALL SERVICE
--------------------------------------------
Balance Enquiry: Give a missed call to designated number
Mini Statement: Send SMS to designated number
Language: Available in English
Free Service: No charge for mandatory SMS alerts

--------------------------------------------
6. UPI THROUGH USSD (*99#)
--------------------------------------------
For: Feature phone users without internet/smartphone
How: Dial *99# on any mobile phone
Languages: English and Hindi
Services: UPI fund transfer, balance check, mini statement
Benefit: Banking without internet — useful for rural areas

--------------------------------------------
7. BHIM AADHAAR PAY
--------------------------------------------
For: Merchant payments using Aadhaar biometric authentication
How: Customer gives Aadhaar number and fingerprint at merchant
No Need: No card, no smartphone required from customer
Benefit: Payments for those without debit cards
Access: indianbank.bank.in/departments/bhimaadhaarpay/

--------------------------------------------
8. MERCHANT UPI QR CODE
--------------------------------------------
For: Merchants accepting UPI payments
Types: Static QR (fixed amount) and Dynamic QR (variable amount)
Benefit: Accept payments from any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.)
Settlement: T+1 settlement to merchant account
Access: indianbank.bank.in/departments/merchant-upi-qr-code/

--------------------------------------------
9. IND QWIKCOLLECT
--------------------------------------------
For: Institutions and businesses collecting payments from customers
Use Cases: Schools, colleges, hospitals, housing societies,
           utilities, e-commerce
Features: Payment gateway, bulk collection, MIS reports
Portal: indianbank.qwikcollect.in

--------------------------------------------

13. NATIONAL COMMON MOBILITY CARD (NCMC)
--------------------------------------------
Also Known As: One Nation One Card
Purpose: Single card for all transit and retail payments
Works For: Metro rail, buses, parking, toll, retail shopping
Interoperable: All NCMC-enabled transit systems across India
How: Tap card at reader — balance deducted automatically
Recharge: Online via IndSMART app or net banking
Access: indianbank.bank.in/departments/national-common-mobility-card-ncmc/

SECTION E: OTHER DIGITAL SERVICES
========================================

--------------------------------------------
14. NETC FASTAG
--------------------------------------------
Purpose: RFID-based electronic toll collection on National Highways
How: Stick tag on windscreen; toll deducted automatically
Recharge: Via IndSMART app, net banking, UPI
Minimum Balance: Rs.150 (low balance alerts sent)
Benefit: No stopping at toll booths; 5% cashback on some plazas
Access: indianbank.bank.in/departments/netc-fastag/

--------------------------------------------
15. INDIAN BANK DIGITAL CURRENCY (e-Rupee / CBDC)
--------------------------------------------
What: RBI's Central Bank Digital Currency pilot
Type: Digital Rupee issued by Reserve Bank of India
How: Digital wallet linked to bank account
Use: Retail payments like cash but in digital form
Access: indianbank.bank.in/departments/terms-conditions-indian-bank-digital-currency/

--------------------------------------------
16. POINT OF SALE (PoS) / SoftPoS
--------------------------------------------
For: Merchants wanting to accept card payments
PoS Terminal: Physical card-swiping machine at merchant location
SoftPoS: Convert any NFC-enabled Android smartphone into a PoS terminal
         No physical machine needed
Accepts: Debit cards, credit cards, contactless, UPI
Settlement: Daily settlement to merchant account
Access: indianbank.bank.in/departments/pos/
