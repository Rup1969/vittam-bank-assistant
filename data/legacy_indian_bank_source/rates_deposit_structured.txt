INDIAN BANK SAVINGS BANK INTEREST RATES (w.e.f. 07.07.2025)
- Balance up to Rs.10 lakh: 2.50% per annum
- Balance Rs.10 lakh to less than Rs.200 Crore: 2.60% per annum
- Balance Rs.200 Crore and above: 2.65% per annum
- Interest calculated on daily basis, credited quarterly

SPECIAL TERM DEPOSIT PRODUCTS:
- IND Secure (444 days): Min Rs.1000, max less than Rs.3 Crore, callable FD/MMD
- IND Supreme 2.0 (300 days): introduced 04.02.2026, callable FD/MMD
- IND Green (555 days): Green deposit for eco-friendly projects, introduced 26.03.2025
- IND Golden Ager: For Super Senior Citizens (80+ years), extra 0.25% over senior rate

SENIOR CITIZEN RULES:
- Additional 0.50% p.a. over card rate for all retail deposits
- Super Senior Citizens (80+ years): additional 0.75% over card rate
- Maximum TD balance eligible: Rs.100 Crore at CIF level
- Bulk deposits (Rs.3 Crore and above per day) NOT eligible for senior citizen benefit

FORECLOSURE / PREMATURE WITHDRAWAL CHARGES:
- Closed before 7 days: No interest paid at all
- Deposits up to Rs.5 lakh, held 181+ days: No penalty; card rate for actual period applies
- Deposits up to Rs.5 lakh, closed before 181 days: Card rate minus 1% p.a.
- Deposits above Rs.5 lakh: Card rate minus 1% p.a. (regardless of period)
- No penalty for: staff, ex-staff, ex-staff cum senior citizens
- Bank may disallow premature withdrawal of deposits Rs.5 Crore and above

NRE TERM DEPOSIT RULES:
- Minimum term: 1 year
- No interest if closed before 1 year
- Same rates as domestic retail term deposits
- No additional 1% staff benefit on NRE/NRO deposits
