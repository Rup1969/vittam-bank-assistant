IB PERSONAL LOAN

Purpose:
Any legitimate personal financial need — medical emergencies, travel, marriage, home furnishing, consumer durables, education expenses not covered by education loan, etc.

Target Group:
- Permanent salaried employees of Central/State Govt, PSUs, reputed companies
- Professionals with established practice
- Pensioners drawing pension through Indian Bank

Eligibility:
- Minimum net monthly salary / income as per bank norms
- Satisfactory credit score (CIBIL)
- Existing relationship with bank preferred

Loan Amount:
- Based on income and repayment capacity
- Typically up to 20–24 times net monthly salary
- Maximum amount as per bank's current guidelines

Repayment:
- Up to 60 months (5 years) — EMI based
- Pensioners: repayment linked to pension credit

Interest Rate:
- Linked to RLLR — floating
- Fixed ROI option also available

Security:
- Clean (unsecured) loan — no collateral for most variants
- Guarantee of spouse or third party may be required

Documents Required:
- Latest salary slips (3 months)
- Form 16 / ITR
- Bank statements (6 months)
- KYC documents
- Employee ID / service certificate
